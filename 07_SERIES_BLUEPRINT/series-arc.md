# Series Arc

> **Document Type:** Primary Series Plot Arc
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED HIGH-LEVEL FRAMEWORK

## Act One: Identity

Book One establishes:

- Veilward Academy
- Aurelia and Aarav
- Their rivalry
- Their growing friendship
- The first signs of a deeper historical mystery

The central question is:

**Who are we when we are no longer protected by our assumptions?**

## Act Two: Truth

Book Two expands:

- The Forgotten Age
- Elyra and Varun
- Serpentfire
- The danger behind incomplete history
- The Outer Dark threat

The central question is:

**What changes when the truth refuses to remain buried?**

## Act Three: Choice

Book Three resolves:

- The Serpentfire mystery
- The historical legacy
- The Outer Dark conflict
- The protagonists' personal arcs

The central question is:

**What do we choose when history tells us to repeat itself?**

## Series Movement

The scope expands:

Personal → Historical → Existential

But the emotional center remains Aurelia and Aarav.
