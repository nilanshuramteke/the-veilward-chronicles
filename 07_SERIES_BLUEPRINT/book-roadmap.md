# Book Roadmap

> **Document Type:** Trilogy Planning Reference
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED STRUCTURE / DETAILS EXPANDABLE

## Book One

### Narrative Role
Establish the world and central relationship.

### Romance
Rivals → Cooperation → Friendship

### Mystery
First meaningful contact with the Serpentfire mystery.

### Threat
The Outer Dark is present as an emerging danger rather than the full central conflict.

### Ending Function
Changes the protagonists' understanding of what is happening.

---

## Book Two

### Narrative Role
Deepen the truth.

### Romance
Friendship → Emotional intimacy → Love

### Mystery
The Forgotten Age and Elyra and Varun become clearer.

### Threat
The Outer Dark escalates from concern to major danger.

### Ending Function
A major revelation creates irreversible consequences.

---

## Book Three

### Narrative Role
Resolve the central conflicts.

### Romance
Love → Challenge → Choice → Commitment

### Mystery
The truth about Serpentfire is fully revealed.

### Threat
The Outer Dark reaches its climax.

### Ending Function
Hopeful resolution and a future chosen by the protagonists.
