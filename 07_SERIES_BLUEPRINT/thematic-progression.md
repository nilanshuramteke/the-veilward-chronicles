# Thematic Progression

> **Document Type:** Trilogy Theme Architecture

## Book One: Identity

Primary themes:

- Belonging
- Assumption
- First impressions
- Personal identity

## Book Two: Truth

Primary themes:

- Memory
- History
- Vulnerability
- Understanding

## Book Three: Choice

Primary themes:

- Responsibility
- Love
- Change
- Hope

## Throughline

The trilogy repeatedly asks whether people must inherit the decisions of those who came before them.

The answer should ultimately be:

**No. History matters, but choice remains possible.**
