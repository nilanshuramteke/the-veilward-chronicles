# Conflict Escalation

> **Document Type:** Escalation Framework
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED STRUCTURE

## Book One: Signs

Conflict begins with:

- Strange events
- Incomplete information
- Local consequences
- Personal stakes

The protagonists do not yet understand the full danger.

## Book Two: Exposure

Conflict expands through:

- Historical discoveries
- Increasing consequences
- Fractured trust
- Broader danger

The protagonists understand enough to know that earlier assumptions were wrong.

## Book Three: Consequence

The conflict reaches:

- Direct confrontation
- World-level consequences
- Personal decisions
- Irreversible choices

## Escalation Rule

Each stage must increase:

- Knowledge
- Stakes
- Emotional cost

Do not increase danger through scale alone.
