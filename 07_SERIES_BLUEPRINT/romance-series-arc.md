# Romance Series Arc

> **Document Type:** Trilogy Romance Blueprint
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED

## Book One: Friendship

### Beginning
Aurelia and Aarav are rivals.

### Middle
They are forced into cooperation and develop respect.

### Ending
They become genuine friends.

Romance remains understated or unrealized.

---

## Book Two: Love

Their friendship deepens.

They become increasingly important to one another.

The transition to romance should emerge through:

- Trust
- Vulnerability
- Shared choices

By the end, love is acknowledged or clearly established.

---

## Book Three: Commitment

Their relationship faces real pressure.

The conflict must test:

- Trust
- Communication
- Individual identity

It must not depend on repetitive manufactured misunderstandings.

The ending confirms that they actively choose a shared future.

> **The relationship progresses because they learn each other, not because the plot declares them destined.**
