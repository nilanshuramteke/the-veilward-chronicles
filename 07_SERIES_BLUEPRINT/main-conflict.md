# Main Conflict

> **Document Type:** Central Conflict Framework
> **Canon Status:** PROTECTED HIGH-LEVEL FRAMEWORK

## External Conflict

The protected world faces a growing threat connected to the Outer Dark.

The exact nature of the final threat remains protected until detailed trilogy planning.

## Historical Conflict

The past contains incomplete truths involving:

- The Forgotten Age
- Elyra
- Varun
- Serpentfire

The present becomes dangerous partly because history has been misunderstood.

## Personal Conflict

Aurelia and Aarav must confront their own:

- Expectations
- Fears
- Defensive habits
- Ideas about strength and control

## Central Conflict Principle

The greatest conflict should not be solved simply by defeating an enemy.

The final resolution must require:

- Understanding
- Responsibility
- Personal growth
- Choice

> **The external threat matters because it forces the characters to decide who they are.**
