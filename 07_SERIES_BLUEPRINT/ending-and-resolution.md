# Ending and Resolution

> **Document Type:** Trilogy Ending Direction
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED ENDING PROMISE / DETAILS PROTECTED

## Required Resolution

The trilogy ends with:

- The central mystery resolved
- The primary external threat addressed
- Aurelia and Aarav together
- Their relationship preserved through mutual choice
- A hopeful future

## What the Ending Must Not Be

The ending must not:

- Kill one protagonist merely for emotional impact
- Reveal that their love was meaningless
- Force a tragic separation as the final resolution
- Remove their agency through destiny

## Emotional Resolution

Aurelia and Aarav should reach the ending as changed people.

They should not become perfect.

They should become more honest about:

- Who they are
- What they want
- What they owe others
- What they choose together

## Final Theme

The past can teach.

It can warn.

It can leave scars.

But it does not have to decide the future.

> **They are not the story that came before them. They are the story they choose to write next.**
