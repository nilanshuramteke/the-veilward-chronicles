# Outer Dark Series Arc

> **Document Type:** External Threat Progression
> **Canon Status:** PROTECTED

## Book One: Distant Threat

The danger is visible but not fully understood.

The protagonists encounter consequences before understanding the cause.

## Book Two: Escalation

The Outer Dark becomes:

- More immediate
- More disruptive
- More connected to historical mysteries

The scale of the threat becomes clear.

## Book Three: Confrontation

The protagonists face the complete consequences of the threat.

The final resolution should require more than raw power.

## Threat Rule

Outer Dark entities must have meaningful narrative logic.

They cannot exist merely as interchangeable enemies.
