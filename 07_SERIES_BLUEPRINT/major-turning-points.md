1. Aurelia and Aarav enter meaningful conflict.
2. Circumstances force cooperation.
3. Their assumptions begin to collapse.
4. The Serpentfire mystery becomes personally relevant.

2. The protagonists' friendship changes emotionally.
3. The Outer Dark threat escalates.

## Book Three

1. The protagonists confront the complete truth.
2. Personal and historical conflicts converge.
3. The relationship is tested by meaningful consequences.
4. The final choice rejects harmful repetition.
5. The story resolves with hope.
