# Serpentfire Series Arc

> **Document Type:** Central Mystery Progression
> **Canon Status:** PROTECTED

## Book One: Discovery

Serpentfire enters the protagonists' lives as something:

- Unexplained
- Historically significant
- Personally relevant

The protagonists leave Book One with more questions than answers.

## Book Two: Revelation

The characters discover that the accepted understanding is incomplete.

Connections to:

- Elyra
- Varun
- The Forgotten Age

become clearer.

## Book Three: Truth

The complete nature and consequences of Serpentfire are revealed.

The resolution must reinforce:

- Choice
- Responsibility
- Love without destiny

## Canon Rule

Serpentfire must not function as a magical shortcut for romance.
