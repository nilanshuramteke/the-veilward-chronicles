# Ending Audit

## Required Checks
- [ ] Central conflict resolves.
- [ ] Major antagonist arc resolves appropriately.
- [ ] Key character choices are earned.
- [ ] Final world-state change follows established principles.
- [ ] Protagonists retain agency.
- [ ] The thematic ending matches the trilogy.
- [ ] Some mystery remains only where intentional.

## Final Question
Does the ending feel inevitable in retrospect while remaining difficult to predict beforehand?
