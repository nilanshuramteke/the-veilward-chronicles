# 13_REVIEWS_AND_AUDITS

## Purpose
Structured deep reviews of the Story Bible, trilogy architecture, and manuscripts.

## Trackers vs Audits
- `10_TRACKERS`: continuous operational tracking.
- `13_REVIEWS_AND_AUDITS`: periodic deep inspections.

## Recommended Audit Points
- Before drafting Book One
- After major structural revisions
- After each completed book
- Before locking major manuscript revisions
- Before final trilogy publication
