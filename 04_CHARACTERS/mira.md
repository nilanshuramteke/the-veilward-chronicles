# Mira

> **Character Type:** Recurring Student Character  
> **Book One Status:** CONFIRMED  
> **Future-Series Status:** OPEN FOR DEVELOPMENT

## Narrative Function

Mira provides:

- Social observation
- Emotional intelligence
- An independent perspective on the protagonists
- A student-level response to Academy secrecy and disruption

She should not duplicate Dev's narrative role.

## Confirmed for Book One

- Mira is a student at Veilward Academy.
- She appears in recurring Academy-life scenes.
- She is part of the wider social environment surrounding the protagonists.
- She can notice changes in people and social dynamics that the protagonists may overlook.

## Relationship Direction

### With Aurelia
Mira may provide an outside perspective on Aurelia's behavior and choices.

She should not exist merely to discuss Aarav with Aurelia.

### With Aarav
Her perspective should remain independent rather than automatically favoring either protagonist.

### With Dev
They may share social scenes and familiarity without becoming interchangeable characters.

## Personality Direction

### Confirmed
- Perceptive
- Socially aware
- Capable of independent judgment

### Open for Future Development
- Species
- Magical specialization
- Personal ambitions
- Family history
- Full emotional history

## Knowledge Boundary

Mira should not become an accidental repository for protagonist secrets.

Her knowledge must come through:

- direct observation,
- believable conversations,
- or events she genuinely witnesses.

## Writing Rule

When Mira notices something important, her observation should have a basis.

She must not possess unexplained insight simply because the plot requires someone to understand the protagonists.

> **Observation is most interesting when it changes what a character chooses to do.**
