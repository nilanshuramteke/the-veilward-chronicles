# Dev

> **Character Type:** Recurring Student Character  
> **Book One Status:** CONFIRMED  
> **Future-Series Status:** OPEN FOR DEVELOPMENT

## Narrative Function

Dev provides:

- A grounded Academy-life perspective
- Friendship and social connection outside the central Aurelia–Aarav dynamic
- A recognizable student response to increasingly unusual events
- Emotional and tonal contrast during periods of investigation

He must not exist only for comic relief.

## Confirmed for Book One

- Dev is a student at Veilward Academy.
- He appears as part of the protagonists' wider student environment.
- He is connected to recurring Academy-life scenes.
- His presence helps show how unusual events affect ordinary students.

## Relationship Direction

### With Aurelia
Potential friendship or established student familiarity.

Exact depth should follow the Book One chapter needs.

### With Aarav
May provide a social connection distinct from Aurelia's relationship with Aarav.

Do not use Dev merely as a commentator on the central relationship.

### With Mira
They may share scenes and social space, but neither character should exist solely as the other's attachment.

## Personality Direction

### Confirmed
- Socially grounded
- Observant enough to react to changes around him
- Able to function naturally in Academy-life scenes

### Open for Future Development
- Species
- Magical specialization
- Family history
- Deeper ambitions
- Full personality profile

## Knowledge Boundary

Dev should know only what a student can reasonably know based on his involvement.

He must not receive protected mystery information simply to make group conversations easier.

## Writing Rule

Dev should contribute something meaningful whenever he appears:

- perspective,
- emotional grounding,
- information,
- pressure,
- or consequence.

> **A supporting friend should feel like a person with a life beyond the protagonists.**
