# Lyra

> **Character Type:** Recurring Student Character  
> **Book One Status:** CONFIRMED  
> **Future-Series Status:** EXPANDABLE

## Narrative Function

Lyra provides:

- Competitive pressure
- An alternative student perspective
- Academy-level stakes outside the central mystery
- A reminder that normal ambitions continue even when larger events begin unfolding

## Confirmed for Book One

- Lyra is a student at Veilward Academy.
- She participates in the competitive environment surrounding the protagonists.
- Her presence intensifies existing student-level pressure.
- She is not defined as a villain.

## Relationship Direction

### With Aurelia and Aarav

Lyra may challenge one or both protagonists through:

- competition,
- achievement,
- disagreement,
- or conflicting priorities.

She must not be written primarily as:

- a jealous rival,
- a romance obstacle,
- an antagonist created only to provoke conflict.

## Personality Direction

### Confirmed
- Competitive
- Capable
- Motivated by her own goals

### Open for Future Development
- Species
- Magical specialization
- Personal motivations
- Family background
- Long-term trilogy role

## Knowledge Boundary

Lyra should know only what her role and direct experiences justify.

Competition does not grant access to protagonist secrets.

## Writing Rule

Lyra should have ambitions unrelated to Aurelia and Aarav.

Her strongest scenes should reveal what she wants rather than simply showing whom she wants to defeat.

> **A rival becomes memorable when victory matters for reasons beyond the protagonists.**
