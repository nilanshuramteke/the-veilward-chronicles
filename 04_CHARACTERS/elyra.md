# Elyra

> **Document Type:** Historical Character Bible
> **Canon Status:** PROTECTED CANON

## Identity

**Name:** Elyra  
**Role:** Historical figure  
**Primary Connection:** Varun and the Serpentfire mystery

## Narrative Function

Elyra represents a past relationship whose incomplete history influences the present.

She must not become merely a plot device for Aurelia and Aarav.

## Known Facts

- Elyra existed before the present generation.
- Her history is connected to Varun.
- Their story is connected to Serpentfire.
- The complete truth is not initially known.

## Unknowns

The following remain protected:

- Species
- Exact era
- Full personality
- Appearance
- Fate
- Nature of her relationship with Varun

## Development Rule

Elyra's eventual story must deepen the central themes of:

- Love
- Choice
- Memory
- History
- Consequences

It should not simply repeat Aurelia and Aarav's relationship.

> **The past should illuminate the present without becoming its prison.**
