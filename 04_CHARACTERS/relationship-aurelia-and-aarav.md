# Aurelia and Aarav Relationship Bible

> **Document Type:** Central Relationship Canon
> **Canon Status:** CORE SERIES CANON

## Relationship Structure

**Enemies → Friends → Lovers**

The transition must be gradual.

Their romance is earned through trust, not instant attraction.

---

# Phase One: Enemies

Their first impression of one another is shaped by:

- Competition
- Misunderstanding
- Personality clashes
- Incorrect assumptions

Neither character should become cruel simply to create romantic tension.

---

# Phase Two: Forced Cooperation

Circumstances require them to work together.

They begin discovering:

- Each other's competence
- Different perspectives
- Shared values beneath conflict

Respect begins before friendship.

---

# Phase Three: Friendship

Their friendship develops independently from romance.

Important elements include:

- Shared humor
- Private conversations
- Mutual support
- Choosing one another as friends

---

# Phase Four: Trust

They become emotionally important to one another.

Trust develops when they reveal things they do not easily reveal to others.

---

# Phase Five: Love

Romantic realization should feel natural because friendship already exists.

Their love should involve:

- Mutual choice
- Emotional honesty
- Respect
- Attraction
- Fear of consequences

---

# Challenges

Their relationship may face:

- Family expectations
- Cultural differences
- Social assumptions
- Fear of repeating historical mistakes
- External danger

No challenge should exist merely because they belong to different species.

---

# Serpentfire Rule

Serpentfire may reveal historical connections.

It must never mean:

- They have no free will
- They are magically forced to love
- Their choices do not matter

> **History can influence them. Destiny cannot replace their consent.**

---

# Ending Direction

Their relationship ultimately receives a positive ending.

The journey may be painful.

The conclusion must preserve:

- Their agency
- Their love
- Hope for their future

> **They choose each other after learning who they truly are.**
