# Varun

> **Document Type:** Historical Character Bible
> **Canon Status:** PROTECTED CANON

## Identity

**Name:** Varun  
**Role:** Historical figure  
**Primary Connection:** Elyra and the Serpentfire mystery

## Narrative Function

Varun is part of a forgotten or incomplete history whose consequences still matter.

He must be developed as a person rather than as an ancient warning.

## Known Facts

- Varun lived before the present generation.
- He is connected to Elyra.
- Their history is connected to Serpentfire.
- Important details are incomplete or restricted.

## Unknowns

Protected areas include:

- Species
- Exact era
- Full personality
- Appearance
- Fate
- His role in the Serpentfire Incident

## Development Rule

His eventual history must not invalidate Aurelia and Aarav's choices.

Historical repetition may create fear.

It must not prove that tragedy is unavoidable.

> **A forgotten life is still a life, not merely a lesson for those who come later.**
