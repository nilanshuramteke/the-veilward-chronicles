# Continuity and Canon Guide

> **Document Type:** Generation and Revision Rules

## Before Writing

Check relevant files for:

- World rules
- Magic limits
- Character details
- Relationship status
- Timeline

## Canon Priority

When documents conflict:

1. `00_CORE`
2. Explicit canon rules
3. Character and world bibles
4. Later detailed files

Conflicts should be resolved before generating final chapters.

## Status Labels

Respect:

- CONFIRMED
- PROVISIONAL
- DISPUTED
- LOST / UNKNOWN
- RESTRICTED
- PROTECTED

Do not convert unknown information into confirmed fact accidentally.

## Continuity Checks

Verify:

- Character knowledge
- Relationship progression
- Injuries
- Locations
- Magical abilities
- Timeline

## Retcons

Avoid retcons unless they solve a meaningful structural problem.

> **Consistency creates trust between the story and the reader.**
