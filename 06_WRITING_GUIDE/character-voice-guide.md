# Character Voice Guide

> **Document Type:** Character Consistency Guide

## Purpose

Characters must remain distinct in:

- Dialogue
- Thought
- Humor
- Emotional response

## Aurelia

Her voice should tend toward:

- Directness
- Energy
- Competitive confidence
- Emotional intensity beneath restraint

Avoid making her permanently sarcastic or permanently angry.

## Aarav

His voice should tend toward:

- Observation
- Measured responses
- Dry humor
- Careful emotional expression

Avoid turning quietness into lack of personality.

## Growth

Character voice can evolve.

Growth should feel connected to experiences rather than becoming a sudden personality replacement.

## Supporting Characters

Every major character should have:

- A recognizable communication style
- Personal priorities
- Different emotional habits

> **Voice comes from personality, not from verbal gimmicks.**
