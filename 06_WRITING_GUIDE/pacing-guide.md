# Pacing Guide

> **Document Type:** Story Rhythm Guide

## The Four Story Rhythms

Balance:

1. College life
2. Romance
3. Mystery
4. External danger

No single rhythm should permanently dominate the book.

## Quiet Scenes

Quiet scenes are necessary for:

- Friendship
- Character development
- Romance
- Recovery
- Humor

## Escalation

Escalation should come from consequences.

Do not increase danger simply by making every threat larger.

## Mystery

Reveal information gradually.

Every major answer should create:

- A new question
- A new consequence
- A changed understanding

## Romance Pace

Do not rush emotional milestones.

Friendship must have time to exist.

> **Pacing is not speed. It is the movement between moments that matter.**
