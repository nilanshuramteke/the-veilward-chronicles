# Action and Magic Scenes

> **Document Type:** Scene Construction Guide

## Action Principle

The reader must understand:

- Where characters are
- What they want
- What threatens them
- What changes

## Magic

Magic should have visible consequences.

Avoid unexplained abilities appearing solely to solve a scene.

## Scene Structure

A strong action sequence generally contains:

1. Immediate objective
2. Escalating complication
3. Character decision
4. Consequence

## Emotional Stakes

Action matters more when characters care about something beyond survival.

## True Forms

When characters use True Forms, describe the transformation clearly without slowing the scene excessively.

## Power Limits

Remember:

- Exhaustion
- Injury
- Control
- Environment
- Emotional state

> **Action should reveal character, not merely display power.**
