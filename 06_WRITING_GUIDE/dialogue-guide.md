# Dialogue Guide

> **Document Type:** Dialogue Writing Rules

## Core Principle

Dialogue should sound like people speaking for reasons.

Every exchange should reveal at least one of:

- Desire
- Personality
- Conflict
- Relationship
- Information

## Avoid

- Exposition disguised as conversation
- Characters repeating what everyone knows
- Every character speaking with identical rhythm
- Constant witty comebacks

## Subtext

Characters should not always say exactly what they mean.

Especially in emotional scenes, what remains unsaid can matter.

## Dialogue Tags

Prefer simple tags such as:

- said
- asked
- replied

Use alternatives sparingly.

## Conflict

Good dialogue conflict does not require shouting.

Silence, avoidance, humor, and disagreement can all create tension.

> **Characters speak differently because they want different things.**
