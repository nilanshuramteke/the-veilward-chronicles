# Prohibited Patterns

> **Document Type:** Writing Quality Guardrails

## Avoid Generic AI Patterns

Do not rely on:

- Repetitive sentence structures
- Constant dramatic fragments
- Excessive rhetorical questions
- Repeated emotional summaries
- Overexplaining obvious meaning

## Avoid Fantasy Cliches

Unless deliberately subverted:

- Instant destined love
- Chosen-one shortcuts
- Perfect mentors
- Evil species
- Power without cost

## Avoid Romance Shortcuts

Do not use:

- Instant emotional intimacy
- Jealousy as the primary proof of love
- Repeated breakups without meaningful cause
- Forced misunderstanding cycles

## Avoid Character Flattening

Do not reduce characters to:

- Their species
- One personality trait
- Their romantic role

## Avoid Exposition Dumps

World-building should appear through story.

## Final Rule

If a scene exists only because it resembles something expected in fantasy romance, question whether it belongs.

> **Familiarity is useful. Predictability is not required.**
