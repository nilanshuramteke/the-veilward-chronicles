# Narrative Voice

> **Document Type:** Narrative Direction

## Overall Feel

The narrative should feel:

- Intimate
- Intelligent
- Emotionally grounded
- Wonder-filled
- Occasionally humorous

## Emotional Distance

Stay close enough to the viewpoint character that the reader understands what matters to them.

Do not explain emotions the scene already demonstrates.

## Mystery

Mystery should emerge through:

- What characters know
- What they notice
- What remains unexplained

Avoid artificial withholding.

## Humor

Humor should come from:

- Personality
- Relationships
- Circumstances

Do not insert jokes that break emotional moments.

> **The voice should invite the reader into the world, not lecture them about it.**
