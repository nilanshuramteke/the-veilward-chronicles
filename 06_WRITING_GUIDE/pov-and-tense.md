# Point of View and Tense

> **Document Type:** Narrative Consistency Guide

## Recommended Approach

Use **third-person limited, past tense**.

## Primary Viewpoints

Book One should primarily follow:

- Aurelia
- Aarav

## POV Rule

Remain inside the active viewpoint character's experience.

Do not reveal another character's private thoughts unless the viewpoint changes.

## POV Changes

Change viewpoint only at:

- Chapter breaks
- Clearly marked scene breaks

Avoid unnecessary switching within scenes.

## Information Control

The reader should learn information through the viewpoint character's available knowledge.

Mystery should not depend on pretending the viewpoint character does not know something they genuinely know.

> **Limited perspective creates intimacy when it remains honest.**
