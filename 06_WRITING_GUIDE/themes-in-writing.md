# Themes in Writing

> **Document Type:** Thematic Application Guide

## Core Themes

The series explores:

- Love as choice
- Identity
- Belonging
- Tradition and change
- Memory
- Responsibility
- Hope

## Application

Themes should emerge through:

- Character decisions
- Relationships
- Consequences

Do not repeatedly state the theme in dialogue.

## Love as Choice

Aurelia and Aarav choose one another.

Their relationship should not become important because prophecy declares it important.

## Tradition

Tradition may provide:

- Belonging
- Wisdom
- Pressure

Avoid portraying tradition as entirely good or entirely harmful.

## Hope

Hope should be earned.

A positive ending should come from choices and growth.

> **A theme becomes powerful when readers discover it inside the story.**
