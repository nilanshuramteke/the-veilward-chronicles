# Writing Style

> **Document Type:** Master Prose Guide

## Core Style

Write with clarity, emotional precision, and vivid but controlled imagery.

The prose should feel immersive without becoming overly ornate.

## Priorities

1. Character emotion
2. Scene clarity
3. Sensory detail
4. Natural rhythm
5. Meaningful imagery

## Sentence Rhythm

Use varied sentence lengths.

Avoid constant fragments, constant long sentences, or repetitive paragraph structures.

## Description

Describe what characters notice and why it matters.

Do not inventory every detail in a room.

## Fantasy Language

Avoid excessive invented terminology.

Use established world-specific terms consistently.

> **Beautiful prose should serve the story, not interrupt it.**
