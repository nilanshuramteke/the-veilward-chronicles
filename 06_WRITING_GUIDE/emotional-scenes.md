# Emotional Scenes

> **Document Type:** Emotional Writing Guide

## Principle

Emotion should be experienced through the scene rather than announced from outside it.

## Use

- Body language
- Silence
- Thought
- Sensory awareness
- Contradiction

## Vulnerability

Characters should not become emotionally articulate simply because the story needs clarity.

Let them struggle to speak when appropriate.

## Reconciliation

An apology is not automatically resolution.

Show:

- Recognition
- Accountability
- Change
- Choice

## Intensity

Do not make every emotional scene the most important moment of the novel.

Quiet scenes can carry equal weight.

> **The strongest emotion is often the one a character is trying not to reveal.**
