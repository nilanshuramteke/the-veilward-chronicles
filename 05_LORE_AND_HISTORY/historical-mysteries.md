# Mystery Management Rules

A mystery should have:

1. A known surface explanation
2. Missing or contradictory evidence
3. A deeper truth
4. Consequences for discovering that truth

Do not answer a mystery before the story benefits from the answer.

> **A mystery is not empty information. It is information waiting for the right moment to matter.**
