# Serpentfire History

> **Document Type:** Central Lore Mystery
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED EXISTENCE / PROTECTED DETAILS

## Purpose

Serpentfire is one of the central mysteries of The Veilward Chronicles.

Its nature must remain undefined until the series structure requires a definitive answer.

## Confirmed

- Serpentfire existed or occurred as something historically significant.
- It is connected to Elyra and Varun.
- Its history has consequences.
- Knowledge about it is incomplete, restricted, or misunderstood.
- It becomes relevant to Aurelia and Aarav.

## Unknown

Do not establish yet whether Serpentfire is:

- A magical power
- A phenomenon
- A bond
- A ritual
- A weapon
- A force
- A historical event with another meaning

## Thematic Rule

Serpentfire must reinforce:

- Choice
- Responsibility
- Consequences
- The danger of incomplete history

It must not force romantic love.

## Historical Problem

The modern generation does not possess the complete truth.

Different characters may believe different things.

## Development Questions

Before final canon is established, answer:

1. What is Serpentfire?
2. Why is it named Serpentfire?
3. What did Elyra and Varun have to do with it?
4. Why was its history lost or restricted?
5. Why does it matter now?

## Final Principle

> **A mystery becomes dangerous when people act on what they think they know.**
