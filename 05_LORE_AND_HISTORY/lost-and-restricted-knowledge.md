# Lost and Restricted Knowledge

> **Document Type:** Knowledge Governance and Mystery
> **Canon Status:** MIXED CANON

## Purpose

Not all knowledge is equally available.

Information may be:

- Lost
- Restricted
- Forgotten
- Misclassified
- Deliberately hidden

## Lost Knowledge

Lost knowledge no longer has a reliable complete source.

Examples may include:

- Damaged records
- Extinct traditions
- Forgotten languages
- Missing historical accounts

## Restricted Knowledge

Restricted knowledge still exists but has controlled access.

Possible reasons:

- Danger
- Ethical concerns
- Political sensitivity
- Historical trauma

Restriction does not automatically mean the authorities are correct.

## The Archive

Veilward Academy contains historical and research materials, including potentially restricted records.

The Archive is not omniscient.

## Discovery Rule

Characters should not discover information simply because the plot needs it.

Discovery should require:

- Research
- Access
- Trust
- Risk
- Interpretation

## Final Principle

> **Knowledge hidden for protection can eventually become dangerous when nobody remembers why it was hidden.**
