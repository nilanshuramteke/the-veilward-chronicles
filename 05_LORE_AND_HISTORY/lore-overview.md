# Lore Overview

> **Document Type:** Lore Index
> **Canon Status:** MIXED CANON

## Purpose

This folder preserves the deeper stories, memories, mysteries, and historical uncertainties of The Veilward Chronicles.

Lore differs from ordinary chronology.

- `timeline.md` answers when events belong.
- World history explains how society developed.
- Lore explores what people believe, remember, dispute, hide, or have forgotten.

## Lore Status System

### CONFIRMED
Established canon.

### PROVISIONAL
Working canon that may be refined.

### DISPUTED
Multiple accounts exist.

### LOST / UNKNOWN
Reliable knowledge no longer survives.

### RESTRICTED
Information is known by some characters or institutions but is not publicly accessible.

### PROTECTED
Deliberately reserved for future story development.

## Core Lore Pillars

The central lore threads are:

- The ancient world
- The formation and deeper purpose of the Veil
- The Forgotten Age
- Elyra and Varun
- Serpentfire
- The Outer Dark
- Lost and restricted knowledge

## Final Principle

> **Lore should create questions before it provides answers.**
