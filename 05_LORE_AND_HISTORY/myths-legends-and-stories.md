# Myths, Legends, and Stories

> **Document Type:** Cultural Lore Framework
> **Canon Status:** EXPANDABLE

## Purpose

Not every story told in the Hidden World is historical fact.

Myths and legends preserve:

- Values
- Fear
- Memory
- Identity

## Categories

### Founding Stories
Stories about communities, places, or traditions.

### Hero Legends
Stories about protectors whose historical truth may be uncertain.

### Warning Tales
Stories intended to teach caution.

### Love Legends
Stories about relationships remembered, distorted, or romanticized.

### Veil Stories
Accounts explaining the Veil in different ways.

## Truth and Myth

A legend may contain:

- Historical truth
- Symbolic truth
- Deliberate exaggeration
- Complete invention

The reader should not always know which immediately.

## Cultural Diversity

Different species may tell different versions of the same event.

No version should automatically be treated as superior.

## Future Registry

New myths should record:

- Title
- Community of origin
- Known version
- Alternate versions
- Possible historical basis
- Story relevance

> **A story can survive for centuries even when nobody remembers why it was first told.**
