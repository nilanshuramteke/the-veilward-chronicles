# Ancient History

> **Document Type:** Deep Historical Lore
> **Canon Status:** PROTECTED / EXPANDABLE

## Purpose

This document holds the oldest layers of known and unknown history.

It intentionally does not establish a complete origin story for the world.

## The Earliest Questions

The following remain unresolved:

- How did mythical species first emerge?
- Did magic exist before known civilization?
- What was the world like before the Veil?
- When did the Outer Dark first become a threat?

## Surviving Fragments

Ancient history survives through:

- Oral traditions
- Ruined records
- Symbols
- Songs
- Protected archives
- Contradictory myths

No single surviving source should automatically be treated as complete truth.

## Ancient Civilizations

PROTECTED.

Future development may establish ancient Hidden World civilizations, but they must not be added merely for spectacle.

Every civilization should matter to:

- Modern history
- Magic
- The Veil
- Serpentfire
- The Outer Dark

## The Veil in Ancient Memory

The Veil is remembered as ancient.

Its complete origin remains UNKNOWN.

Some traditions may disagree about whether it was:

- Created
- Discovered
- Strengthened
- Repaired

These possibilities must remain open until the central lore is developed.

## Final Principle

> **The oldest history is not necessarily the most truthful. It is simply the farthest from those who can verify it.**
