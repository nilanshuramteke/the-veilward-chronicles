# The Forgotten Age

> **Document Type:** Historical Mystery
> **Canon Status:** LOST / PROTECTED

## Overview

The Forgotten Age is a period of history whose surviving records are incomplete, fragmented, contradictory, or deliberately restricted.

It is not defined by a single confirmed date range.

## What Is Known

The Forgotten Age occurred before the modern generation.

Events from this period may connect to:

- Elyra
- Varun
- Serpentfire
- Changes in Hidden World history

## What Is Unknown

The following remain protected:

- Why the history became fragmented
- Whether records were deliberately destroyed
- Whether an event caused widespread loss
- The complete relationship between this era and the Outer Dark

## Historical Fragments

Future stories may reveal the Forgotten Age through:

- Incomplete archives
- Personal journals
- Oral traditions
- Damaged magical records
- Conflicting accounts

## Narrative Rule

Discovery must happen gradually.

The Forgotten Age should not be explained through one convenient exposition scene.

## Final Principle

> **Forgotten does not mean unimportant. Sometimes history disappears because remembering it became dangerous.**
