# The Outer Dark: Historical Lore

> **Document Type:** Threat History
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED CONCEPT / PROTECTED ORIGINS

## Overview

The Outer Dark refers to threats beyond the protected world.

Its complete nature remains intentionally undefined.

## Confirmed

- The Outer Dark exists as a genuine threat.
- It is connected to the defensive purpose of the Hidden World.
- Its influence or invasions create danger.
- The modern generation does not understand its full history.

## Unknown

Protected areas include:

- Origin
- Nature
- Number of entities
- Motivation
- Relationship to the Veil
- Relationship to Serpentfire

## Historical Memory

Knowledge about earlier encounters may survive unevenly.

Some accounts may exaggerate.

Others may hide important details.

## Narrative Rule

Outer Dark threats should not be interchangeable monsters.

Before creating a major entity, define:

- What it wants
- How it operates
- What it threatens
- Why ordinary defenses are insufficient

## Final Principle

> **The darkness beyond the world should be frightening because it is understood too little, not because it is explained too much.**
