# Aurelia's Book One Arc

## Beginning
Aurelia is capable, competitive, proud, and deeply accustomed to carrying responsibility alone.

## False Belief
If she needs others, she risks becoming less reliable.

## Pressure
The mystery repeatedly creates situations she cannot solve through instinct alone.

## Relationship Challenge
Aarav frustrates her because he notices when she is pretending to be unaffected.

## Turning Point
She chooses to trust another person's judgment without surrendering her own.

## Ending
Aurelia understands that independence and connection can coexist.

She remains fiery and determined, but no longer mistakes isolation for strength.
