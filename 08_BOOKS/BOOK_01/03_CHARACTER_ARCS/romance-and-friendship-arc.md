# Romance and Friendship Arc

## Stage 1 — Rivals
They compete and misunderstand one another.

## Stage 2 — Forced Allies
Circumstances require cooperation.

## Stage 3 — Respect
They recognize each other's strengths and limitations.

## Stage 4 — Trust
One protects the other through meaningful personal cost.

## Stage 5 — Friendship
They actively choose each other's company.

## End State
No official romantic relationship.

Subtle attraction may exist, but the emotional accomplishment is friendship.

## Rule
Friendship scenes must be enjoyable in their own right and not exist only as romance preparation.
