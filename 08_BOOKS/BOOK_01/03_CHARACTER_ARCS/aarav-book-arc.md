# Aarav's Book One Arc

## Beginning
Aarav is observant, controlled, intelligent, and comfortable maintaining emotional distance.

## False Belief
Control prevents unnecessary harm.

## Pressure
The mystery becomes too unpredictable to manage through observation alone.

## Relationship Challenge
Aurelia forces him to engage rather than remain detached.

## Turning Point
He chooses openness when retreat would be easier.

## Ending
Aarav learns that trust requires participation, not merely understanding.

He remains measured but becomes more emotionally available.
