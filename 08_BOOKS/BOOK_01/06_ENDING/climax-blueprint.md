# Climax Blueprint

## Location
The Hidden Chamber beneath Veilward.

## Objective
Stop the Echo from using the chamber's remaining magical structure to complete its search or establish a stronger connection.

## Character Requirement
Aurelia cannot solve the crisis through raw power alone.

Aarav cannot solve it through analysis alone.

They succeed because they finally coordinate naturally.

## Emotional Payoff
Their victory demonstrates the difference between:

- Competing
- Cooperating
- Trusting

## Mystery Payoff
The climax answers what the Echo wanted.

It does not answer the full meaning of the First Ember.
