# Bridge to Book Two

## What Carries Forward
- Aurelia and Aarav's friendship
- Serpentfire
- Elyra and Varun
- The First Ember
- The restricted history
- The larger Outer Dark search

## What Changes
The protagonists can no longer believe that Veilward's history is complete.

They also understand that their cooperation is no longer accidental.

## Book Two Launch
Book Two should begin from the consequences of the discovery, not repeat the Book One academy introduction.

The central trilogy movement shifts:

**Identity → Truth**
