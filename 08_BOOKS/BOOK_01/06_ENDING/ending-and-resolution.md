# Ending and Resolution

## Immediate Plot Resolution
The Echo is defeated, dispersed, or successfully contained.

Veilward survives the immediate crisis.

## Mystery Resolution
The protagonists learn:
- The threat was already inside Veilward.
- The hidden chamber once held the First Ember.
- The Ember was removed long ago.
- Elyra and Varun were connected to the erased history.

## Emotional Resolution
Aurelia and Aarav become genuine friends.

The friendship is explicit through behavior and choice, not necessarily a dramatic speech.

## Final Mood
Warm, relieved, curious, and quietly unsettling.

Book One closes a mystery while proving that the larger story has only begun.
