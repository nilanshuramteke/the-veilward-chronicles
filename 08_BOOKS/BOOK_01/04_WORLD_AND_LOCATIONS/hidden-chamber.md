# The Hidden Chamber

## Location
Beneath older foundations of Veilward Academy.

## Atmosphere
Ancient but not abandoned in a conventional sense.

The chamber feels deliberately preserved and deliberately forgotten.

## Features
- A central empty containment space
- Damaged inscriptions
- The recurring symbol
- Evidence of historical intervention

## Function
The chamber proves:
- The mystery predates the protagonists
- Something important was removed
- Academy history is incomplete

## Inscription Fragment
The surviving fragment connected to Elyra and Varun reads:

> The fire was never meant to belong to either of them.

## Rule
Do not reveal the chamber's full historical meaning in Book One.
