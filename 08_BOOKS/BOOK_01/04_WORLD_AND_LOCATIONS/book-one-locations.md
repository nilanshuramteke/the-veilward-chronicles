# Book One Locations

## Veilward Academy
Primary setting. Use established Academy canon.

## Training Grounds
Location of the Impossible Breach.

## Archives
A place of discovery, restriction, and historical contradiction.

## Student Commons
Used for friendships, humor, and ordinary academy life.

## Dormitory Spaces
Private environments where characters can lower their defenses.

## Old Foundations
Older layers beneath the Academy lead toward the hidden chamber.

## Hidden Chamber
The climax location.

## Location Rule
Each location should accumulate emotional meaning rather than functioning as interchangeable scenery.
