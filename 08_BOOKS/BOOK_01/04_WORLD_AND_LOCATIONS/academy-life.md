# Academy Life in Book One

## Purpose
The Academy must feel like a lived-in college environment, not only a military facility.

## Include
- Classes
- Training
- Rivalries
- Friendships
- Meals
- Student traditions
- Informal conversations
- Academic pressure

## Balance
The characters should have lives outside the mystery.

Readers must understand what is worth protecting.

## Training
Training scenes should reveal:
- Character
- Skills
- Relationships
- World rules

Do not use training only as a power demonstration.
