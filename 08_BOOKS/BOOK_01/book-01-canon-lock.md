# Book One Canon Lock

## Purpose
This document defines the minimum locked canon required before drafting *The Veilward Chronicles: Book One*.

## Opening State
- Aurelia and Aarav are already students at Veilward Academy.
- They know each other before Book One begins.
- Their relationship begins in rivalry, not traditional enmity.
- Their rivalry is based on competition, assumptions, and conflicting approaches.

## Relationship State
Beginning:
**Rivals**

Book One movement:
**Rivals → Forced Cooperation → Respect → Trust → Friendship**

Book One must not complete the romance.

## Mystery Boundaries
Book One may reveal:
- the first layers of the central disturbance,
- the significance of selected historical clues,
- growing importance of Elyra and Varun,
- the existence or relevance of the First Ember,
- traces of a larger pattern.

Book One must not fully reveal:
- Elyra and Varun's later survival truth,
- the complete nature of the Seeker,
- the Harbinger's full role,
- the final purpose of the First Ember,
- the complete truth of the Veil,
- the final truth of Serpentfire.

## Drafting Safety
Consult:
1. `00_CORE/canon-rules.md`
2. `00_CORE/timeline.md`
3. `07_SERIES_BLUEPRINT/revelation-matrix.md`
4. relevant Book One blueprint files

If a planned scene conflicts with any of these, stop and resolve the conflict before drafting.

## Ending Lock
The Book One ending must expand the mystery rather than resolve the trilogy-level questions.

## Status
This file is a pre-drafting lock and should be updated only through an explicit canon decision.
