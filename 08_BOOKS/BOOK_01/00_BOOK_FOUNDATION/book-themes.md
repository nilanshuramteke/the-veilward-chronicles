# Book One Themes

## Identity
Who are people beneath expectations, inherited roles, and first impressions?

## Assumption
Aurelia and Aarav repeatedly discover that their judgments about one another are incomplete.

## Belonging
Veilward is both home and institution. Students must decide whether belonging means obedience, friendship, or shared responsibility.

## Trust
Trust begins through small decisions, not declarations.

## History
The past is not neutral when parts of it have been deliberately removed.

## Hope
The book ends with unanswered questions, but connection and choice create hope rather than despair.
