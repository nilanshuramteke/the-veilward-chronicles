# Book One Overview

## Narrative Role
Book One establishes the emotional foundation of the trilogy and gives the reader a complete academy mystery.

## What the Book Must Deliver
- A living introduction to Veilward Academy
- Aurelia and Aarav as distinct protagonists
- A believable rivalry that evolves into friendship
- The Impossible Breach mystery
- The Echo as an immediate threat
- The first meaningful appearance of Serpentfire
- A satisfying climax and unresolved larger mystery

## Trilogy Function
Book One is about **Identity**.

The protagonists begin by believing they understand themselves, each other, and their world. By the ending, all three assumptions have changed.

## Ending Promise
The immediate danger is resolved. Aurelia and Aarav become genuine friends. The larger mystery expands.
