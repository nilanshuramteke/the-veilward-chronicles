# Book One Continuity

## Required
- Aurelia and Aarav begin as rivals.
- They become reluctant allies through necessity.
- Book One ends with genuine friendship.
- No official romance is completed.
- Serpentfire remains largely unexplained.
- The First Ember's full nature remains hidden.
- The Outer Dark is not fully understood.
- The immediate Book One threat is resolved.

## Forbidden
- Destiny forcing Aurelia and Aarav together.
- Instant emotional intimacy.
- Full answers to trilogy mysteries.
- Random new powers without established cost.
- A cliffhanger that prevents Book One from feeling complete.

## Canon Principle
Book One may reveal that accepted history is incomplete. It must not casually overwrite established canon.
