# Book One Premise

Aurelia has come to Veilward Academy determined to prove that she deserves her place among the next generation of defenders. Aarav appears to be everything she dislikes: controlled, difficult to read, and impossible to provoke in the satisfying ways she expects.

When a competitive academy exercise is interrupted by an impossible entity connected to the Outer Dark, their rivalry becomes part of an investigation neither of them wanted.

Veilward's wards record no breach.

Because nothing entered.

As Aurelia and Aarav uncover evidence of an ancient presence hidden beneath the Academy, they discover that someone has erased pieces of history connected to a word neither of them understands: **Serpentfire**.

To stop the entity before it finds what it is searching for, two rivals must learn to trust the one person they expected to trust least.
