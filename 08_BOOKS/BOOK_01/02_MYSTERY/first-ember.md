# The First Ember

## Public Knowledge
Almost none.

Most surviving references describe it as myth, metaphor, or corrupted historical material.

## Book One Discovery
A hidden chamber beneath Veilward was believed to contain the First Ember.

The chamber is empty.

## What the Reader Learns
- Something was once kept there.
- Its removal was deliberate.
- The Echo was not simply searching for the object.
- It was checking whether the object remained.

## Connection
The chamber contains damaged references to:

- Serpentfire
- Elyra
- Varun

## Protected Truth
The full nature of the First Ember is reserved for later books.

## Rule
The First Ember is not a generic weapon or magical battery.
