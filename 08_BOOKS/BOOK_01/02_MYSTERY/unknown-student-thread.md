# Unknown Student Thread

> **Document Type:** Mystery Thread Control
> **Canon Status:** TEMPORARY PLACEHOLDER
> **Identity Status:** INTENTIONALLY UNLOCKED

## Purpose

This document controls the Unknown Student appearance introduced near the restricted archive.

The thread exists to ensure the appearance has a deliberate narrative function.

It must not become an unresolved atmospheric detail.

---

# What Is Confirmed

During the investigation phase, a student-like figure is observed near the restricted archive.

The observation occurs in circumstances where:

- access is unusual,
- the protagonists are already suspicious,
- the Academy's secrecy is becoming more apparent.

The figure creates an immediate question:

> Who was there, and why?

---

# What Is Not Yet Canon

The following remain intentionally unresolved:

- identity,
- species,
- motive,
- whether the figure was physically present,
- whether the figure was connected to the Echo,
- whether the appearance was a deliberate false lead.

No draft should establish any of these as fact without an explicit canon decision.

---

# Locked Narrative Function

For Book One, the Unknown Student serves as:

> **A controlled uncertainty that redirects suspicion toward human or student-level involvement while the protagonists are investigating an anomaly whose true nature is not yet understood.**

This creates pressure between two possibilities:

1. Someone within Veilward knows more than they should.
2. The protagonists may be interpreting incomplete or distorted evidence incorrectly.

---

# Permitted Outcomes

Before the final Book One draft is locked, the thread must resolve into one of these functions:

## Option A — Explainable False Lead

The figure was a real student with an unrelated but believable reason for being nearby.

The appearance redirects the investigation without being malicious deception.

## Option B — Echo-Related Perception Event

The figure was not a reliable physical observation.

The Echo exploited the protagonists' uncertainty or perception.

If this option is chosen, earlier Echo behavior must fairly support it.

## Option C — Genuine Minor Mystery Connection

The figure was a real person connected to restricted information but not responsible for the central threat.

Their role may become relevant later.

---

# Current Restriction

No option is selected yet.

Until a later canon decision:

- do not name the student,
- do not give the figure a dedicated character file,
- do not imply guilt,
- do not reveal an identity in dialogue,
- do not allow the thread to solve the central mystery.

---

# Book One Closure Requirement

The Unknown Student thread must not be forgotten.

Before the final Book One manuscript pass, its chosen function must be recorded.

The eventual resolution may be:

- explicit,
- quietly explained,
- or intentionally deferred.

If deferred, the reader must understand that the uncertainty itself is deliberate rather than accidental.

---

# Recommended Decision Point

Select the final function only after:

- the Book One mystery scenes are drafted,
- Echo appearances are mapped,
- Book Two's opening mystery direction is reviewed.

This preserves flexibility without allowing uncontrolled improvisation.

> **The Unknown Student is currently a question, not a character.**
