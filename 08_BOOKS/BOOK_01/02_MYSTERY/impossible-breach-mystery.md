# The Impossible Breach

## Established Rule
Veilward's ancient defenses cannot be crossed by Outer Dark entities without detection.

## The Incident
During a controlled exercise, an entity manifests inside the protected training grounds.

The defenses activate.

They detect no intrusion.

## Initial Theory
Something breached Veilward.

## Midpoint Truth
Nothing breached Veilward.

The entity, or the connection allowing it to awaken, had already existed inside the Academy.

## Book One Resolution
The protagonists identify the dormant presence and prevent its immediate objective.

## Larger Question
How long has the hidden presence existed, and who allowed history to forget it?
