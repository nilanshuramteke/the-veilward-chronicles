# The Echo

## Classification
A temporary name used by characters for the Book One entity.

## Nature
The Echo is not a ghost.

It is a fragmented remnant of consciousness connected to the Outer Dark. It lacks a stable body and survives by attaching itself to magical disturbances and vulnerable spaces.

## Distinctive Behavior
The Echo does not merely create darkness.

It imitates:

- Familiar voices imperfectly
- Memories it has partially encountered
- Shapes that change at the edge of vision

Its imitations always contain a subtle error.

## Abilities
- Distorts nearby magical perception
- Causes environmental cold and silence
- Mimics fragments of voices
- Travels through disrupted magical pathways
- Feeds on unstable magical energy

## Limitation
It cannot maintain strength without a suitable magical disturbance.

## Objective
It is searching for evidence of the First Ember's location.

## Book One Fate
The immediate manifestation is defeated or dispersed, but the wider intelligence behind its origin remains unknown.
