# Final Revelation

## What the Characters Believe
The Echo has been stopped.

## What They Discover
The hidden chamber beneath Veilward contains an empty place where the First Ember was once held.

## Surviving Inscription
A damaged inscription contains:

**ELYRA**

**VARUN**

and the surviving fragment:

> The fire was never meant to belong to either of them.

## Final Twist
The Echo was not trying to retrieve the Ember.

It was verifying whether it remained inside Veilward.

## Epilogue Revelation
A restricted record changes by itself.

**SERPENTFIRE REMAINS UNRESOLVED.**

Then:

**THE EMBER HAS MOVED.**

## Meaning
The Book One events were part of a search, not the complete attack.
