# Plot Blueprint

## Opening
Aurelia and Aarav collide during an academy competitive exercise.

## Inciting Incident
The training ground becomes unstable and an impossible entity manifests. Veilward detects no intrusion.

## Investigation
The two protagonists possess different fragments of evidence and are repeatedly forced into contact.

## First Shift
They discover that the apparent breach may not have happened at all.

## Midpoint
The central assumption collapses:

**The danger did not enter Veilward. It was already inside.**

## Escalation
Their search leads toward erased historical records, Serpentfire, and a hidden structure beneath the Academy.

## Emotional Shift
They consciously choose to trust and protect one another.

## Climax
The Echo reaches the hidden chamber it has been seeking. Aurelia and Aarav prevent it from achieving its immediate goal.

## Resolution
The Echo is defeated or contained, but the protagonists discover the First Ember was removed long ago.

## Final Revelation
An ancient restricted record changes and delivers the warning:

**THE EMBER HAS MOVED.**
