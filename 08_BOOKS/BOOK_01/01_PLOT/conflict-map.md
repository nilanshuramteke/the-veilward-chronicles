# Conflict Map

## Personal
Aurelia fears appearing dependent. Aarav relies on control and emotional distance.

## Interpersonal
Their rivalry is built on misunderstanding, competition, and incompatible approaches.

## Institutional
Veilward protects its students but does not reveal everything it knows.

## Mystery
Records contradict one another. Important history has been restricted or erased.

## External
The Echo and the larger Outer Dark connection create escalating danger.

## Convergence
The climax should unite these conflicts rather than resolving them in unrelated scenes.
