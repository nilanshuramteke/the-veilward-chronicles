# Pacing Plan

## Rule
Mystery, academy life, friendship, humor, and danger must alternate rather than compete for the entire page.

## Approximate Rhythm
- Chapters 1–4: fast introduction and hook
- Chapters 5–8: consequence and investigation
- Chapters 9–14: discovery mixed with academy life
- Chapter 15: major reversal
- Chapters 16–21: rising danger and emotional closeness
- Chapters 22–27: sustained convergence and climax
- Chapter 28: quiet but unsettling aftermath

## Quiet Scenes
At least one meaningful quiet relationship scene should appear between major action sequences.

## Mystery Rule
Every major answer should either reveal a consequence or create a better question.
