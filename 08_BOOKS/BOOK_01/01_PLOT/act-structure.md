# Act Structure

## ACT I — ARRIVAL AND RIVALRY
**Chapters 1–8**

Establish academy life, protagonists, rivalry, supporting cast, and the impossible incident.

**Act Question:** What entered Veilward?

## ACT II-A — INVESTIGATION
**Chapters 9–14**

Forced cooperation, clues, academy politics, and growing respect.

**Question:** How is this possible?

## MIDPOINT
**Chapter 15**

The breach theory is false.

**The threat was already inside Veilward.**

## ACT II-B — ESCALATION
**Chapters 16–21**

The mystery becomes personal and dangerous. Serpentfire appears in historical fragments.

**Question:** What has Veilward forgotten?

## ACT III — CONSEQUENCE
**Chapters 22–27**

The Echo reaches the hidden chamber. Personal trust and the mystery converge.

## EPILOGUE
**Chapter 28**

Immediate danger is over. A larger search has begun.
