# Chapter 5 Audit Checklist

## 1. Canon Audit

### POV
- [ ] Aurelia remains close-third POV throughout.
- [ ] No access to Aarav's private thoughts.
- [ ] Inference remains distinguishable from fact where evidence is incomplete.

### Chapter 4 continuity
- [ ] Shared anomalous event remains established fact.
- [ ] Ward record contains no accounting disturbance.
- [ ] No external-origin conclusion is introduced.
- [ ] Witness mismatch remains secondary.

### Recurring symbol
- [ ] Both Aurelia and Aarav notice it.
- [ ] Recurrence is concrete and observable.
- [ ] Meaning remains unknown.
- [ ] No creator, age, origin, or magical classification is invented.
- [ ] No deeper lore connection is implied.

### Protected information
- [ ] No Outer Dark.
- [ ] No Serpentfire.
- [ ] No First Ember.
- [ ] No erased/ancient history revelation.
- [ ] No ancient containment.
- [ ] No hidden chamber.

### Relationship
- [ ] Rivalry remains unresolved.
- [ ] No romance framing.
- [ ] No attraction framing.
- [ ] No friendship breakthrough.
- [ ] No premature forced partnership.
- [ ] No permanent hierarchy.

## 2. Continuity Audit

- [ ] Evaluation remains unresolved.
- [ ] No winner is declared.
- [ ] Warden Seraphine remains operational and non-expository.
- [ ] Chapter 3 wind remains unrelated.
- [ ] Chapter 4 event is not retroactively expanded beyond established facts.
- [ ] Aurelia's and Aarav's methodological disagreement remains unresolved.
- [ ] Chapter 6's complementary-evidence movement is not prematurely resolved.

## 3. Narrative Quality Audit

### Pacing
- [ ] Chapter does not merely repeat Chapter 4's incident.
- [ ] Institutional consequence produces a meaningful turn.
- [ ] The recurring symbol is a genuine new plot movement.
- [ ] Scene 4 or the clue sequence provides strongest forward pull.

### Characterization
- [ ] Aurelia stays active, capable, direct, and skeptical.
- [ ] Aarav remains independently analytical.
- [ ] Neither is reduced to an exposition device.
- [ ] Seraphine does not become a lore-delivery mechanism.

### Mystery
- [ ] The primary contradiction remains concrete.
- [ ] The symbol creates a better question rather than a vague unease.
- [ ] No explanation is disguised as observation.
- [ ] No ominous-negation habit is used to manufacture mystery.

### Repetition watch
Scan specifically for:
- [ ] trailing `which...` explanatory clauses,
- [ ] `the way [he/she]...`,
- [ ] `the kind of...that`,
- [ ] reduced-relative variants,
- [ ] a newly repeated substitute template,
- [ ] repeated sentence openings,
- [ ] close-proximity word echoes introduced by corrections.

### Final question
By the end of the chapter, can a first-time reader identify:
1. what happened previously,
2. what remains unexplained,
3. what institutional response is preventing easy answers,
4. what new concrete clue appeared,
5. why the story must continue?

If not, Chapter 5 is not ready for finalization.
