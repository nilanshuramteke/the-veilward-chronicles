# Chapter 5 Canon Constraints

## Authority order
Use the established canon authority matrix and Book One canon locks.

When information is absent or ambiguous, do not invent an explanation in manuscript prose.

## POV
**Aurelia, close third person, past tense.**

Aurelia may:
- observe,
- infer,
- test,
- compare,
- disagree,
- revise her view.

She may not know another character's private thoughts.

Inference must remain distinguishable from directly established fact when the evidence is incomplete.

## Chapter 5 authorized movement
Chapter 5 is authorized to:
- show Academy investigation and consequence,
- show Warden Seraphine restricting information or access,
- preserve the ward contradiction,
- continue Aurelia/Aarav disagreement,
- introduce a recurring symbol noticed by both,
- advance reader curiosity without explaining the anomaly.

## Recurring symbol firewall
The symbol may be concrete and observable.

Do not establish or strongly imply:
- its meaning,
- its creator,
- its age,
- its origin,
- its magical classification,
- its relationship to the anomaly,
- its relationship to Serpentfire,
- its relationship to the First Ember,
- its relationship to ancient containment or hidden history.

Do not use narration to tell the reader that the symbol is important beyond what the characters can reasonably infer from its recurrence.

## Ward result
Do not convert the clean record into an origin theory.

Forbidden continuity shortcuts include:
- "something entered Veilward,"
- "the wards were breached,"
- "the wards failed,"
- "the anomaly came from outside."

## Mystery firewall
Protected:
- Outer Dark,
- Serpentfire,
- First Ember,
- erased history,
- ancient history,
- ancient containment,
- hidden chamber,
- identity and origin of the Chapter 4 phenomenon.

## Relationship state
Aurelia and Aarav remain publicly visible rivals.

No:
- romance framing,
- attraction framing,
- jealousy,
- friendship breakthrough,
- emotional confession,
- permanent alliance,
- permanent hierarchy.

A forced or practical overlap may occur if scene planning requires it, but Chapter 5 must not advance to the Chapter 7 forced-partnership state prematurely.

## Warden Seraphine
Use only her established operational/security function.

She may:
- restrict movement,
- restrict access,
- manage safety response,
- deliver operational facts.

She may not become a source of hidden historical truth.

Specific procedures remain **Provisional/Local** unless canon-established elsewhere.

## Magic
Follow the Book One magic-usage lock.

No:
- Serpentfire,
- combined powers,
- unexplained mastery,
- invented permanent magic mechanics.

## Evaluation
The evaluation remains unresolved.

Do not assign a winner.

## Repetition watch
Avoid mechanically repeating known project patterns:
- trailing `which...` explanatory clauses,
- `the way [he/she]...`,
- `the kind of...that`,
- reduced-relative variants that merely disguise the same structure.

Do not solve avoidance by introducing a new repeated substitute template.

## Tone
The mystery should arise from concrete contradictions and observable consequences.

Avoid:
- vague prophecy language,
- "watching/waiting" language,
- ominous negation used as atmosphere,
- retrospective claims that earlier ordinary events were signs.

## Chapter 5 exit
By the end:
- institutional consequence is active,
- the anomaly remains unexplained,
- the ward contradiction remains intact,
- the recurring symbol is established as a clue,
- Aurelia and Aarav remain unresolved rivals,
- Chapter 6 is given a concrete investigative pressure point.
