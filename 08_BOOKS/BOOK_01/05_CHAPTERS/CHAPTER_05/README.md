## Core function
Chapter 5 begins the consequence-and-investigation phase.

The Academy responds to the anomalous event from Chapter 4. Institutional restrictions create pressure rather than answers. Aurelia and Aarav remain divided over how much significance to assign to their differing recollections. During the investigation, both independently notice the same recurring symbol.

The chapter must advance the mystery without explaining the Chapter 4 phenomenon.
