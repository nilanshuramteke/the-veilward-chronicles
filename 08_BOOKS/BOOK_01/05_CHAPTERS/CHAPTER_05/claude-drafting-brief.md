# Chapter 5 — Claude Drafting Brief

## Read before beginning

Read this complete Chapter 5 package before planning.

Then read the relevant upstream canon and continuity sources required by the repository's canon authority order.

At minimum, treat these package documents as mandatory:
- README.md
- chapter-05-blueprint.md
- chapter-04-continuity-input.md
- canon-constraints.md
- scene-breakdown.md
- chapter-05-audit-checklist.md

Also consult the finalized Chapter 4 manuscript/finalization materials and relevant Aurelia, Aarav, and Warden Seraphine character files.

## Workflow

### Phase 1 — Orientation
Confirm:
- chapter function,
- POV,
- Chapter 4 entry state,
- mystery boundaries,
- relationship state,
- evaluation status,
- authorized Chapter 5 movement.

Do not draft prose.

### Phase 2 — Detailed Scene Planning
Create the detailed scene plan.

For every scene mechanic introduced, label it:
- **Canon-established**, or
- **Provisional/Local**.

Do not silently promote local staging into Academy canon.

For the recurring symbol, explicitly distinguish:
- directly observed facts,
- reasonable character inference,
- information withheld.

### Phase 3.1 — Structural Calibration
Stress-test:
1. Is the ward contradiction still the primary inherited mystery?
2. Does the symbol function as a clue rather than an answer?
3. Does institutional restriction create pressure without exposition dumping?
4. Does Aurelia remain active?
5. Does Aarav remain an independent rival rather than becoming an ally early?
6. Is Chapter 6's separate-accounts function being prematurely consumed?

### Phase 3.2 — Final Targeted Calibration
Only after reviewing Phase 3.1.

Confirm:
- the symbol's meaning remains unknown,
- no deeper lore is implied,
- the ward result remains neutral on origin,
- the witness mismatch remains secondary,
- no Chapter 7 forced-partnership state occurs early,
- Chapter 3 wind remains unrelated.

### Phase 4 — Manuscript Draft
Draft only after the scene plan is approved.

POV: Aurelia, close third, past tense.

Write concrete observation before interpretation.

Aurelia may be wrong, skeptical, impatient, or competitive. She must not become passive.

Do not explain the anomaly or the symbol.

### Phase 5 — Combined Audit
Run:
- Canon Audit
- Continuity Audit
- Narrative Quality Audit

Report findings without editing unless explicitly instructed.

### Phase 6 — Correction and Verification
Apply only authorized corrections.

Then independently verify:
- mystery firewall,
- POV boundaries,
- ward neutrality,
- recurring-symbol boundary,
- rivalry continuity,
- repetition patterns,
- change integrity.

## Drafting priorities

1. **Concrete consequence over vague atmosphere.**
2. **Investigation pressure over exposition.**
3. **A clue is not an answer.**
4. **Aurelia's skepticism is methodological, not denial of reality.**
5. **Aarav's analytical approach must remain distinct from Aurelia's.**
6. **Do not use shared danger as a relationship shortcut.**

## Hard prohibitions

Do not reveal or imply:
- Outer Dark,
- Serpentfire,
- First Ember,
- ancient containment,
- erased history,
- hidden chamber,
- origin or identity of the phenomenon.

Do not claim:
- something entered,
- something crossed the wards,
- the wards failed.

Do not interpret Chapter 3's wind as foreshadowing.

Do not explain the recurring symbol.

## Stop condition
After each requested phase, stop and await instruction.
