# Chapter 5 Scene Breakdown

## Planning status
This is a drafting scaffold, not a finalized scene plan.

Phase 2 must preserve the outline-authorized events while deciding exact staging.

## Scene 1 — Restricted Morning

### Purpose
Show immediate institutional consequence and return to Aurelia's POV.

### Canon-established
- Chapter 4 event occurred.
- Restrictions were imposed afterward.
- Evaluation remains unresolved.

### Provisional/Local
- Exact timing after the event.
- Exact restriction staging.
- Exact location and enforcement procedure.

### Required movement
Aurelia encounters the practical consequences of the incident and resists being treated as a passive witness.

### Must preserve
- No explanation from authorities.
- No evaluation winner.
- No romance shortcut with Aarav.

---

## Scene 2 — What the Wards Did Not Say

### Purpose
Center the institutional contradiction without repeating Chapter 4 as exposition.

### Canon-established
- The ward record contains nothing accounting for the shared event.
- Warden Seraphine may operate as a security responder.

### Provisional/Local
- How information is communicated.
- Who is present.
- What immediate follow-up checks are visible.

### Required movement
Aurelia pushes for usable facts and receives restriction or limited operational information rather than answers.

### Must preserve
- No external-origin theory.
- No claim that the wards failed.
- Seraphine does not reveal hidden lore.

---

## Scene 3 — Methodology

### Purpose
Continue the Aurelia/Aarav disagreement under investigative pressure.

### Required conflict
Aurelia and Aarav disagree about the significance of their differing recollections.

The disagreement must remain methodological:
- Aarav considers the mismatch worth accounting for.
- Aurelia does not accept that mismatch alone as evidence of deeper significance.

### Required movement
Neither wins the argument.

The rivalry remains useful and active.

### Must not do
- Turn disagreement into attraction framing.
- Turn shared mystery into friendship.
- Resolve the mismatch.

---

## Scene 4 — The Recurring Symbol

### Purpose
Create Chapter 5's new concrete clue.

### Required fact
Both Aurelia and Aarav notice the same recurring symbol.

### Critical planning discipline
Phase 2 must decide:
- where Aurelia observes it,
- where Aarav observes it,
- whether they notice independently or compare observations later,
- what makes recurrence objectively recognizable,
- what is directly observed versus inferred.

### Canon boundary
The symbol's recurrence is authorized.

Its meaning is not.

### Must not establish
- origin,
- creator,
- age,
- magical purpose,
- Echo connection,
- deeper lore connection.

### Ending pressure
The recurrence should leave the reader with a specific question:

> Why is the same symbol appearing in more than one relevant place?

Do not answer that question.

---

## Recommended structural weighting
Scene 4 should carry the chapter's strongest forward movement.

Scene 3 should preserve rivalry pressure without becoming the chapter's emotional climax.

The chapter should move from consequence → institutional limitation → methodological conflict → concrete clue.
