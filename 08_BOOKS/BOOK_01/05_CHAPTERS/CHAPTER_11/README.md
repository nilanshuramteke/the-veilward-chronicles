Phase 3.2 is the final authority for Chapter 11 architecture. The Drafting Package translates that architecture into drafting instructions; it does not introduce new story decisions. Phase 4 drafting must follow this package unless a later authorized correction explicitly changes it.

## Critical Chapter Principle

**Chapter 11 lets Aurelia's instinct and Aarav's verification jointly confirm that something real and localized is wrong, at a training-ground point neither could have proven alone — advancing the mystery from "unexplained" to "unexplained but independently verifiable," and beginning Respect through demonstrated competence rather than declaration.**
