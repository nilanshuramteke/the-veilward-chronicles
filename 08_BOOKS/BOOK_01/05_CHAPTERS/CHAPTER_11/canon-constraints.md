# Chapter 11 — Canon Constraints

Phase 3.2 is the final authority for Chapter 11 architecture. This document translates that architecture into hard drafting constraints. It does not introduce new story decisions.

## 1. POV and Narrative Constraints

- Aurelia — close third person, past tense, throughout.
- No omniscient knowledge or narration.
- No Aarav interiority — he may be observed and inferred from, never read directly.
- No Archivist/Voss/instructor interiority.
- Aurelia's cognition proceeds through sensation → instinct → doubt → physical action → irritation → recognition, not analysis → classification → hypothesis → conclusion.

## 2. Chapter 10 Continuity Constraints

- Restitution complete; supervised archive access active under Voss's tightened personal supervision (background fact only, not restaged).
- The Chapter 10 historical precedent and its restriction remain background pressure — not reopened, not re-explained, not resolved.
- No new institutional policy, restriction, or authorization sequence.
- Chapter 11's active engine is the present-day Training Grounds event, not the Archive thread.

## 3. Relationship Constraints

**Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation, ending at the beginning edge of Stage 3 — Respect.**

Allowed: rivalry; competitive friction; Aarav choosing to test rather than dismiss; a small behavioral adjustment after the result is confirmed.

Forbidden: friendship; trust as a named milestone; romance beyond one optional, unexamined proximity beat; vulnerability-as-bonding; any line naming "trust," "respect," or "friendship"; "we make a good team" sentiment; reconciliation or confession language.

## 4. Romance Temperature Constraint

At most **one** small, unexamined proximity/awareness beat is permitted (e.g., both crouching near the same point). It must be brief, non-romantic in explicit interpretation, immediately subordinated to the task, and never repeated. If it does not arise naturally from the blocking, omit it entirely.

## 5. Mystery Constraints

Chapter 11 may establish: a present-day, bounded magical irregularity; that it is real, not imagined or a technique failure; that it can be independently confirmed by two different methods; that Aurelia's perception is reliable in this instance; that their methods complement each other.

Chapter 11 must not establish: cause, mechanism, entity identity, motive, intelligence, agency, or any causal/spatial connection to the specific Chapter 4 or Chapter 9/10 locations. The connection to the broader mystery is thematic/categorical only (another instance of the Academy's unexplained-magic problem) — never spatial or causal.

## 6. Magic Constraints

Allowed: Aurelia's instinctive Phoenix-fire sensitivity to a magical irregularity (produces a question, not an answer); Aarav's established Naga sensitivity to magical disturbance, used independently; ordinary controlled-training fire use; a brief, informal comparison against one neutral nearby point.

Forbidden: numerical measurement; instruments or diagnostic devices; formal experimental controls; a repeatable/on-demand activation procedure; a named classification or invented terminology for the phenomenon; any implication the characters now understand how it works; any combined Phoenix–Naga ability; any magical restoration of the Chapter 10 missing record; any fated-attraction implication.

## 7. Location Constraint

Training Grounds, a different section from the specific site emphasized in Chapter 9. No implication that this is the same physical spot as Chapter 4 or Chapter 9/10. No implication that the Training Grounds itself is anomalous or that the Academy has a network of known anomaly sites.

## 8. Dialogue Constraints

The "That's not X, that's Y" mirrored-negation construction is prohibited anywhere in this chapter. Avoid constant witty comebacks or manufactured banter. Favor clipped instructions, practical disagreement, one unfinished thought, silence carrying meaning, and action preceding explanation.

## 9. Information Firewall

Aarav retains his Chapter 8 behavioral-mismatch knowledge about the Dev-like voice. Aurelia does not know it and must not learn it, receive any hint of it, or infer it from his behavior in this chapter. No accidental transfer through dialogue, narration, or implication.

## 10. Protected Lore

The following remain fully unrevealed and unimplied: Serpentfire, Elyra, Varun, the Unknown Student, the First Ember, the Seeker, the Harbinger, the Outer Dark, the Lost Path, true Veil origin/weakness, breach/intrusion theory, the hidden chamber, and any Echo identity, origin, motive, or intelligence beyond what is already established. Serpentfire and the Unknown Student remain reserved for Chapter 12.

## 11. Other Locked Clues

**Angular mark:** dormant; not referenced; no connection to this chapter's irregularity.
**Chapter 3 wind:** unrelated; not referenced.

## 12. Supporting Cast Constraint

Only an unnamed training instructor may appear as a named-function supporting presence. No named characters (Dev, Mira, Lyra, Voss, Seraphine) may appear unless a later authorized revision provides an explicit functional reason. The instructor must be ordinary, competent, and reasonable — never secretly informed, never an exposition device.

## 13. Structural Constraint

No artificial ticking clock, emergency, attack, countdown, or institutional confrontation. Pressure comes from: Aurelia may be wrong → she checks herself → the evidence nearly fails to confirm → Aarav checks independently → the observation survives scrutiny. The chapter must remain physically active throughout, not conversation-dominant.

## 14. Ending Constraint

No authority figure, discipline, restriction, new rule, or formal assignment at the close. No walk-away ending, no "they still didn't know" statement. The final beat must be behavioral or image-based, showing a small, concrete, wordless shift in how they operate around each other.
