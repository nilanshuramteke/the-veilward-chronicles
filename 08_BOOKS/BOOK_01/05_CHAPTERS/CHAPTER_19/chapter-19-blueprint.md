### 11. Reader Experience Contract

| After | Reader should think |
|---|---|
| **S1** | I know two names and see damaged context, but I don't know who they were. |
| **S2** | I understand two competing readings, and that Aurelia intends to report. |
| **S3** | I understand Aurelia has voluntarily exposed an earlier omission. |
| **S4** | I notice that Voss never asks the obvious question. |
| **S5** | I understand Aurelia chose to put more into the record than she was required to. |

**The reader must not leave knowing** who Elyra and Varun were, what the Mark means, what Voss knows, what caused the damage, what the Echo is, or how the wall connects to the larger mythology. The chapter must be intelligible without the Story Bible.
