## Chapter Function

Chapter 19 introduces the names ELYRA and VARUN as a physical discovery, and nothing more. Aurelia finds a wall of names whose surface has been lost in a bordered band, with the two names surviving on separate lines, two scraps (STRIFE, OLD FIRE) and, low on the wall, the Angular Mark she has already seen twice and left out of her statement. The chapter is a decision chapter: she and Aarav argue two readings of the damage (neglect versus choice) and do not resolve them; she overrules his advice to hold the Mark back and reports it, including her own earlier omission; Voss examines the wall and sets the terms of the record without answering the question the reader expects him to answer; and she chooses, alone and unprompted, to put the Ch18 Echo into her own written account. The mystery is not explained.

## Final Scene Structure

Scene titles are working labels only. The manuscript prints no scene titles; scenes are separated by unlabeled `---` breaks.

1. **Scene 1 — The Wrong Kind of Damage.** Aurelia takes the long way round the east passage under her own Ch18 terms and finds the roofed walk. She reads the wall of names, the bordered band of lost surface, ELYRA and VARUN on separate lines, the fragments STRIFE and OLD FIRE, and the Angular Mark at knee height. She checks twice for heat from her own experience and rules it out as a visual judgment. She frames the loss as neglect, copies the text and sketches the Mark without touching the stone, and goes to find Aarav.
2. **Scene 2 — Two Readings.** Neglect versus choice. Aarav's evidence-first objections ("Lost and left aren't either"), her falsifier, his concession on the not-heat call, and their split on sequencing: he asks to hold the Mark until the transcription is checked; she will not hand Voss half of anything. She decides. He almost says something else and does not; she notices and does not ask. Protected and unchanged after Phase 4.
3. **Scene 3 — Before He Hears It.** The index room. Aurelia reports the wall, the Mark, her two earlier sightings, and her omission ("I should have told you then"). Voss states the omission plainly and asks seven ordinary questions. Aurelia names Aarav's shed mark ("It's his to describe"). "Show me."
4. **Scene 4 — At the Wall.** Voss reads Aarav's transcription against the rows, from a distance, and asks seven questions of clear function. He asks nothing about who Elyra and Varun were, and Aurelia says so. He sets two documents: a joint record of physical findings under both names, and separate statements of what each knew, and when, due at the last bell. He will read them tonight, and will say what he thinks only after that. His closing instruction is scoped: "Anything about the mark or the wall that you never brought to me goes in. Oldest first."
5. **Scene 5 — Oldest First.** Aurelia writes her statement at a carrel. The rail and the post go down first. She reaches the walk, decides the east passage is hers to say, and in three sentences chooses to include it. She strikes out "called", sets the entry under experienced / knew / could not verify, and keeps writing.

## Final Canon State (Established by Chapter 19)

- Aurelia discovers a damaged wall of names on a roofed walk behind the practicum hall, past the end of the back path.
- The names ELYRA and VARUN survive on separate lines within a bordered band of lost surface. Nothing legible surrounds them.
- The other surviving fragments are the single word STRIFE and, below it, OLD FIRE.
- The damage is described without an agent. "Not heat" is Aurelia's visual judgment only (pale, dry, no soot, no glaze, no cracking). Aarav concedes it on her experience ("I'll go with it") without confirming it independently.
- The **Angular Mark** is the only active mystery-thread convergence: "three straight strokes that don't quite close, with a gap where they should have met", shallow, hand-cut, at knee height under the band. It is Aurelia's third sighting (rail, post, wall) and matches Aarav's shed mark (crate lid) only as "the same kind". Neither of them, nor Voss, draws a link from the Mark to the names.
- Light Academy and world orientation comes through the wall of names ("somebody had decided these should be kept") and the weekly standing sheet pinned at eye height. There is no exposition block.
- Aurelia independently chooses to report the discovery, and volunteers her earlier omission ("I should have told you then").
- Voss sets the reporting structure on the page: a joint record of physical findings under both names, written at the wall; and separate statements of what each knew about the mark and the wall, and when, due at the last bell, not to be compared. He will examine the wall himself, this afternoon and again after reading. He will say what he thinks only after reading both statements. The wall stays as it is.
- As of the final page, Aurelia is writing her statement. She has chosen to include the Ch18 Echo (east passage, about two-thirds along, before the linen door: "a voice"). Voss has not asked for it and has not been told it. It is her own disclosure.
- The mystery remains unexplained.
