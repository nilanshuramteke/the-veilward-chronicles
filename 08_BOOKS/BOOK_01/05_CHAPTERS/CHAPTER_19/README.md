## Chapter Function

Chapter 19 gives the mystery its first human names. Aurelia finds a damaged stretch of carved names in an unnamed memorial space, with ELYRA and VARUN legible on separate lines and the surrounding text lost. She recognizes the Angular Mark cut shallow into the stone nearby, forms her own reading (Neglect), tests it against Aarav's (Choice), and reports anyway. She voluntarily discloses her own earlier Mark omission to Voss. Voss examines the wall and shows what he knows only by the question he never asks. He orders a joint record of the wall's physical facts and an individual written statement from each of them. Aurelia's statement, written alone, puts the Chapter 18 Echo event into the record by her own choice. Nothing about who Elyra and Varun were, what the Mark means, or what Voss knows is revealed.
