## 2. Hard Canon Established by Chapter 19

### The wall

- A wall of names, cut in rows, runs the length of a roofed walk behind the practicum hall, past where the back path ends. Different hands, different depths, different weathering. Neither protagonist had ever used the walk.
- A bordered band of lost surface sits a little past the middle. It is pale, dry and shed in thin sheets, with sharp rows above and below.
- Within the band: ELYRA and VARUN on separate lines, a hand's width apart, with nothing legible around them; the single word STRIFE with stone broken off short on either side; and, beneath it, OLD FIRE.
- The Angular Mark is cut low on the wall at knee height under the bottom edge of the band: "three straight strokes that don't quite close, with a gap where they should have met." Flakes and dust lie at its foot. Nothing else is on the ground.
- The judgment "not heat" is Aurelia's visual reading (no soot, no glaze, no cracking, no colour change at the rim). Aarav concedes it on her experience. It is not a forensic result, and no cause has been established.
- Voss says the wall "stays as it is."

### What Voss knows (on the page)

- The physical findings above, at the wall, with the transcription in his hand.
- That Aurelia saw the Mark twice before (a rail on the training field on the morning of the event; the third marker post the next morning), and left both out of her original statement. She told him herself, in the index room.
- That Aarav found a Mark of his own (the underside of a crate lid in the gear shed, the morning after the event) and did not report it. Aurelia named it first ("It's his to describe"). Aarav confirmed it at the wall: "I didn't report it."
- That Aarav's view was to record the wall first, and that "what she's told you about the marks is accurate."
- That Aurelia chose to bring the wall to him now ("Mine").
- That the pair disagree about the wall: Aurelia read it as neglect ("Nobody kept it up"), Aarav as undecided ("The wall doesn't say whether it was let go or chosen").
- Voss has **not** been told anything about the east passage or the Echo on the page. Aurelia's statement includes it by her own choice. Whether he has read it yet is Chapter 20's to set (see §1).

### What Voss set

- **Two documents.** The **joint record** covers findings only: what is cut, what is missing, where it sits, where the Mark is, what the damage looks like. It goes under both names, written at the wall, Aarav writing and Aurelia checking each line against the stone. The **statements** cover what each of them knew about the Mark and the wall, and when. They are separate and "you won't compare them". Both are due at the last bell, and Voss will read them that night.
- **His stated intention.** "I'll tell you what I think once I've read what each of you saw and what each of you knew. Anything I say before that ends up in your accounts." It is deferred, not resolved.
- **The wall.** He will examine it himself, "this afternoon, and again once I've read what you write."
- **His closing instruction.** "Anything about the mark or the wall that you never brought to me goes in. Oldest first." It is scoped to the mark and the wall. It does not name or imply the Echo, the east passage, Aarav's Chapter 8 comparison or the Fourteen-C copies.
- **Voss's one tell.** The unasked question: he never asks who Elyra and Varun were. Aurelia noticed and said so aloud. He does not lie, explain or add a restriction. No second tell exists.

### The Echo

- Aurelia is writing the Ch18 east-passage event into her own statement, unprompted: "Last evening, on the east passage, about two-thirds along, before the linen door: a voice." She struck out "called" and hedged ("She didn't think there had been words"), and filed it under experienced / knew / could not verify.
- It is her disclosure alone. Voss did not ask for it and did not pressure her. Chapter 20 must not retroactively make him demand it.
- **Not on the page:** any account of Aarav's own Ch18 experience (the second manifestation) in either statement.
