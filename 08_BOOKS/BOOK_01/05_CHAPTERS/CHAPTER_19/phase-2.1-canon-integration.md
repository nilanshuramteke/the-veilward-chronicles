## 2. Canon Conflicts Found

**Conflict 1 — Separate accounts vs Voss's method**
- **Source A:** Ch9. Findings come out "under both your names together. One record," because two competing accounts of restricted material is "a worse outcome." Ch7 (recalled in Ch9) has him forcing reconciliation of statements.
- **Source B:** Phase 2's order for separate accounts, with the stated reason of "testing whether they converge."
- **Why it matters:** It contradicts his stated logic with no on-page precedent.
- **Correction:** Two different instruments.
  - **(a) One joint record of the wall's physical facts**, per Ch9.
  - **(b) An individual statement from each of what they have known and when**, because the joint record covers findings and only each of them can say what each of them held back. Voss's reason is the Ch14 instruction "Everything. Not what you decided was relevant," which the Mark's omission did not meet. Ch9's rule is untouched.

**Conflict 2 — Pre-report checking vs Ch16**
- **Source A:** Ch16. "Anything further happens through me, directly, every time." Latitude "going where you judged it reasonable to go, checking what you judged reasonable to check… ends today."
- **Source B:** Phase 2's Aarav "recording window" and joint-check activity.
- **Correction:** Pre-report activity is limited to *looking and hand-transcribing*, the same register Ch18 used ("a look at a public passage"). No measuring, probing, touching beyond what is needed, or tests. Voss may note the transcription in Scene 4 as a minor cost.

**Conflict 3 — Voss's verbal acknowledgment**
- **Source A:** [author-hidden-truths.md](00_CORE/author-hidden-truths.md) header: do not use author-only information in narration unless the revelation is scheduled.
- **Source B:** Phase 2 Scene 4's minimal admission.
- **Correction:** Level 1 default. Level 2 becomes an author-elective variant (§3).

**Conflict 4 — Memorial staging**
- **Source A:** Canon "Memorial spaces," provisional and generic.
- **Source B:** Phase 2's roll, columns, recent rows and panel.
- **Correction:** See §4, Scene 1. Also ensure the inscription is **non-funerary**. A memorial-of-the-dead reading would imply their *fate*, which is protected. No death, loss, "fell," "in memory" or date wording, and no character assumes or states death or survival.

**Conflict 5 — Depth asymmetry:** removed (see §1).

**Conflict 6 — Mark's reporting history and timing wording**
- **Source A:** Ch6. Aurelia first saw the Mark on the **rail during the event**. Aarav asks "anything you… didn't put in the statement," and she reveals it. Her Ch5 statement to Seraphine (cold, silence, voices) had no mention. In Ch5 she "almost asked the proctor… thought better… filed it."
- **Source B:** Ch13–14. The Mark is absent from "three things that don't fit" and from the Ch14 disclosure (marker stone, archway figure, annex marks) *after* Voss's "Everything."
- **Source C:** Phase 2's "the day after the breach."
- **Correction:** Use "since the incident." Her first sight was during it, and the post and shed marks came the next day. The silence is aggravated: omitted from a formal statement, then omitted again after an explicit instruction to say everything. Her Ch5 choice not to ask the proctor is partly hers.

**Conflict 7 — Mark geometry (finalized-chapter conflict; do not silently choose)**
- **Source A:** Ch5. The lines "should have closed into a triangle and stopped just short."
- **Source B:** Ch6. "A gap where a fourth line should have closed it."
- **Correction:** Three straight strokes that don't quite close, with a gap. Drafting uses only that, never "triangle" and never "fourth." This also avoids any "fourth stone" echo. It is a candidate for a later repair pass.

**Conflict 8 — Ch13 pattern**
- **Source:** Ch13. Aurelia: "We should tell someone… Tonight." Aarav argues to wait. She yields.
- **Why it matters:** Scene 2's tactical dispute repeats it.
- **Correction:** Treat it as a deliberate inversion. She does *not* yield this time, and the beat is time-boxed so it cannot replay Ch13's stairwell argument.

**Minor terminology**
- The Ch11 "fourth stone" is one of eight *marker stones*, not a drill-post. The anomaly is fire guttering at the corner plus, in Ch17, the wear mismatch.
- The annex has *two* worn grooves.
- The Ch17 handoff's "Ch3/5–6" for the Mark is a tracker error.

### Scene 4 — At the Wall
- **Purpose:** Institutional response, tell and consequence.
- **Location / Time:** The wall, late morning.
- **Aurelia's objective:** Have the physical evidence read by authority.
- **Conflict:**
  - **Tell:** his eyes go first to the fragment and Mark, not the names. This is deniable as professional prioritizing. It is not a Ch12 repeat: he takes and locks nothing, and there is no "isn't cleared" line.

  - Her one challenge: "You didn't ask who they were." His answer is **Level 1 plus procedural reticence.** He will say what he thinks after reading what they saw, because anything he said now would end up in their accounts. He does not confirm or deny prior knowledge, and the reason is plainly stated (contamination risk).
- **Order:** (a) one joint record of the wall's *physical facts*, per Ch9; (b) an individual written statement from each, by the end of the day, of everything they have known and when since the incident, including anything not previously brought to him, with no comparing; (c) he examines the wall himself and does not seal or restrict it. Voss states the difference: the joint record is for findings, and the statement is about what each of them knew.
- **Evidence:** Aarav's transcription, her not-heat observation, the Mark.
- **Reader learns:** Voss knows more than he says. Their divergent frames and the earlier omission now have a consequence. **Contradiction:** the working belief that the Academy has forgotten these names is put in doubt by how Voss behaves.
- **Aurelia knows:** He didn't ask. Aarav is carrying something she can't name.
- **Aarav knows:** Everything said at the wall. He did not know she would disclose the Mark.
- **Voss knows:** Wall, Mark, prior omission. **Not** the Ch18 event.
- **Mystery state:** Institutional secrecy advances (tell only).
- **Relationship state:** Aarav backs her evidence and does not endorse her sequence, and neither smooths it over.
- **Disclosure state:** Statements ordered. The scope is not wording that forces the east passage.
- **Canon locks:** No lore from Voss. No link of names, Mark, Echo, Ember or Serpentfire. Seraphine stays out. "They'd have to wait" must not be the scene's ending beat, so the order lands as an obligation.
- **Transition:** They are sent to write, apart.
- **Word range:** 1,300–1,600
