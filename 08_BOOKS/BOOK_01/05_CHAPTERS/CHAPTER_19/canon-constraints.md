### 2. Locked Character States

#### Aurelia

**Entering:**
- Direct and physically confident, at her most controlled: an unwatched prep hour, evaluation under ten days, ranked third.
- She carries a first-hand, unverified experience from the east passage.
- She holds a joint agreement with Aarav (not that night, with conditions) and made her own decision ("That's my decision").
- She has never reported the Mark, either to the proctor (Ch5), in her Ch5 statement, or in Ch14.
- She is bound by Ch16's "through me" rule. She knows the Fourteen-C copies exist (unreported, Ch15) and does not touch them here.
- She does not know Aarav's Ch8 comparison or his attention shift.
- She has no fear. Her emotional texture is impatience with lost time.

**Agency beats (all hers, none transferred):**
1. She stops and investigates.
2. She makes the not-heat observation.
3. She forms and defends the Neglect frame, and names what would change her mind.
4. She refuses to delay the report.
5. She volunteers her earlier Mark omission, unasked.
6. She includes the Ch18 event in her statement, outside the letter of the instruction.

**Exit:** Resolved and exposed. Her statement is written and includes the Echo. Aarav doesn't know what she wrote. Voss has not answered her observation. Aarav's unspoken objection to her sequence is unaddressed.

#### Aarav

**Entering:**
- Analytical and verification-first.
- He knows all three Mark sightings (V1).
- He carries private material Aurelia doesn't know: the Ch8 comparison, his attention shift (Ch18), and the Fourteen-C copies.
- He is bound by Ch16.
- He is present only in S2 and S4.

**Function:**
- He makes two substantive claims, and no more:
  1. The evidence can't establish whether the missing context is accident or choice.
  2. Record first.
- He transcribes visually, and never probes, measures, touches or tests.
- He accepts her not-heat call on her experience.
- In S4 he confirms the shed mark when Voss asks directly, backs her evidence, and neither endorses nor overrules her sequence.

**Exit:** He holds an unspoken objection and doesn't know what she wrote.

#### Voss

**On the page:**
- Controlled, procedural, competent, and non-sinister.
- His register is flat "without heat," "no more weight than a weather report" (Ch16).
- His Ch16 terms stand.
- He knows what Aurelia tells him: the wall, the Mark's history, and her omission. He does not know the Ch18 event until he reads her statement tonight.

**Author-level only (never narrated):**
- He knows the names.
- Level 1 only: the unasked question.
- He never lies ("I don't know them" is forbidden).
- He never claims a complete account.
- He never acknowledges recognition.

**One commitment made at the wall:** he will say what he thinks after reading what each of them saw and knew. This stays unfulfilled in Ch19 and belongs to Ch20.

- **Current stage:** Stage 2 / edge of Stage 3. Nothing is assumed on entering Ch19 beyond trust built through shared uncertainty. Aarav's Ch18 attention beat is private, unlabeled and unspoken.
- **Movement in Ch19:** trust through disagreement. Each concedes one real thing: Aarav accepts her not-heat call, and Aurelia accepts his alternative can't be excluded. Aarav backs her evidence but not her sequence, and neither smooths it over. Aurelia doesn't seek his notes.

  - Repairing the sequence disagreement.
- **Romance-coded beats: ZERO.**

### 8. Canon Protection / Forbidden Reveals

**Never reveal or imply:**
- Who Elyra and Varun were, or their species, relationship, romance, fate, era, death, role, reputation or legendary status.
- First Ember, Serpentfire's meaning, the Lost Path, the Veil's origin.
- Outer Dark, Seeker, Harbinger, Hidden Chamber history.
- What the Mark means, who made it, or how old it is.
- What Voss knows, how, or since when.
- What the Echo is, or any link to the wall, the Mark or the names.
- The cause or age of the damage.
- Whether the Academy suppressed anything.

**Forbidden words and constructions:**

| Category | Words |
|---|---|
| **Mark** | triangle, fourth, emblem, seal, signature, symbol of X, ancient mark, "identical," and the author label "Angular Mark." Use only "mark" or "shape," and the safe formulation. |
| **Names** | ancient, legendary, famous, infamous, forgotten heroes, lost lovers, important figures, lost, dead |
| **Inscription** | against, between, betrayed, betrayal, responsible, guilty, belong/belonged, either, first, ember, war, feud, quarrel, versus; no grammatical sentence; no legible verbs or pronouns; no dates |
| **Damage** | erased, chiseled, scraped, hacked, smoothed, removed, deliberately, covered up, suppressed. Use worn, missing, flaked, lost, broken surface. |
| **Frames** | blame, guilty, "falsely accused," erased, covered up |
| **Magic** | flame, glow, sensing, palm hovering, any touch of the wall or Mark |

**Also forbidden:**
- Level 2 acknowledgment.
- A second Voss tell.
- Any "isn't cleared" construction.
- Ch25's reserved line ("The fire was never meant to belong to either of them").
- Naming or elaborating the space.
- Quoting any other carved name.
