# Chapter 4 Scene Breakdown — Planning Framework

## Status
This is a drafting framework, not a locked scene plan.

Phase 2 must convert this into a detailed scene plan before manuscript drafting.

## Scene architecture

### Scene 1 — Normal Before
**Purpose:** Re-establish immediate normality after Chapter 3 and anchor the chapter in Aarav's perspective before the anomaly begins.

**Required work:**
- Aarav enters or continues the evaluation environment.
- Public awareness of the Aurelia–Aarav rivalry may exist as background pressure, not the chapter's main engine.
- Establish enough ordinary procedure that its interruption will register clearly.
- Aarav's attention should favor structure, timing, patterns, and observable facts.

**Do not:**
- seed retroactive mystery into Chapter 3's wind,
- manufacture ominous atmosphere before the actual incident,
- over-explain evaluation mechanics.

---

### Scene 2 — The Change
**Purpose:** Make normality fail in a way witnesses cannot easily categorize.

**Canon event targets:**
- cold,
- silence,
- environmental/training change,
- the first sign that the expected situation no longer holds.

**Narrative requirement:**
The anomaly should be experienced before it is interpreted.

Aarav should initially attempt ordinary explanations where plausible.

**Do not:**
- assign intention to the anomaly,
- identify its source,
- imply the wind caused it,
- explain mystery lore.

---

### Scene 3 — Voices / Echo
**Purpose:** Deliver the chapter's central impossible event.

**Canon event targets:**
- voices that do not belong,
- the Echo manifests.

**Planning requirement:**
Phase 2 must define what characters can directly observe without defining what the Echo actually is.

**Information control:**
Observation is allowed.
Explanation is not.

Avoid prematurely establishing:
- intelligence,
- motive,
- origin,
- species,
- historical identity,
- Outer Dark connection,
- or containment history.

---

### Scene 4 — The Impossible Result
**Purpose:** Convert the event into a durable mystery.

**Canon event target:**
Veilward's defenses detect no intrusion.

**Required dramatic movement:**
Immediate event → attempted verification → contradiction.

The contradiction must be clear:
something happened, but the expected system provides no confirming explanation.

**Ending function:**
Create forward pressure into Chapter 5's investigation.

Do not resolve:
- whether anything entered,
- where it came from,
- what the Echo is,
- or what the Academy knows.

## Scene-weight guidance
Scene 3 should carry the most dramatic weight.

Scene 1 should be long enough to establish Aarav's baseline.
Scene 2 should make the transition unmistakable.
Scene 4 should land the contradiction cleanly and avoid exposition dumping.

## Phase 2 requirement
For every proposed scene mechanic, label it:
- **Canon-established**, or
- **Provisional/local**.

Do not allow provisional mechanics to harden into canon merely through planning language.
