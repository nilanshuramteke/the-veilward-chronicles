# Chapter 4 Audit Checklist

## 1. Canon audit
Check:
- Chapter 4 POV is Aarav.
- The Impossible Breach events match the chapter outline.
- No invented specific timeline durations.
- No unsupported Academy rules become permanent canon.
- No unsupported character backstory is added.
- Magic remains within established locks.
- No Serpentfire or hybrid power.
- No permanent Aurelia/Aarav hierarchy.

## 2. Continuity audit
Check against Chapter 3:
- Evaluation remains unresolved at entry.
- Rivalry is publicly visible.
- No winner has been established.
- Lyra remains independent.
- Chapter 3 wind remains ordinary and is not retroactively converted into foreshadowing.
- No mystery signs are falsely claimed to have appeared earlier.

## 3. POV audit
- Aarav has no access to other characters' private thoughts.
- Inferences remain clearly distinguishable from facts.
- Aarav's analytical register remains distinct from Aurelia's POV voice.
- Aarav does not become an omniscient mystery narrator.

## 4. Mystery firewall audit
Allowed:
- cold,
- silence,
- voices that do not belong,
- Echo manifestation,
- defenses detecting no intrusion.

Forbidden unless independently authorized:
- Echo explanation,
- origin,
- motive,
- Outer Dark connection,
- Serpentfire,
- First Ember,
- ancient history,
- hidden chamber,
- later-book answers.

## 5. Narrative quality audit
Evaluate:
1. Opening hook
2. Pacing
3. Scene purpose
4. Emotional progression
5. Aarav characterization
6. Aurelia characterization through Aarav POV
7. Dialogue quality
8. Rivalry dynamic
9. Exposition density
10. Description density
11. Sentence variety
12. Repetitive language
13. Artificial AI writing patterns
14. Chapter ending strength

## 6. Repetition scan
Specifically scan for:
- trailing `which...` clause repetition,
- `the way he/she...` constructions,
- repeated replacement templates,
- repeated `the kind of...` constructions,
- repeated sentence openings,
- unnecessary close-proximity word echoes.

Do not mechanically replace one repeated structure with another.

## 7. Wind check
Confirm:
- no sentient wind,
- no suspicious wind,
- no retroactive Chapter 3 connection,
- no mystery attribution to ordinary environmental conditions.

## 8. Reader-promise check
Confirm Chapter 4:
- introduces a genuine plot-level question,
- changes the book's narrative engine,
- makes the reader want an explanation,
- but does not reveal answers scheduled for later chapters.

## 9. Final approval requirement
Before finalization:
- Canon PASS
- Continuity PASS
- Narrative PASS
- Repetition PASS
- Mystery firewall PASS
- Reader-promise PASS
