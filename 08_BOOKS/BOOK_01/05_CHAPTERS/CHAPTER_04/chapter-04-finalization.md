## Primary Mystery State

### Established Fact

Something observable and shared happened inside Veilward. The ward record contains nothing capable of accounting for what the witnesses experienced.

### Secondary Complication

Aarav and Aurelia do not fully agree on certain specific details of their recollections. This mismatch is unresolved and remains secondary to the larger contradiction.

### Explicitly Unknown
