## Core purpose
Chapter 4 begins Book 1's central mystery.

The Academy evaluation environment is interrupted by an impossible event. Cold, silence, and voices that do not belong enter the immediate experience of the chapter. The Echo manifests, yet Veilward's defenses detect no intrusion.

The chapter must create a plot-level question without answering it.

## Key downstream rule
Chapter 3's wind remains ordinary environmental difficulty. Chapter 4 must not retroactively reinterpret it as mystery foreshadowing.
