# Chapter 9 — Scene Breakdown

## Scene 1 — Consequence in Motion
**Purpose:** Establish that Chapter 8 has real, active consequences.

Aurelia is already resetting the disturbed cord line at dawn, under a proctor's watch, when the chapter opens. Aarav arrives to discover he's been assigned the identical duty. Dev passes by briefly — ordinary, oblivious, mildly humorous, entirely unaware. No recap of Chapter 8. No supernatural content.

**Exit:** The proctor says Seraphine is coming to inspect the work herself.

## Scene 2 — Institutional Terms
**Purpose:** Define the stakes and introduce Voss's pending role.

Seraphine arrives and inspects the work while walking the line, not seated. She confirms the restitution and states that a further condition, governed by Voss, depends on the work being completed properly first. Strictly procedural — no lore, no speculation.

**Exit:** Seraphine leaves; Voss will come once the work is done.

## Scene 3 — Negotiation / Friction
**Purpose:** Surface the real operational disagreement.

While continuing the labor, Aurelia argues for maximum latitude under any future access; Aarav argues for strict adherence to whatever is granted, given that unauthorized action already cost them once. This is a disagreement about method, not about the mystery's content. Aarav visibly registers one of her points without conceding.

**Exit:** They reach the hardest remaining task — the leaning marker post.

## Scene 4 — Demonstrated Cooperation
**Purpose:** Show cooperation through action, including a real failure.

The post must be reset correctly or the proctor won't sign off. First attempt: Aurelia sets it fast by feel, misjudging the alignment; it settles crooked. Second attempt: she holds and adjusts by feel while Aarav measures against the spacing of the other posts and directs the precise angle; it seats correctly. Aurelia gives one narrow, grudging line of credit; Aarav adjusts his directions to her pace.

**Exit:** The proctor confirms the work; word is sent that Voss has arrived.

## Scene 5 — Active Continuation
**Purpose:** Lock the arrangement and launch Chapter 10.

Voss states the terms on-site: limited, supervised access to relevant records, contingent on one shared account between them, echoing his Chapter 7 method. He proposes personally supervising every session; Aurelia negotiates this down to supervision by any authorized faculty member — a narrow, one-time concession, not a policy. He names the next available supervised session as the point the arrangement takes effect.

**Chapter ends here — no walk-away, no departure, the arrangement already active.**
