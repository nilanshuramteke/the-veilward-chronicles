# Chapter 9 — Phase 4 Combined Audit Checklist

## Structure
- [ ] Five functional scenes (or fewer if collapsed without losing function).
- [ ] Single continuous location/timeframe; no unnecessary travel.
- [ ] Scene 1 opens in active consequence, not recap or reflection.
- [ ] Scene 4 is the chapter's strongest physical/action scene.
- [ ] Scene 5 is short and non-expository.
- [ ] No static evidence-comparison structure.
- [ ] No walk-away ending; ending is an already-active obligation.

## Physical Progression
- [ ] Aurelia is already performing restitution labor when the chapter opens.
- [ ] Aarav's assignment to the same task is discovered, not arranged off-page.
- [ ] Seraphine and Voss arrive into the physical scene; protagonists do not travel to them.
- [ ] The marker-post task appears on the page as real physical action.
- [ ] The first attempt at the post fails plausibly; the second succeeds through combined effort.

## Information Boundary
- [ ] Aurelia's knowledge is limited to "the voice resembled Dev."
- [ ] Aarav's specific behavioral-mismatch observation is never transferred to Aurelia's knowledge or narration.
- [ ] No new mystery evidence, inference, or manifestation appears anywhere in the chapter.

## Institutional Canon
- [ ] Restitution is used as an already-established disciplinary category, not invented.
- [ ] Voss's authority remains personal and narrow (gatekeeper of restricted knowledge), not a department or program.
- [ ] Any supervision concession is framed as Voss's one-time discretion, not a policy/roster/certification.
- [ ] Seraphine remains strictly procedural; she does not interpret or explain the anomaly.
- [ ] The Chapter 7 "one account, not two" precedent is reused accurately if a shared-account requirement appears.

## Dev / Supporting Cast
- [ ] Dev (if present) is ordinary, brief, and unaware; no suspicion, no clue function.
- [ ] Mira/Lyra are not inserted without a concrete function.
- [ ] Seraphine and Voss each stay within their established knowledge boundary.

## Mystery Firewall
- [ ] No Outer Dark, Seeker, Unnamed, Harbinger, First Ember, Serpentfire, Elyra, Varun, Lost Path.
- [ ] No Veil origin/weakness, historical precedent, breach theory, hidden chamber.
- [ ] No Echo identity/origin/motive/intelligence/awareness/intent.
- [ ] Angular mark absent or purely background with no new causality.
- [ ] Chapter 3 wind not referenced or paralleled.

## Relationship
- [ ] Aurelia and Aarav remain rivals — Stage 2 only.
- [ ] Permitted movement present: tactical dependence, one grudging credit, one point-registered-without-conceding beat, one negotiated concession.
- [ ] No friendship, trust, romance, vulnerability-as-bonding, or emotional reflection on the relationship.

## POV
- [ ] Aurelia close-third throughout.
- [ ] No access to Aarav's private reasoning beyond observable behavior/inference.
- [ ] Aurelia's register stays kinetic/direct; does not drift into Aarav's analytical/cataloguing register.

## Prose Pattern Audit
- [ ] Repeated trailing "which..."
- [ ] "the way [subject][verb]"
- [ ] "the kind of...that"
- [ ] "didn't have a clean answer" / equivalent
- [ ] Cataloguing/"filed it away" language
- [ ] Excessive "already"
- [ ] Symmetrical dialogue volleys
- [ ] Repeated "one character leaves while the other watches"

## Ending / Chapter 10 Bridge
- [ ] Ending states the next supervised session as already active, not a future promise.
- [ ] Chapter 10's archive-access opening is fully and legitimately set up.
- [ ] No archive content, historical material, or protected lore is revealed in Chapter 9.

## Correction Protocol
If audit finds issues, use the authorized Phase 5 correction process. Do not silently expand the story while correcting prose. Any proposed canon/mechanism change must stop the correction pass and return to calibration.
