# Chapter 9 — Rules of Cooperation
## Final Blueprint

### Core Function
Convert Chapter 8's disciplinary consequence into a concrete, contested, practical working arrangement — demonstrated through action, not discussion.

### Dramatic Engine
**institutional consequence → practical constraint → rivalry-based negotiation → demonstrated cooperation → active continuation**

### Setting
Single continuous location and timeframe: the restricted field, one morning. No travel between locations; authority figures (Seraphine, Voss) arrive into the ongoing physical labor rather than summoning the protagonists elsewhere.

### Scene Sequence

**Scene 1 — Consequence in Motion**
Aurelia is already performing restitution labor (resetting the disturbed cord line) at dawn when the chapter opens. Aarav arrives to find he's been assigned the same duty. Dev passes by briefly — ordinary, oblivious, mildly humorous, unaware. No recap of Chapter 8. No supernatural content.
*Exit: Seraphine is coming to inspect the work.*

**Scene 2 — Institutional Terms**
Seraphine arrives and inspects the work while talking, not seated. She confirms restitution and states that a further condition — governed by Voss — depends on the work being completed properly. Procedural only; no lore.
*Exit: Seraphine leaves; Voss will come once the work is done.*

**Scene 3 — Negotiation / Friction**
While continuing the labor, Aurelia and Aarav argue about how cautiously to treat any future access: Aurelia wants maximum latitude; Aarav wants strict adherence, given that unauthorized action already cost them once. This is an operational disagreement, not a debate about the mystery's content. Aarav visibly registers (without conceding to) one of her points.
*Exit: They reach the hardest remaining task — the leaning marker post.*

**Scene 4 — Demonstrated Cooperation**
The post must be reset correctly or the proctor won't sign off. First attempt: Aurelia sets it fast by feel, misjudging the alignment; it settles crooked. Second attempt: she holds and adjusts by feel while Aarav measures against the other posts' spacing and directs the precise angle; it seats correctly. Aurelia gives one narrow, grudging line of credit. Aarav adjusts his directions to her pace.
*Exit: The proctor confirms the work; word is sent that Voss has arrived.*

**Scene 5 — Active Continuation**
Voss states the terms on-site: limited, supervised access to relevant records, granted personally by him, contingent on producing one shared account between them (reusing his Chapter 7 method). He proposes supervising personally every time; Aurelia negotiates this down to supervision by any authorized faculty member — a narrow, situation-specific concession, not a new policy. He names the next available supervised session as the point the arrangement takes effect — already active.
*Chapter ends here.*

### Ending State
- Restitution complete.
- Conditional, supervised research access granted, with Aurelia's negotiated flexibility on supervision.
- Relationship: rivals with a contested, jointly-shaped, functioning working arrangement. No advancement beyond Stage 2.
- Mystery: unresolved; unchanged since Chapter 8.
- Next supervised session already the active next step.

### Chapter 10 Bridge
Chapter 9 hands Chapter 10 a legitimate, earned, conditional research-access arrangement with an already-active next session. Chapter 10 opens directly into Aarav using that access. No archive content, historical material, or protected lore is revealed in Chapter 9.
