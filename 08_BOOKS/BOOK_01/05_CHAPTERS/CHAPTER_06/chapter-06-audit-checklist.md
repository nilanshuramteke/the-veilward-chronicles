# Chapter 6 Audit Checklist

## 1. Canon Audit

Verify:
- [ ] Aarav is strict close third, past tense.
- [ ] No protected lore appears.
- [ ] The anomaly is not identified or explained.
- [ ] No intelligence, awareness, motive, or intent is assigned to the anomaly.
- [ ] Witness discrepancies are not explained.
- [ ] The mark's meaning, origin, maker, age, purpose, and magical status remain unknown.
- [ ] The mark is not declared a ward or sigil.
- [ ] No causal link between the mark and the anomaly is established.
- [ ] Ward language remains neutral.
- [ ] No permanent Academy mechanics are accidentally invented.
- [ ] No Serpentfire or unauthorized magic appears.

## 2. Continuity Audit

Verify:
- [ ] Chapter 4 shared-event facts remain accurate.
- [ ] The event itself remains undisputed.
- [ ] Granular witness differences remain possible.
- [ ] The ward contradiction remains unresolved.
- [ ] The field restriction and suspended evaluation remain consistent if referenced.
- [ ] No winner is declared.
- [ ] The Chapter 5 mark recurrence remains accurate.
- [ ] Chapter 3 wind is not reinterpreted or connected.

## 3. Mystery Hierarchy

Verify:
- [ ] Ward contradiction remains primary.
- [ ] Witness discrepancy supports the primary mystery.
- [ ] The mark remains secondary.
- [ ] The mark is a clue, not an answer.
- [ ] Aurelia's detail creates factual overlap without explanation.

## 4. Character Audit

### Aarav
- [ ] Precise and analytical.
- [ ] Tests ordinary explanations.
- [ ] Does not convert pattern recognition into certainty.
- [ ] Does not gain privileged knowledge.

### Aurelia
- [ ] Independently competent.
- [ ] Her observation remains genuinely hers.
- [ ] She can challenge Aarav's methodology.
- [ ] She is not reduced to confirming his theory.

## 5. Relationship Audit

Verify:
- [ ] Rivalry remains unresolved.
- [ ] No friendship breakthrough.
- [ ] No romance shortcut.
- [ ] No emotional intimacy created by shared information.
- [ ] No joint plan.
- [ ] No investigative partnership.
- [ ] Complementary evidence is established without collaboration.

## 6. Reader-Contract Audit

Verify:
- [ ] Chapter opens on active mystery movement.
- [ ] No routine drill or evaluation delays the mystery.
- [ ] New information changes the reader's understanding of the evidence.
- [ ] The chapter ends with forward pressure into Chapter 7.

## 7. Narrative Quality Audit

Scan for:
- [ ] vague manufactured unease,
- [ ] entity-like environmental framing,
- [ ] trailing `which...`,
- [ ] `the way [subject][verb]`,
- [ ] `the kind of...that`,
- [ ] disguised reduced-relative variants,
- [ ] repeated replacement templates,
- [ ] unnecessary "already" clustering or positional echo,
- [ ] symmetrical dialogue volleys,
- [ ] exposition explaining what evidence does not support.

## Finalization Gate

Chapter 6 is not finalization-ready unless:
- [ ] canon audit passes,
- [ ] continuity audit passes,
- [ ] mystery firewall passes,
- [ ] relationship boundary passes,
- [ ] reader-contract audit passes,
- [ ] any major narrative-quality finding is corrected and re-verified.
