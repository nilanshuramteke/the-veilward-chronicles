# Chapter 6 Canon Constraints

## Authority Order
Use the established Book One canon authority matrix, canon locks, Chapter 5 finalization state, and Chapter 5 → Chapter 6 continuity handoff.

When information is absent or ambiguous, do not invent an explanation in manuscript prose.

## POV
**Aarav, close third person, past tense.**

Aarav may:
- observe,
- compare,
- test,
- infer,
- revise a hypothesis,
- notice inconsistency.

He may not:
- know another character's private thoughts,
- narrate protected facts as intuition,
- convert incomplete evidence into certainty.

Observation and interpretation must remain distinguishable.

## Chapter 6 Authorized Movement
The chapter is authorized to:
- advance the witness-account contradiction,
- establish that granular perceptions of the shared event differ,
- reveal that Aurelia perceived a detail Aarav did not,
- connect that detail factually to the already-established three-stroke angular mark,
- increase investigative pressure without providing explanation.

## Mystery Firewall

Do not reveal or imply:
- what the Echo is,
- its identity,
- origin,
- nature,
- purpose,
- intelligence,
- awareness,
- motive,
- whether it has appeared before.

Protected lore remains unavailable:
- Outer Dark,
- Serpentfire,
- First Ember,
- ancient or erased history,
- ancient containment,
- hidden chamber,
- later Book One revelations.

**Observation is not explanation.**

## Witness Discrepancy Boundary

The event itself remains undisputed.

Witness differences may concern:
- exact voice fragments,
- count,
- placement,
- sequence,
- visible particulars,
- other granular sensory details.

Do not use disagreement to imply:
- the event was imaginary,
- one witness is lying,
- the phenomenon is intentionally tailoring itself,
- the phenomenon is intelligent or aware.

No explanation for the discrepancy is authorized.

## Angular Mark Firewall

Confirmed:
- three straight strokes,
- angular,
- incomplete,
- does not cleanly close,
- recurrence in at least two independently discovered locations.

Chapter 6 may establish a factual resemblance between Aurelia's recalled detail and this mark.

Do not establish:
- meaning,
- maker,
- age,
- origin,
- purpose,
- magical status,
- ward/sigil classification,
- causal relationship to the anomaly.

The mark remains a clue, not an answer.

## Ward Neutrality

Established fact:

**Veilward's ward record contains no disturbance capable of accounting for what witnesses experienced.**

Do not introduce:
- entry,
- crossing,
- breach,
- exit,
- inside-origin,
- outside-origin,
- ward failure,

as explanations or established facts.

## Relationship Boundary

Aurelia and Aarav remain:
- rivals,
- independent,
- not friends,
- not romantic partners,
- not investigative partners.

No:
- romance shortcut,
- emotional confession,
- friendship breakthrough,
- permanent alliance,
- joint investigation.

Chapter 7 remains responsible for the forced-cooperation transition.

## Institutional Characters

Warden Seraphine remains operational, not explanatory.

If present, she may:
- manage safety response,
- restrict access,
- manage witness procedures,
- provide operational facts.

She may not explain hidden history or protected lore.

## Magic

Follow the Book One magic-usage lock.

Do not introduce:
- Serpentfire,
- combined powers,
- unexplained mastery,
- permanent new magic mechanics.

## Evaluation

The evaluation remains unresolved.

No winner may be declared.

## Chapter 3 Firewall

The Chapter 3 wind remains unrelated.

Do not reinterpret it, connect it to the anomaly, or identify it as an earlier warning.

## Repetition Watch

Avoid mechanically recurring:
- trailing `which...` explanatory clauses,
- `the way [he/she]...`,
- `the kind of...that`,
- reduced-relative variants that disguise the same construction.

Do not replace these with a new repeated substitute template.

## Tone

The mystery should arise from:
- concrete observations,
- conflicting accounts,
- failed reconciliation of facts,
- institutional limits.

Avoid:
- vague unease,
- prophecy language,
- "watching/waiting" language,
- entity-like framing,
- narration that tells the reader something is important before evidence supports it.

## Chapter 6 Exit

By the end:
- the ward contradiction remains primary and unresolved,
- witness discrepancies are more specific,
- Aurelia has a detail Aarav did not perceive,
- that detail factually intersects with the recurring mark,
- the mark remains unexplained,
- Aarav and Aurelia possess complementary evidence,
- they remain rivals and independent,
- Chapter 7 has pressure to force controlled cooperation.
