# Chapter 6 — Separate Accounts

## Package Purpose
This is the regenerated drafting package for Chapter 6 of *The Veilward Chronicles*, Book One.

Chapter 6 follows the finalized Chapter 5 state and prepares the story for Chapter 7, **Unwanted Partners**.

## Core Chapter Function
Witness accounts of the Chapter 4 event are compared and found to contradict one another in specific details. Aarav discovers that Aurelia perceived something he did not. A detail from her account intersects with the recurring three-stroke angular mark established in Chapter 5.

The chapter advances the mystery without explaining it and establishes that Aurelia and Aarav possess different pieces of relevant evidence.

## POV
Aarav — close third person, past tense.

## Primary Mystery
A shared anomalous event occurred, but Veilward's ward record contains nothing capable of accounting for it.

## Secondary Clue
The same three-stroke angular mark has appeared independently in two locations.

## Chapter 6 Exit State
Aurelia and Aarav possess complementary evidence but remain rivals, not partners. No shared investigation begins here. Chapter 7 remains responsible for forcing or authorizing cooperation.
