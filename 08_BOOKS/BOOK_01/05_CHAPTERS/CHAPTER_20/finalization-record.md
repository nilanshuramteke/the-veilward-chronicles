## Final Scene Structure

Scene titles are working labels from the drafting package; the manuscript prints none.

1. **Scene 1 — After the Statement.** Aarav, alone at the cloister rail while the lanterns are lit, the evening his statement is with Voss. He recognizes that answering to the edge of Voss's question is not the same as answering the responsibility behind it.
2. **Scene 2 — Voss's Assessment.** Voss's office. He sets out what the two statements establish (agreement on the physical wall; the meaning, cause and connections not established; Aurelia's east-passage report filed as one unverified event) and sets his rule: they do not decide alone what is worth bringing to him. Aarav realizes privately that the rule covers something he kept back from the east passage.
3. **Scene 3 — Consequence.** Walking back along the Training Grounds fence, past the roped-off stretch where workers are levering at the fourth marker stone (named; nothing explained), Aarav tells Aurelia the Fourteen-C pages are still in his pack. She owns her share of not having pressed it.
4. **Scene 4 — What We Do Alone.** A public courtyard bench. Aarav discloses his Chapter 8 comparison: a family resemblance between the field voice and what he heard in the passage. He states its limits and claims nothing more.
5. **Scene 5 — The Choice.** The long gallery. Aarav and Aurelia hand Voss the Fourteen-C transcription (the holdings entry marked fourteen-C). He puts the pages inside his coat, against his chest, and buttons the coat over them. Closing image: "Voss stood where he was, the pages inside his coat."
