## Purpose

This package documents the approved Chapter 20 planning process and consolidates it into a single, implementation-ready drafting contract for the downstream Phase 3 Full Manuscript Draft step.

## Chapter Function

Chapter 20 is where the rules around knowing change. Aarav answered what he understood he had been asked, and stopped. Voss reads both Chapter 19 statements and tells them, on the page, what the records establish and what they do not. Aarav admits to Aurelia that the Fourteen-C transcription is still in his pack and was never surfaced. She asks what else he has decided not to say, and he gives her the Chapter 8 comparison he kept to himself. She refuses to let him decide alone what she gets to know, without asking him for certainty. Together they hand the Fourteen-C transcription to Voss. Nothing about the Echo, the Mark, the wall, Elyra and Varun, or Fourteen-C's meaning is explained.
