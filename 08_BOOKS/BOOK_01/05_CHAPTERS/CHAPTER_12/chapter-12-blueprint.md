# Chapter 12 — Blueprint

## Chapter Title
**SERPENTFIRE** (outline title: "A Name Without Meaning")

## POV
Aarav — close third person, past tense.

## Core Dramatic Question

**What happens when the investigative tools Aurelia and Aarav have begun relying on — supervised access, institutional trust, and each other's judgment — are no longer fully under their control?**

## Primary Narrative Engine

**Discovery under institutional constraint → consequence.**

An authorized task incidentally surfaces SERPENTFIRE; Voss's reaction to it creates a narrowed window of reduced attention; an independently-present Unknown Student is noticed in that window; the resulting report triggers an institutional review that suspends the protagonists' primary investigative resource.

## Core Chapter Movement

**Controlled investigation → new discovery → uncontrolled complication → institutional consequence.**

## Mandatory Functions

1. Supervised Archive session, opening under ordinary procedure.
2. Damaged archival material, handled as part of an authorized task connected to (but distinct from) the Chapter 10 historical-precedent thread.
3. Mundane physical handling (not magic) makes the surviving word legible.
4. The word **SERPENTFIRE** is revealed.
5. Voss recognizes/reacts to it — controlled, not explanatory.
6. Voss makes a brief, procedurally justified movement (securing the fragment elsewhere in the same room/area) without abandoning supervision.
7. The Unknown Student is independently present near the restricted section — not caused by Voss's movement.
8. Aarav notices the student, because his own attention happens to be free, not because the plot directs him to look.
9. Aarav's observation (behavioral detail + inconclusive Naga-sensitivity impression) remains genuinely ambiguous.
10. Aurelia and Aarav disagree over whether to pursue the student — a real difference in risk tolerance, resolved by neither side "winning."
11. Aarav weighs the personal/investigative cost and chooses to report the sighting.
12. Voss responds procedurally — concerned, not riddling.
13. Seraphine's authority initiates a security/procedural review (compressed on-page presence, if any).
14. Supervised Archive access is suspended pending review.
15. Chapter 13 inherits this constraint as its opening condition.

## Forbidden Functions

Do not:
- explain what SERPENTFIRE is or means,
- resolve or explain the Unknown Student's identity, motive, or nature,
- connect either thread to protected lore (Outer Dark, Seeker, Harbinger, First Ember, Elyra, Varun, Lost Path, true Veil origin/weakness, Echo identity/origin/motive, true breach theory),
- solve the larger mystery or the Chapter 4 breach,
- repeat Chapter 11's validation structure (Aurelia notices → Aarav confirms),
- advance the relationship past the beginning edge of Respect,
- create artificial danger, a hard ticking clock, or an invented Academy system/department/policy,
- let Aurelia's magic function as a detector,
- let Aarav's Naga sensitivity function as a diagnostic or identity-confirming sense,
- transfer Aarav's Chapter 8 knowledge to Aurelia.

## Ending Requirement

The chapter must end with a materially altered situation — supervised Archive access suspended pending review — not a walk-away, a vague promise to investigate further, or a generic authority warning.
