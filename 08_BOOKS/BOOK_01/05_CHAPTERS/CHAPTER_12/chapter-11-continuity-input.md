## Important Transition

Chapter 12 must **not** repeat Chapter 11's engine:

> Aurelia notices → Aarav tests → Aurelia is independently confirmed.

Chapter 12 uses a different engine: **discovery under institutional constraint, collapsing into consequence.** Agency shifts away from the protagonists — Voss and Seraphine make the chapter's pivotal decisions — and the chapter ends with something taken from them (Archive access) rather than something earned.

## What Chapter 12 Inherits

- The still-dormant Chapter 10 Archive/historical-precedent thread (background pressure only).
