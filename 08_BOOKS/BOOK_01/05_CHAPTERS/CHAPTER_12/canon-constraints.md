# Chapter 12 — Canon Constraints

> **Authority:** This document is binding for Phase 4 Drafting. It consolidates Phase 1 Orientation, Phase 2 Detailed Plan, Phase 3.1 Targeted Calibration + Corrections, and Phase 3.2 Final Calibration. Where any earlier Chapter 12 document conflicts with this one, this one governs.

## POV

**Aarav — close third person, past tense.** This is locked by the Book 1 chapter outline and reaffirmed through every planning phase. Chapter 12 is Aarav's alternating counterpart to Aurelia's Chapter 11.

- Full Aarav interiority is permitted and expected — his classification-failure regarding the Unknown Student and his internal weighing before reporting are both interiority beats that require his POV to function.
- Everything about Aurelia must be inferred through her speech, behavior, physical action, and Aarav's interpretation. No Aurelia interiority.
- His private Chapter 8 knowledge (the Dev-like voice's behavioral mismatch) may appear in his interiority only insofar as it stays unshared and unreferenced in connection with this chapter's events — it must not surface as a comparison point for the Unknown Student or anything else in Chapter 12.

## Relationship

**Current state:** Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation, beginning edge of Stage 3 — Respect (carried forward from Chapter 11).

**Chapter 12 movement:** Respect is *tested* through genuine disagreement (chase vs. restraint) and *holds* through restraint on both sides — not advanced through validation or declaration.

Maintain:
- rivalry and friction as intact and undiminished,
- practical cooperation,
- growing behavioral (never verbal) respect.

Do not advance to:
- established friendship,
- emotional intimacy,
- declared trust,
- romance.

**At most one subtle, unforced romance-temperature beat** (Aarav's reflexive half-step between Aurelia and the doorway during their Scene 2 disagreement) — physical, involuntary, unremarked, immediately subordinated to the argument. Do not name it, do not expand it, do not make it mutual in the moment.

## Characterization

### Aurelia
- Direct, physically confident, action-oriented, impatient with unnecessary restraint, independently motivated.
- Her contribution to the SERPENTFIRE discovery is practical (ordinary physical handling of the fragment as a one-time situational task) plus a separate, non-informational personal sensation — never a magical detection mechanism.
- Her disagreement with Aarav in Scene 2 is her own judgment call (risk tolerance), not a repeat of Chapter 11's "notice → validated" function.
- Do not reduce her to a device that makes SERPENTFIRE legible.

### Aarav
- Analytical, observant, verification-first, institutionally aware, measured under uncertainty.
- Encounters something his method cannot classify (the Unknown Student's composed, ambiguous departure) — this must remain unresolved, not explained away by his own analysis.
- His decision to report the sighting must read as a weighed, character-consistent choice (verification-first, procedurally honest), not an automatic plot requirement.
- His Naga sensitivity contributes one bounded, physical, inconclusive data point — never a diagnosis.
- His Chapter 8 voice-mismatch knowledge remains entirely private and unreferenced.

## Magic — Hard Locks

- Aurelia's fire affinity does **not** detect hidden information and does **not** make SERPENTFIRE legible. Legibility comes from ordinary physical handling (turning the fragment toward light, clearing loose char) — a mundane action.
- Aurelia's own sensation (unease, a flicker of warmth not her own) is strictly non-informational: it identifies nothing, answers nothing, and cannot be used by her or the reader to infer the word's meaning.
- Aarav's Naga sensitivity near the Unknown Student registers only an unclassifiable impression — no comparison to wards, no magical-signature language, no diagnostic certainty. It must not imply the student is magically unusual, warded, or suppressing anything.
- No new magical instrument, numerical measurement, formal experiment, or repeatable detection procedure may be introduced.
- No combined Phoenix–Naga ability. No destiny-confirming magic.
- Governing principle: **Aurelia's affinity can produce a question, not an answer.**

## Mystery

- **SERPENTFIRE** is introduced as a surviving word in damaged archival material — a new question, not a partial answer. No meaning, origin, philosophical significance, or connection to Elyra/Varun/First Ember/the Veil may be explained.
- The **Unknown Student** remains fully unresolved: no name, species, motive, affiliation, guilt, innocence, or confirmed connection to SERPENTFIRE, the Ch4 breach, the angular mark, the Echo, or any protected lore.
- No causal connection may be asserted between the Chapter 11 Training Grounds irregularity and either SERPENTFIRE or the Unknown Student, beyond the existing thematic/categorical link already locked in the Ch11 handoff.

## Institutional

- **Voss** remains a competent, credible gatekeeper: he recognizes/reacts to SERPENTFIRE without explaining it, refuses to answer directly, and makes a brief, procedurally mundane movement (securing the fragment elsewhere in the same room/area) without abandoning supervision. He must not fully leave the protagonists unsupervised, and must not reveal protected history.
- **Seraphine's** involvement is procedural and concise — she authorizes or triggers the access-suspension review; her on-page presence, if used at all, is a single brief, functional line, not a scene. She must not become an exposition source.
- The **Archive-access suspension** is framed explicitly as a security/procedural review of how an unauthorized presence occurred during supervision — **not** punishment or accusation of Aurelia and Aarav. It is proportionate, credible, and removes a meaningful investigative resource.

## Protected Lore — Explicit Lock

The following must not be revealed, confirmed, or strongly implied by dialogue, archival terminology, Unknown Student behavior, environmental description, or character inference:

- Outer Dark's true nature
- The Seeker
- The Harbinger
- The First Ember's purpose
- Echo identity, origin, motive, or intelligence
- Elyra
- Varun
- The Lost Path
- True Veil origin
- True Veil weakness
- True breach theory
- Serpentfire's true philosophical/historical meaning

## Existing Continuity Locks

- The angular mark remains dormant; no new causality assigned to it.
- The Chapter 3 wind remains unrelated; unreferenced.
- The Chapter 11 Training Grounds anomaly remains unexplained and is not restaged or resolved on-page.
- The Chapter 10 historical precedent (sealed supplement, ref. 14-C) remains background pressure only — not resolved, not expanded, not referenced beyond its existing locked status.
- Aarav's Chapter 8 knowledge of the Dev-like voice's behavioral mismatch remains private and unshared.

## Superseded Design Elements — Do Not Use

The following formulations from earlier Chapter 12 drafts are explicitly superseded by Phase 3.1 corrections and must not appear in Phase 4 drafting:

- Aurelia's fire/proximity causing the archival text to become legible (superseded by: ordinary physical handling causes legibility).
- Voss fully leaving the room/wing after his reaction (superseded by: a brief, same-area procedural movement without abandoning supervision).
- The Unknown Student's presence being causally dependent on Voss's absence (superseded by: the student is independently present; the gap affects only whether Aarav happens to notice).
- A standing Academy policy that Phoenix-affiliated students handle fire-damaged material (superseded by: a one-time, situational task assignment).
- Aarav's Naga sensitivity being compared to "inactive wards" (superseded by: an unclassifiable, non-diagnostic impression with no named comparison).
