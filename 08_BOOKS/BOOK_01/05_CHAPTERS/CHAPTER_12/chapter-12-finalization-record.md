## Chapter Function

Chapter 12 lets a routine, supervised Archive session slip out of Aurelia and Aarav's control. An incidental discovery of the word SERPENTFIRE and an independently-present Unknown Student combine, through institutional response rather than protagonist choice, to cost them their primary investigative resource.

Locked progression:

**Authorized supervised Archive work → mundane discovery of SERPENTFIRE → Voss's controlled reaction → an independently-present Unknown Student, noticed rather than caused → genuine disagreement over pursuit vs. restraint → Aarav's weighed decision to report → institutional escalation → supervised Archive access suspended pending review.**

The chapter does not explain SERPENTFIRE or resolve the Unknown Student. It advances the mystery from "one confirmed present-day irregularity" (Chapter 11) to "two new, unresolved threads, discovered under conditions the protagonists did not control" — and it removes, rather than grants, an investigative capability.

## Final Narrative State

### Mystery
The primary mystery remains unresolved. Chapter 12 adds two new, genuinely open threads: the surviving word SERPENTFIRE (discovered in damaged archival material, its meaning withheld even from the protagonists) and the Unknown Student (independently present near the restricted Archive section, fully unresolved in identity, motive, species, and affiliation). No connection is asserted between these two threads, or between either of them and the Chapter 4 breach, the Chapter 10 historical precedent, or the Chapter 11 Training Grounds irregularity, beyond the thematic/categorical.

### Institutional State
Supervised Archive access — active since Chapter 9 and the chapter's primary investigative resource — is suspended pending a security/procedural review triggered by the unauthorized presence near restricted material. This is framed explicitly as institutional process, not punishment or accusation of Aurelia or Aarav. The duration of the suspension is unresolved ("I don't know yet. That's rather the point.").

### Character State
**Aarav** remains analytical, verification-first, and institutionally aware; his decision to report the sighting is shown as a weighed, character-consistent choice, not an automatic plot requirement. **Aurelia** remains direct, physically confident, and impatient with restraint; her disagreement with Aarav (pursuit vs. restraint) is her own judgment call, not a repetition of Chapter 11's validation structure. **Voss** remains a competent, credible institutional gatekeeper who recognizes SERPENTFIRE's significance without explaining it. **Seraphine** appears only by reference, through a single compressed line conveyed by Voss, and remains procedural rather than expository.

### Relationship State
Aurelia and Aarav remain **Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation**, at the **beginning edge of Stage 3 — Respect**. Chapter 12 tests this Respect through genuine disagreement rather than confirming it through validation: Aurelia does not blame Aarav for reporting despite disagreeing with the choice, and Aarav registers her restraint; Aurelia, in turn, registers that his honesty cost him something. Both beats are mutual, small, and never named. No advancement to Trust or Friendship. No romance beat exists in the finalized manuscript — the optional proximity beat specified in planning was deliberately not added.

### Information Lock
Aarav's Chapter 8 knowledge of the specific behavioral mismatch in the Dev-like voice is never referenced in Chapter 12, directly or by inference. Aurelia does not know it.

### Protected-Lore Lock
Chapter 12 does not reveal or establish: the true nature of the Outer Dark, the Seeker, the Harbinger, the First Ember's purpose, Elyra, Varun, the Lost Path, the true Veil origin or weakness, breach/intrusion theory, the hidden chamber, or any Echo identity, origin, motive, or intelligence. Serpentfire's true meaning is not revealed — only the surviving word and Voss's unexplained reaction to it.

### Magic Lock
SERPENTFIRE's legibility is caused entirely by ordinary physical handling (turning the fragment, clearing char), not by Aurelia's affinity. Her separate sensation is non-informational. Aarav's Naga sensitivity registers only an unclassifiable impression, with no comparison to wards, magical signatures, or species identity. No new instrument, measurement, formal experiment, or repeatable magical system was introduced. No combined Phoenix–Naga ability occurs.

### Angular Mark and Chapter 3 Wind
