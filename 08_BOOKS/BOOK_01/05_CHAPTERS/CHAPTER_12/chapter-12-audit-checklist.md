## Relationship Ledger

**Starting state:** Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation, beginning edge of Stage 3 — Respect (inherited from Ch11).

**Intended movement:** Respect tested by genuine disagreement, held through mutual restraint — not advanced.

**Allowed:** practical reliance under pressure; real disagreement (chase vs. restraint); mutual, unlabeled behavioral recognition; one small, unforced physical-awareness beat (Aarav's half-step in Scene 2).

**Forbidden:** confession, explicit attraction, emotional intimacy, friendship declaration, sudden trust, any romance-stage advancement, naming the relationship shift in dialogue or interiority.

## Consequence Ledger

**Before Chapter 12:** Supervised Archive access exists and is actively used as an investigative resource.

**Chapter 12:** An unauthorized presence is observed near the restricted section during a supervised session; Aarav reports it after weighing the cost.

**End state:** Supervised Archive access is suspended pending security/procedural review — framed as institutional process, not punishment.

**Chapter 13 inheritance:** The protagonists cannot continue their Archive investigation through the same supervised arrangement. The exact Chapter 13 resolution is not decided here and must not be pre-invented in Chapter 12.

## Reader-Condition Test

- **Supporting cast:** Voss and Seraphine both make plot-altering decisions — genuine agency, not decoration.
- **Pressure:** Archive access, the investigation's core resource, is materially jeopardized by chapter's end — a real, carried-forward consequence.
- **Story widening:** The chapter's pivotal decisions belong to institutional authorities, not solely to Aurelia and Aarav — the story widens beyond the two-hander.
- **Structural variety:** The engine (discovery under institutional constraint → consequence) does not repeat Chapters 5–11's investigation-and-discussion shape.
- **Mystery progression:** SERPENTFIRE and the Unknown Student both create new questions rather than resolving existing ones.
- **Relationship:** Respect develops behaviorally, through disagreement and restraint, without romance escalation.
