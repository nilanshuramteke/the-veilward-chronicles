## 1. Immediate Starting Situation

At the end of Chapter 12:

- During a supervised, authorized Archive cross-referencing task, ordinary physical handling of a fire-damaged inventory sheet exposed one surviving word: **SERPENTFIRE**. Neither Aurelia nor Aarav knows what it means. Voss reacted with controlled recognition, confiscated the material, and refused to explain.
- While Voss was briefly occupied securing the fragment (still within the same room), Aarav noticed a student, already independently present, near the restricted archway — composed, deliberate, and gone before he could get a clear look. Aurelia did not see them.
- Aarav and Aurelia disagreed over whether to pursue the figure; Aarav chose restraint and, after weighing the cost, reported the sighting to Voss.
- Voss escalated the incident as a security/procedural matter. Supervised Archive access — active since Chapter 9 and their primary investigative resource — is now suspended pending review. Its duration is unknown.
- No cause, identity, mechanism, or motive was established for either SERPENTFIRE or the Unknown Student. No connection to the Chapter 4 breach, the Chapter 10 historical precedent, or the Chapter 11 Training Grounds irregularity was asserted beyond the thematic/categorical.

Chapter 13 begins from this state. It must not restage the Chapter 12 Archive session or require Aarav's presence to re-litigate it.

The investigation has gained two new, unresolved threads and lost its primary institutional resource in the same chapter. The obstacle has shifted from "how do we get more answers" to "how do we investigate at all without the Archive." Chapter 13 inherits pressure, not information.
