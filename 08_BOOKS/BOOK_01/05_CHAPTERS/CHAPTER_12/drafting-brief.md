# Chapter 12 — Drafting Brief

> The primary Phase 4 writer-facing document. Read alongside `canon-constraints.md` and `scene-breakdown.md`. This brief translates approved architecture into execution instructions; it introduces no new story decisions.

## POV Discipline

Aarav, close third person, past tense, throughout.

Full Aarav interiority is expected and necessary — his classification-failure regarding the Unknown Student, and his internal weighing before deciding to report, are both required interiority beats.

Everything about Aurelia must be inferred through her speech, physical action, and behavior, filtered through Aarav's interpretation. No Aurelia interiority.

## Narrative Texture

The chapter should feel:
- grounded and physical,
- institutional (procedure, supervision, restricted boundaries),
- quietly tense rather than ominous,
- curious rather than portentous,
- progressively constrained — control narrows scene by scene.

Avoid melodrama. Voss's concern and Seraphine's response should read as competent institutional behavior, not genre-mystery theatrics.

## The Archive

Make it feel physically real through damaged material, handling procedure, restricted boundaries, and Voss's supervisory rhythm — not through description blocks. Do not over-describe the space; texture should arrive through action.

## SERPENTFIRE

Reveal only the surviving word. Legibility comes from Aarav reading it after Aurelia's ordinary physical handling of the fragment (turning it, clearing char) — not from her magic. Voss's reaction (a controlled flicker of recognition, refusal to explain, confiscation) carries all the weight; no character explains what the word means. Do not let Aarav's analytical narration speculate toward an answer.

## Aurelia's Sensation

If included, keep it brief and clearly non-informational — a private, personal reaction (unease, a flicker of warmth not her own) that identifies nothing and cannot be used to infer meaning. Do not let it read as a magical detection beat.

## The Unknown Student

Make them concrete, not a silhouette: one or two specific, deliberate observable behaviors (the composed self-adjusting gesture; the chosen route avoiding Voss). Give the reader enough to be interested, not enough to conclude guilt, identity, or nature. Aarav's Naga-sensitivity impression must stay unclassifiable — avoid any phrasing that reads as a magical diagnosis or a comparison to a known phenomenon (e.g., do not compare it to wards).

## Aurelia

Her contribution is practical and character-driven: the situational handling task, her own disagreement in Scene 2 (risk tolerance, not instinct-vs-analysis), and her restraint in Scene 3 (not blaming Aarav for a decision she opposed). Do not repeat Chapter 11's "notice → validated" structure in any form, including in reverse.

## Aarav

His decision to report must be visible as a weighed choice — a brief interior beat in Scene 2 establishing that silence would cost more than disclosure, paying off in Scene 3's report. He should not be omniscient: his sensory impression of the student stays inconclusive, and his classification failure should remain unresolved through the chapter's end. His Chapter 8 knowledge stays entirely unreferenced — do not let his narration draw any comparison between the Dev-voice mismatch and the Unknown Student.

## Relationship

Keep rivalry and friction fully intact. Allow the one permitted small, unforced proximity beat (Scene 2) and the mutual behavioral-recognition beat (Scene 3) — both unnamed, undiscussed, and subordinate to the scene's actual action. Do not let either character articulate what changed.

## Institutional Consequence

The Archive-access suspension should read as procedural, proportionate, and credible — a security-protocol review of how an unauthorized presence occurred during supervision, not a disciplinary action against Aurelia and Aarav. Compress Seraphine's presence to, at most, a single brief functional line; prefer having Voss convey that the review is already in motion under her authority.

## Scene 3 Pacing

Give this scene proportionally more room than Scenes 1–2. Let the report, Voss's reaction, the consequence, and the relationship beat each land with a breath of space rather than compressing them into rapid dialogue exchanges.

## Prose Watchlist

Carried forward from prior chapter audits and specific to this chapter's risks:
- repeated "the way [subject][verb]" constructions,
- mirrored negation ("That's not X, that's Y"),
- trailing "which..." clauses,
- abstract emotional labels not physically grounded,
- clustering of "already,"
- symmetrical dialogue,
- over-explanation of SERPENTFIRE or the student,
- excessive fire metaphors (given Aurelia's sensation is intentionally minor),
- artificial or portentous mystery language,
- villain-coding the Unknown Student (avoid shadow/menace-cue language for what must remain ambiguous),
- Aarav's narration drifting toward certainty/omniscience about the student,
- magical-detection language for either Aurelia's sensation or Aarav's sensitivity,
- excessive institutional exposition (show Voss's and Seraphine's competence through decision/action, not explanation).
