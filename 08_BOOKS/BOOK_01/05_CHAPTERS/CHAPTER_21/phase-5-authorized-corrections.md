Replaced "For a moment neither of them said anything, and she found that she was on her knees with her forehead against the cold wall, and that his hand had closed on her shoulder" with a specific physical beat: "She was on her knees with her forehead against the cold wall, breathing in ragged pulls, and his hand closed on her shoulder, shaking as hard as hers was."
