### 3. Locked Mystery State

**Known entering (reader and leads):** cold and silence; imitated voices with a subtle error; wards register nothing; the marks; the wall; Fourteen-C; the fourth stone anomaly; an open question whether the phenomenon responds to them.

**Established in Chapter 21 as observation (character-level):**
1. The cold reached Aarav and altered the environment: seep froze, masonry split, a bank failed. It can physically harm a person.
2. **There was no visible sign that the phenomenon reacted to Aurelia or Aarav as people.** No voice, no imitation, no pressure toward either of them.
3. Frost and rime lay in a **line along the channel**; slabs were split along it; rime was heavier on one face; warmth returned to the stone in a sequence along one side.
4. Three independent accounts overlap in part; each holds a singular detail.
5. The Academy's ward check registers nothing.

**Permitted, hedged inference (a character may say or think it, tentatively, once at most):** something may have moved along or through old ground.

**Unknown (say nothing that resolves it; the text may state that it is unknown):** whether it noticed them; whether it ignored them; whether it targets anything; why it moved; what it wanted; whether the apparent direction is intention; how it entered; whether the drain is a route.

#### SCENE 1 — ORDINARY WARMTH (500–700)

**Objective:** Give the reader breath and a place to love before danger; plant every prop; answer Mira's grievance by behavior.

**Entry state:** late light on the far practice apron above the works; Aurelia running evaluation drills alone; Mira arrives with a thin excuse; the near pitches are full of second-years; the cut lies roped below; the gate proctor is a long way up the field.

**Beats (proportions, not quotas):**
1. **Open on an action** (~15%): a held flame counted by someone else. No weather, no atmosphere opener.
2. **Mira's presence and the plants** (~30%): she times with her sheet and pencil; a first-year lantern or coil anecdote lands the two conventions as ordinary Academy memory, in a line or two, without explanation.
3. **Aurelia stops managing Mira** (~25%): a bounded truth that is hers: something is wrong on the grounds; she does not know what; she will not lie to her again; some of it is not hers to tell. Mira takes it with relief and refuses to be grateful. Nothing about Voss's records, the marks, the wall, the pages or Aarav's private material.
4. **Aarav arrives openly** (~20%): no interval; Mira notices it in one beat; he crouches at the roped margin to look at the seep out of habit; chalk appears (a small tally joke or a mark on the wall); one friendly, specific remark about her flame that is not the old jab.
5. **Closing detail** (~10%): light going; her breath faintly visible when it should not be, noticed and dismissed.

**Information:** the layout of the cut; the propped slab; the two conventions; Mira's role. Nothing about the drain's meaning.
**Dialogue intent:** light, quick, character-specific; Mira dry; Aurelia direct; Aarav qualifying. The "gripping" motif returns with reversed valence (she invites his read).
**Physical staging:** stationary drills; one crouch; no walk. The wall behind them; the fence and lantern to one side; the cut below.
**Magic/costs:** ordinary controlled flame, bead-sized, one small widening corrected. No cost beyond a mild slip.
**Exit state:** last drill under way; Aarav near the works side; a faint cold breath. No warning that would make the reader feel manipulated.
**Forbidden drift:** exposition about the works or the Echo; naming the fourth stone; a Voss reference; an investigation impulse; any tension not rooted in evaluation nerves or Mira's question.

#### SCENE 2 — THE COLD (450–650)

**Objective:** The danger begins with the cold and no voice; the edge fails; Aarav falls.

**Entry state:** the closing detail of S1; all three close to the wall and lip; light low.

**Beats:**
1. **The cold arrives** (~25%): she feels it before she can name it (Ch8). Her flame stutters and fails to rise; do **not** attribute cause. Breath fogs. Hands numb.
2. **The silence** (~20%): sound thins and goes. Mira's mouth moves and nothing arrives. She waits for a voice and none comes; the absence is felt, not explained.
3. **The cold pools** (~10%): it moves out of the cut low and downhill like water; frost creeps on the cord and stakes; the lantern at the low end dims first (Mira's singular detail; do not point at it).
4. **The edge** (~30%): Aarav is nearest the lip and turns toward them; the bank goes; his reflex puts him between her and the failing ground; she is pulled back; he is not; her grip on his sleeve or wrist holds a moment and fails on cold and wet.
5. **The fall** (~15%): the split slab and spoil slide across the opening behind him; the hole narrows; cold pours from the gap.

**Information:** the cold can break ground and drop a person. Nothing about what it is. No voice occurs.
**Dialogue intent:** **none.** The scene is soundless. No lines, no mouthed words rendered as speech.
**Physical staging:** apron to the lip in a few steps; Aurelia on her knees at the lip; Mira beside her.
**Magic/costs:** none used; her instinct for the cold; her flame will not answer for a moment.
**Exit state:** Aurelia at the lip holding nothing; the hole below is a dark gap breathing cold; silence total.
**Forbidden drift:** any voice or mimicry; a monologue; slow-motion melodrama; any statement that the cold "chose" him; a symbol or mark; the fourth stone.

#### SCENE 3 — THE DECISION (600–850)

**Objective:** Do everything the rule asks, then break it, on record.

**Entry state:** silence total; Aurelia shaking and thinking fast.

**Beats (in order of what she does):**
1. **Check the ground before moving** (~10%): she pulls Mira back from the margin; Mira has already stopped. The rule is known and sound.
2. **The signal** (~15%): Aurelia gestures; Mira runs to the fence post and swings the lantern in the taught pattern; it works because it needs no sound. Far up the field the gate proctor turns toward it and starts down.
3. **The line** (~10%): Mira fetches the coil from the gear box; Aurelia shows her three tugs by gesture.
4. **The gap and the stone** (~25%): she cannot see him; voice fails; she lays her palm on the slab out of stone-reading habit and feels knuckles through it. She answers. One rap yes, two no, counts for anything more. It is procedural, improvised by Aarav, imperfect, and once wrong. **No one explains why stone carries.**
5. **The question the rule exists for** (~15%): can she wait? His raps slow; frost closes the gap; the proctor is far; the groundsmen further.
6. **The note** (~15%): Aurelia writes short and plain on the back of Mira's timing sheet: where he is, that he is alive, that she has gone in on purpose, not to pull spoil or slab, to get shoring. Mira reads it and holds it for the first adult.
7. **The breach** (~10%): she names the breach to herself in the terms of the rule, ties on, and goes. Not calm; a refusal to let waiting be the choice.

**Information:** the rule's reasons; the clock; the stone channel; the note as record.
**Dialogue intent:** **none spoken.** Communication is gesture, raps, and the note. The note's content is fixed above and must be plain and factual.
**Physical staging:** lip → fence post → gear box → slab → lip; the line tied at the fence post's base; Mira tending; the spoil slope down.
**Magic/costs:** a small flame for light only, kept low; hands numbing.
**Exit state:** Aurelia at the top of the slope with the line on her waist and a small flame; Mira's hands on the line; she goes down.
**Forbidden drift:** debate with Mira; Aurelia justifying herself aloud; a slogan about trust; recklessness (every safe step must be shown first); Voss.

#### SCENE 4 — THE PROP (1,300–1,800)

**Objective:** The rescue. Cooperation between two people who cannot verify each other; fire useful, imperfect and costly; Aarav's choices; sound returns.

**Entry state:** descent into thickening cold; small flame; wet moving spoil; the line taking weight.

**Beats (progression, not choreography):**
1. **Descent** (~15%): slow, tested; the line takes weight twice; rime is heavier on one face of the channel.
2. **Reaching him** (~15%): the channel is narrow and low; she sees a grey, alert face and hands by flame. Air-borne speech still fails. He communicates by gesture and **chalk on the stone**: which stone is the prop, which slab is split, what will move. His uncertainty is physical (a flat tilted hand, a chalked question mark, a partial nod).
3. **The wrong plan** (~10%): her instinct is to haul with the line; he refuses by gesture and chalk. She wants to argue and does not. First act of trust.
4. **The method** (~15%): the fused ice, not the rubble, holds his leg. Slow heat at chosen points frees it; fast heat splits stone. He marks the points and sets the pace by hand; she supplies the flame.
5. **Sound returns** (~10%): all at once, as in Ch4. She hears her own breath, drip, the line creak, then his voice, hoarse and hedged. One beat of doubt whether it is him; resolved because it is not smooth. He begins to report aloud what he cannot be sure of. **No voice other than his and hers.**
6. **The slip** (~10%): the prop groans; fear spikes; her flame widens; flash steam and hot stone spall burn her right forearm and hand; the stone beside the plug cracks; grit falls; the prop shifts. He tells her to stop. She stops and shuts the flame down with effort.
7. **Aarav's choice** (~7%): he could call it off and wait for shoring. He tells her to go on at a slower rate, giving his reason in one plain sentence about the arithmetic, not about her.
8. **Aurelia's choice** (~8%): she acts on his "I think the prop holds," putting her hands under it on his count.
