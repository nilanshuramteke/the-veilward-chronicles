## 2. Fixed Design Decisions (Phase 2 resolves these; Phase 3 must not reopen them)

### 2.1 Geography (plausible, minimal, new only where needed)
- The Training Grounds slope down from the gate (high end, proctor post, near pitches) to the low stretch by the far fence. A worn apron of flat ground with a low retaining wall lies along the fence about thirty paces above the works. It is the only free practice ground because the near pitches are full of second-years (as in Ch20). A signal lantern hangs on the fence post at each pitch and a gear box holds practice coils.
- The **cut** is a regrading trench across the low stretch, well away from the eight-stone line's fourth seat (stated at most as "the line of stones is further along"; the fourth stone is not named, seen, or discussed). It is roped with stakes and cord. Timber shoring is partial: a frame at one end, bare at the other. Spoil banks stand along its upper lip. Ground is wet and always has been.
- At the bare end the cut has opened the **roof of an old stone-lined drain** (dressed stone channel, flat slab roof, older than the current line). Crew had propped one slab and left. This is ordinary drainage, found by ordinary work. It runs under the ground on a line the characters can see only as far as the opened section. Where it goes is not shown and not asked.
- **Distances.** Mira and Aurelia at the wall; the cut ~30 paces down; the gate proctor at the top of the field, out of hearing in any case and a hard run away. The rope line sits inside the ground that then fails: the bank crumbles **beyond** the marked margin. Aarav is outside the cordon when he falls. He commits no breach.

### 2.2 Mechanism of the fall and the trap
- The cold arrives low and fast from the cut, pooling downhill as Ch8 established (cold "close to the ground"). Seep water in the wet bank and the opened drain freezes. Frost expanding in the joints splits the propped roof slab and undercuts the spoil bank's edge.
- **Aarav is nearest the lip** (he has come down toward them from the practicum path along the works side). As the edge goes, he moves to put himself between Aurelia and the failing edge and pulls her back. The bank takes his footing. She catches his sleeve or wrist for a moment; cold and wet defeat her grip.
- He drops into the opened section of the drain. The split slab and spoil slide across the opening behind him, so the surface hole is **narrowed to a gap a person can look through but not climb through**. He is pinned below the knee by rubble that freezing seep has fused into the channel floor. The fallen slab leans on the next intact slab and is acting as a **prop**. Freeing his leg by force or haste risks moving it.
- His pocket is a few paces of channel, water and ice, no light. Injury is bounded: badly bruised or wrenched lower leg, cold exposure. He can move his upper body, his hands and his voice once sound returns.

### 2.3 Clock and why procedure cannot solve it
- Signal → gate proctor → runner to the works lodge for groundsmen and shoring → ward-team clearance is the sound procedure. It is measured in tens of minutes at least. The cold, wet and enclosure give him fewer than that, and the margin visibly shrinks (his signals slow; frost closes the gap). No exact minutes are stated.
- Ward slates read clear throughout (Ch4 rule; magic lock §7). There is no alarm and no ward-based confirmation available to anyone.
- **Why not "just haul":** the prop. Only he can tell where it holds.

### 2.4 Taught emergency practice (minimum new canon, established in S1 as prior training)
- **Signal lantern:** every pitch has one. A fixed swing pattern means "emergency at this pitch; send help." First-years drill it. Mira has a comic first-year memory of setting it off by accident.
- **Line rule:** enclosed or unstable ground is never entered untethered; someone stays on the surface tending the line. First-years drill this with practice coils from the gear box.
- **Works rule:** a roped works site is not entered by students. It applies to the cut, not to where the bank fails.

### 2.5 The channel (what silence blocks, what it does not)
- The cold's **silence** kills air-carried sound: voices, shouts, taps in air. Stone-borne contact is not blocked: a hand flat on a slab feels a knuckle rap on the other side. Discovered under pressure (Aurelia lays a palm on the slab out of instinct, the way she reads stone; Aarav raps to test). No prior agreement.
- The code is improvised by Aarav (one rap yes, two no or stop, a count for direction). It is simple, unpoetic, and gets wrong once.
- When the cold thins, sound returns, all at once, as in Ch4 ("the air let go"). Voice through the gap is hoarse and ordinary. Aurelia has one flicker of doubt about whether it is really him (the Ch18 fear, internal only). The voice is ordinary, hedged and uncertain, so the doubt is resolved by how it sounds. **No Echo voice occurs anywhere in the chapter.**

### 2.6 Fire and its cost
- Established uses only: a small steady flame (Ch1 "bead the size of a coin"), light, judged heat, warming a surface. No True Form (confined structure, unstable). No new fire ability.
- Method: a small flame held against the ice plug at a slow rate, on his instruction, at chosen points. Rate and location matter because fast heat on frozen wet stone splits it.
- **The slip.** Under fear her flame widens without her deciding it (the Ch1 pattern where the fire answers the thought and not the intention). It cracks the stone beside the plug with a sound, drops grit and hot flakes, burns her forearm and palm (second-degree-level, bounded), and shakes the prop. He stops her. She shuts it down. The control returns imperfectly.
- **Costs that persist:** the burn, numb clumsy hands, exhaustion that leaves her unable to raise more than a spark afterward. These are visible in S5–S6.
- Her fire-control arc is not resolved. The chapter ends with her flame still less reliable than she wants.

### 2.7 Epistemic discipline (author instruction; binding)
The characters and text may state only:
- **Observation:** cold arrived before anything else and with no voice; silence; frost formed in the cut and drain; slabs split in a line along the channel; rime is heavier on one face of the channel; the cold thinned in a sequence along the channel; sound returned; no voice, mimicry or sign of response occurred toward either of them.
- **Inference (hedged, characters own it):** something moved along old ground.
- **Unknown (stated as unknown):** why it moved there, whether it was intentional, what it wanted, whether it noticed them.

The text must not say the Echo "passed through" them, "did not notice" them, was "heading" anywhere or was "searching" (the last is a reader-level idea, not a character one). The right phrasing is negative and narrow: *there was no sign that it answered to either of them.* Aurelia may note that this differs from the two voice events; she does not name it as a mode.

### 2.8 Discipline (built from the Academy framework)
- Aurelia: **formal disciplinary review opened, pending.** Basis: entering an unshored cut in a works area without shoring or clearance; using fire in an unstable structure. Interim: restricted privileges (no Training Grounds outside timetabled sessions, no unsupervised fire practice off the sanctioned pitch). Her **pattern** (Ch8 cordon at dusk, Ch19 unreported marks) is what makes a review credible; intent and outcome mitigate. The evaluation date is not changed by the notice; whether she sits it is for the review. **Unresolved.**
- Aarav: no breach. He volunteers into the record that the decision to proceed inside the cut was made on his instruction. The record now carries both names on that entry. The review remains hers.

### 2.9 Voss
Offstage throughout. After the incident, Aurelia asks the Warden's office to send word to him that evening and says she and Aarav will bring it themselves. Stated as fact; no debate, no scene. His Ch20 state is unchanged (holds Fourteen-C; has not interpreted it; does not know Aarav's Ch18 interpretation).

## 4. Scene Plan

### Scene 1 — The Far Apron

**POV:** Aurelia
**Location:** The flat practice apron along the fence at the low end of the Training Grounds, above the works.
**Approximate time:** Late light, before lanterns; the second evening after Ch20.
**Characters present:** Aurelia, Mira, then Aarav. Distant: a gate proctor at the top of the field; a few second-years on the near pitches.
**Scene purpose:** Breathing room and warmth. Establish location, the cut, the taught signal and line, the friendship's current baseline, Mira's return, and Aurelia's decision to stop managing Mira. Plant the "gripping" motif and Aarav's habit of reading ground. Show that the two leads no longer arrange their movements to avoid being seen together.

**Opening state:** Aurelia is running evaluation forms alone at the last usable ground; Mira has turned up with a reason that is not quite a reason. The regrading works lie below, roped and quiet. The Ch20 handover has left Aurelia lighter, and she is aware of owing Mira something.
**Immediate objective:** Get through the drills; and say something true to Mira that is hers to say.
**External action:** Timed flame drills (hold a bead, widen, return) with Mira spotting and counting as a favor. Mira's dry commentary; a first-year joke about the signal lantern and the practice coil, which places both on the page. Aurelia gives Mira a bounded honest statement: something is wrong on the grounds, she does not know what, she will not lie to her again, and there are parts that are not hers to tell. Mira takes it with visible relief and refuses to be grateful. Aarav arrives from the practicum path openly, not at an interval, and is noticed by Mira for it. He crouches at the lip of the roped margin to look at the seep, out of habit; Aurelia teases him about it. He watches a drill and says one small thing about her flame. It is friendly, and not the old jab.
**Internal movement:** Aurelia registers how easy it is, and that easy is new. She is briefly afraid of noticing it.
**Relationship movement:** Rival jab and dry humor now carry affection rather than defence. They stand close without arranging distance.
**Mystery movement:** None. Reader learns only the cut's layout, the exposed drain edge with a propped slab, and the taught signals. Do not linger on the drain. Do not mention the fourth stone or the marks.
**Supporting-character function:** Mira is here because it is the evaluation window, she has been watching Aurelia's practice, and she wants an answer. She supplies humor, the friendship's missing middle, and the first-year lantern and line memory that Scene 3 will use.
**Magic usage:** Aurelia's ordinary controlled flame, small and steady, with the familiar tendency to widen when her attention wanders. No cost beyond a mild slip corrected.
**Conflict/escalation:** Small: Mira's question, Aurelia's evaluation nerves, the "gripping" habit. The scene should not be tension-free, only light.
**Decision:** Aurelia chooses to tell Mira the truth she can stand behind. Aarav chooses to arrive openly.
**Consequence:** Mira is no longer being managed. The Ch18 grievance is answered by behavior.
**Exit state:** Light going. A last drill under way. Aarav has stepped toward the wall from the works side. No warning yet, except one detail Aurelia notices and dismisses: her breath is faintly visible when it should not be.

### Scene 2 — Cold Before Anything

**POV:** Aurelia
**Location:** The apron and the roped margin above the cut.
**Approximate time:** Minutes later, the same evening.
**Characters present:** Aurelia, Aarav, Mira.
**Scene purpose:** The danger begins. Cold with no voice, then the collapse. This is the first physical harm from the Echo on the page.

**Opening state:** Ordinary evening interrupted by a sensation Aurelia has learned to trust.
**Immediate objective:** Understand what she is feeling, then get people clear.
**External action:** She feels the cold before she can name it (Ch8: she felt it first). Her flame stutters and does not answer. Breath fogs in a way it should not. Sound thins and goes: Mira's mouth moves with no sound. There is no voice. She waits for a voice, and it does not come. Cold pools out of the cut and downhill, low, like water. Aarav, at the lip, turns toward them. The edge goes. He reaches for her by reflex. She is pulled back a step, and he is not. Her hand closes on his sleeve or wrist and holds for a moment; the cold-numbed grip fails. He drops into the drain. Slab and spoil slide behind him. She is on her knees at the lip holding nothing.
**Internal movement:** Recognition of the cold; the absence of the voice she braced for; then the physical fact of his weight leaving her hand. The Ch8 rhyme reversed: she had pulled him back once; this time he pulled her.
**Relationship movement:** He chose her safety by reflex, at cost. Unspoken and undiscussed. She will not think of it as a debt until later.
**Mystery movement:** The cold can physically break ground and drop a person. No voice occurs. Do not say what this means. Keep the reader's attention on the loss, not on the Echo.
**Supporting-character function:** Mira is a witness to the cold arriving, the lack of sound, and the fall. She reacts as an ordinary student would: frozen for one beat, then in motion. She sees the fixtures nearest the cut dim first and no more (her singular detail, unremarked in the scene).
**Magic usage:** Aurelia's instinct for cold. Her flame fails to answer for a moment (cold, fear).
**Conflict/escalation:** Silence prevents any call. Nothing the leads know how to do addresses it.
**Decision:** None conscious. Two reflexes, his and hers.
**Consequence:** Aarav is trapped in the drain. The silence is total and the cold is still deepening.
**Exit state:** Aurelia at the lip. Mira beside her, mouthing something no one hears. The hole below is a dark gap with cold coming out of it.

### Scene 3 — What the Rule Is For

**POV:** Aurelia
**Location:** The lip above the cut; the fence post with the signal lantern; the gear box; the slab surface.
**Approximate time:** The next several minutes.
**Characters present:** Aurelia, Mira. Aarav below, in contact. The gate proctor seen far off, later.
**Scene purpose:** Show the rule and why it is right. Show her doing everything that procedure asks. Show the calculation change. Show her decision and the way she makes it.

**Opening state:** The silence is total. Aurelia is shaking and thinking fast.
**Immediate objective:** Get help and get to him without killing them both.
**External action, in order of what she does (all of it correct first):**
1. She checks the ground before she moves and pulls Mira back from the margin. Mira, who knows the rule, has already stopped. The rule is known and sound.
2. Mira runs to the fence post and swings the lantern in the taught pattern. It works because it does not need sound. She returns with a coil from the gear box. Far up the field the gate proctor turns toward the light and starts down.
3. Aurelia tries the gap by eye and reach. She cannot see him. She tries voice. Nothing. She lays her palm on the slab out of stone-reading instinct and feels a knuckle rap through it. She answers. The channel exists.
4. Contact establishes: he is alive, pinned, in cold water; one rap yes, two no. He indicates the prop and the danger of pulling. She raps a question; he answers; the code is clumsy and wrong once.
5. She asks the question the rule exists for: can she wait? His raps answer. They slow. He does not say the number of minutes. Frost is closing the gap; she watches it creep.
6. She looks up the field. The proctor is a long way off. The groundsmen are further. The cold is not going to wait for shoring.
**Internal movement:** Fear that is physical, not flavored. She runs the arithmetic she ran in Ch17 and finds that it does not come out. She notices she is already deciding. The decision is not calm; it is a refusal to let waiting be the choice.
**Relationship movement:** She asks what he thinks before she acts. He answers with what he knows and what he does not. Neither pretends. Their practiced Ch20 honesty, now under pressure.
**Mystery movement:** Physical harm is real. The ward slate on Mira's practice band or the nearest fixture shows nothing (optional brief detail; Seraphine's ward-check in S5 is the main statement of this).
**Supporting-character function:** Mira: signal, line, anchor. Ties the line to the fence post's base. Takes the coil's tail and stands to tend it. Mira asks, or Aurelia tells her, exactly what to say to the proctor. Content only: he is in the drain at the bare end of the cut; I have gone in on purpose; do not pull spoil or slab; get shoring; tell them he is alive. Mira's response: she does not argue, and she does not pretend to be calm. She asks one practical question (how will she know if Aurelia needs to be pulled out) and Aurelia gives her a signal on the line. This is where Mira answers "I'm asking to be trusted."
**Magic usage:** A small flame for light only, kept low.
**Conflict/escalation:** The rule versus the clock. Mira's fear. The line is a lawful compromise: she does not go in untethered.
**Decision (the chapter's hinge):** Aurelia enters the cut, tethered, before shoring or clearance, and says out loud that it is her decision and that she is doing it on purpose. Not reckless: every safe step was taken first, and the rule has been outrun by the frost.
**Consequence:** She has broken a rule in plain sight, with a witness, on record in her own words. The discipline cost is now certain.
**Exit state:** Aurelia at the top of the spoil slope with a line on her waist, a small flame in her hand, and Mira's hands on the line. She goes down.

### Scene 4 — The Prop

**POV:** Aurelia
**Location:** Inside the cut and the opened drain.
**Approximate time:** The rescue, in the deepening cold.
**Characters present:** Aurelia, Aarav (below), Mira on the line above, the gate proctor arriving near the end. Groundsmen not yet.
**Scene purpose:** The centerpiece. Real cooperation between two people who cannot verify each other's information. Fire useful, imperfect and costly. Aarav's choices. The Echo's cold thins, sound returns, and the physical evidence appears without interpretation.

**Opening state:** Descent into a cold that closes on her hands. The flame is small and its light goes only a little way.
**Immediate objective:** Reach him. Free his leg without moving the prop. Get out.
**External action (progression, not choreography):**
1. **Descent.** The spoil is wet and moves. She goes slowly, testing, and the line takes weight twice. The cold thickens with depth; rime lies on the stone in one direction more than the other, and she does not yet understand the pattern.
2. **Reaching him.** The gap opens into a narrow low channel. She sees his face and hands by her flame. He is grey, alert, precise. His first action is to tell her what she cannot see: which stone is the prop, which slab above is split, what will move if touched. He reports it with hedges. He says he is not sure. He says it anyway.
3. **The wrong plan.** Her first instinct is haul: the line, force, his leg. He refuses it. She wants to argue. She does not. That is her first act of trust in his judgment under danger.
4. **The method.** The fused ice, not the rubble, is what holds him. Heat applied slowly at chosen points frees it. Fast heat would split the stone. He sets the points and the pace, by touch and sight. She supplies the flame. Neither can do this alone.
5. **The channel changes.** Somewhere in this the silence lets go: sound returns all at once. The first thing she hears is his real voice, hoarse and hedged, and for one beat she cannot trust it, then does, because it is not smooth. Along with sound comes the cold's retreat in a sequence she feels through her hands and he feels along the channel. Neither has time to interpret it.
6. **The slip.** A spike of fear as the prop groans. Her flame widens. The stone beside the plug cracks, throws grit and hot flakes, burns her forearm and palm, and the prop shifts. Both go still. He tells her to stop. She stops. She shuts it down with effort.
7. **Aarav's choice.** He could call it off and wait for shoring. He does not. He looks at what she just did and tells her to go on at a slower rate. He trusts her control after seeing it fail. His reason is stated in one plain sentence about the maths, not about her.
8. **Aurelia's choice.** She acts on "I think the prop holds" and puts her hands under it to take the final weight, on his count and no one else's, because he has read it and she has not.
9. **Release and extraction.** The plug gives. He moves on his own count and drags himself free; she takes weight on his good side. The prop holds. The two of them get to the spoil slope, up it, and to Mira's line, with the gate proctor now at the lip and Mira on the rope. They are told not to haul and they do not; the line is taken with them.
**Internal movement:** Fear held down to a working level, then one loss of hold, then its correction. A moment when she thinks about not being able to finish. She does not stop. She feels the cold in her hands as a limit and a fact.
**Relationship movement:** They cooperate without the old negotiation. There is no argument about who leads. Each yields the piece of the task that belongs to the other, and neither becomes submissive. His stop-order and her stopping. His "go on" and her going on. One dry exchange under strain that would once have been a fight and is now a joke.
**Mystery movement:** Observation only: frost laid in a line, slabs split along the channel, cold thinning in sequence. No voice, no mimicry, no sign it answered to either of them. Aurelia notes to herself that this is unlike the voice events, and that is all. No name for it.
**Supporting-character function:** Mira tends the line and marks the time. She feels the rope go slack and hard and gives the signal she and Aurelia agreed. She reports nothing beyond what she sees.
**Magic usage:** Small steady flame at slow rate; heat judged by hand. Cost: burn, numb hands, exhaustion. No True Form, no new ability. Aarav uses only stone-reading, discrimination, precision, and cold-tolerance no greater than a person's own.
**Conflict/escalation:** Cold, dark, wet stone, the prop, his leg, her fear, the slip, the clock, and the fact that neither can verify the other's information.
**Decision:** Aarav: refuse the haul, then trust her flame after the slip. Aurelia: stop arguing, then put her hands under the prop on his word.
**Consequence:** Aarav is free, injured but functional. Aurelia is burned and depleted. The cold has gone from the cut.
**Exit state:** At the lip in the last of the light. He is on his feet with his weight on her shoulder and Mira's, or sitting with a coat around him. The cut is silent and ordinary except for frost.

### Scene 5 — After the Cold

**POV:** Aurelia
**Location:** The lip of the cut, then the fence apron.
**Approximate time:** Immediately after.
**Characters present:** Aurelia, Aarav, Mira, the gate proctor, groundsmen arriving, Warden Seraphine, a clerk or second proctor.
**Scene purpose:** Decompress. Register genuine emotion. Bring in the institution. Make the incident institutionally real.

**Opening state:** The danger has stopped and nothing else has caught up.
**Immediate objective:** Stay upright; make sure he is all right; be honest to whoever asks.
**External action:** Warm coats and hot-water flasks from the proctor's post. Aurelia's burn is dressed. Aarav is checked for cold and the leg is strapped. A run of small, real behavior: her hands will not stop shaking; she is angry at him for what he did at the edge and cannot say it; he says something plain about needing her; she laughs, badly, and so does Mira, and so, briefly, does he. Mira sits down suddenly and stays down. Seraphine arrives at speed and takes charge without asking what happened first: cordon, headcount, groundsmen held back from the cut, the ward-team sent for. She goes to the lip and looks. She is shown the frost, the split slabs and the rime. The ward slate is read to her: clear. She takes that in and says nothing about it.
**Internal movement:** Aurelia stops performing calm. She lets it show and finds that no one, including Aarav, corrects it. The Ch1 line about looking calm being louder than she thinks is inverted: nobody needs her to look calm.
**Relationship movement:** He does not thank her. She does not ask him to. A small practical courtesy from each that only they would recognize. Nothing said is a label.
**Mystery movement:** The Academy sees physical trace of a physically dangerous event that the wards did not record. Seraphine's silence over the ward slate is the beat. The evidence ledger's physical items enter here.
**Supporting-character function:** Mira as witness who tells the truth without shaping it. Seraphine as institution: cordon, safety, factual accounts, procedure. She does not explain, theorize or offer hidden knowledge. Her Ch8 suspicion is answered by what she sees, not by any remark.
**Magic usage:** None. Aurelia cannot raise more than a spark, and she does not try.
**Conflict/escalation:** Seraphine's flat authority; the rule Aurelia broke; the burn; the question of who is responsible.
**Decision:** Seraphine separates the three and orders separate accounts. Aurelia asks that word be sent to Professor Voss that evening and says they will bring it themselves. Not debated.
**Consequence:** The grounds are closed; the works are sealed; the incident is now the Academy's.
**Exit state:** Lanterns are being lit. Three people are walked toward the gatehouse by separate escorts and at three separate paces.

### Scene 6 — The Record

**POV:** Aurelia
**Location:** The gatehouse office or the proctors' room off the Training Grounds gate.
**Approximate time:** Later that evening.
**Characters present:** Aurelia, Aarav, Mira (each separately, then together), Seraphine, a clerk.
**Scene purpose:** Convert the event into a record and a consequence. Show the witness pattern without staging a comparison. End on the changed state of the relationship, not a clue.

**Opening state:** Three accounts have been given separately. Aurelia is bandaged and exhausted.
**Immediate objective:** Get her account down accurately and take the consequence.
**External action:** Seraphine reads back the accounts to the clerk as procedure requires. The reader sees, in a few beats, that they overlap on the cold coming first, the silence, no voice, and sound returning together; and that each carries one detail no one else has. Seraphine does not comment on the pattern and does not compare aloud. She asks a small number of questions limited to this event: what they were doing, why they were there, why Aurelia entered. The Warden opens the review on Aurelia and states the interim restrictions and that the evaluation date does not change and that whether she sits it is for the review. She names the rule and says it was the right rule. She says it in her own flat register and adds nothing about intent. Aarav, at the clerk's table, corrects the record on one point: the decision to proceed inside the cut was made on his instruction. He asks that his name be entered against it. Seraphine records it. The entry now carries both names.
**Internal movement:** Aurelia hears the consequence, accepts it, and notices that she is not afraid of it. She is more concerned that Aarav's name is on the page. She lets it stay.
**Relationship movement:** He puts himself into the record beside her. She does not stop him. They sit together, not at an interval. Mira watches. She says one dry thing about staggering, or nothing.
**Mystery movement:** The Academy formally holds physical evidence and three matching-in-part accounts and a ward record that says nothing. Seraphine may state only that the low stretch is closed and the works sealed until examined. Aurelia and Aarav share a hedged inference (along old ground) with each other or with no one; it is not stated to Seraphine as a conclusion. The Fourteen-C "lower ground" phrase may occur as a single hedged private thought of Aurelia's, with no claim that it applies. Default: omit.
**Supporting-character function:** Mira: the third account, and the person Aurelia now does not have to protect from the truth. Seraphine: procedure and consequence.
**Magic usage:** None.
**Conflict/escalation:** The review, the restrictions, the evaluation uncertainty. Aurelia's pattern is stated as the reason by Seraphine, plainly.
**Decision:** Aarav enters his name; Aurelia lets him. Aurelia signs.
**Consequence:** A formal review is open. A record carries both names. The incident belongs to the Academy. Voss has been sent for.
**Exit state (final image):** Aurelia signs with a hand that will not steady. The clerk turns the page for Aarav's line. He writes his name against hers, badly, with his cold hand.
