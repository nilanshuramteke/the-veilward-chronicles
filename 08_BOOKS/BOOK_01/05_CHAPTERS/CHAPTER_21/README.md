## Chapter Function

Aarav is trapped in an exposed old drain when the Echo's cold, arriving with no voice, breaks the ground at the edge of the regrading cut. The rule against entering an unshored cut is sound and slower than the cold. Aurelia does everything the rule asks, then breaks it, tethered and on record, with Mira on the line. Two people who cannot verify each other's information free him together. Aurelia is burned and depleted. The Academy opens a formal review on her, three partly matching accounts and physical trace enter the record beside a ward check that accounts for nothing, and Aarav enters his name beside hers. Nothing about the Echo, the fourth stone, the marks, or the drain's meaning is explained.
