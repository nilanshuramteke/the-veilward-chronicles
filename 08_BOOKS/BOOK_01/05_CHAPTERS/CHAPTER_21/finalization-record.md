## Final Scene Structure

Scene titles are working labels; the manuscript prints none.

1. **Scene 1 — Ordinary Warmth.** Practice on the far apron with Mira and Aarav. Signal lantern, the practice line, first-year training context. Aurelia tells Mira there is something wrong on the grounds and that part of it is not hers to tell.
2. **Scene 2 — The Cold.** Cold arrives with no voice. Sound thins. Frost climbs the rope. Aarav, at the rope on the safe side, pushes Aurelia back by reflex. The bank fails beneath him and he falls into the exposed drain.
3. **Scene 3 — The Decision.** Aurelia does not go in at first. She raises the alarm with the lantern, sets the line with Mira, and hears Aarav's raps on the slab. She acknowledges the rule, writes a note, and goes in on purpose.
4. **Scene 4 — The Prop.** Aarav directs the melt by chalk and hand signal. Sound returns. The prop groans, and her fire flares and burns her arm. He sets the pace, and she holds the slab's weight on his count. The rescue succeeds.
5. **Scene 5 — After the Cold.** Relief, anger and laughter. Seraphine responds procedurally: cordon, headcount, the ward team, separate accounts.
6. **Scene 6 — The Record.** Three accounts are read. The ward check records nothing. The low stretch stays closed and the works sealed. A formal review is opened. Aurelia signs, and Aarav enters his name beneath hers.
