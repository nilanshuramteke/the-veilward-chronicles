**Chapter function:**
1. Give the book its first genuine physical danger and its first on-page physical consequence of the Echo's cold.
2. Convert Chapter 20's "trust enough to tell the truth" into "trust enough to risk something," through action, not statement.
3. Complete the friendship threshold: Aarav's reflex at the edge; Aurelia's decision; reliance on each other's uncertain information; a shared record.
4. Show Veilward as a functioning institution through action (signal, line, cordon, Warden, review).
5. Advance the mystery only as a consequence: physical evidence, overlapping and singular witness details, a ward check that accounts for nothing.
6. Widen the story beyond two people (Mira as participant and third account).
7. Break the investigation loop and the controlled emotional register.
8. Close Act II-B with a changed situation and hand Act III a state, not a cliffhanger.

**Core promise:** On an ordinary evening at the Training Grounds, the Echo's cold arrives with no voice, the ground at the edge of the regrading cut fails, and Aarav is trapped in an exposed old drain under a prop slab. The rule is sound and slower than the cold. Aurelia does everything the rule asks, then breaks it, tethered and on record. Two people who cannot verify each other's information free him together. A formal review opens on her, and Aarav writes his own name beside hers.

**Engine:** danger → decision → action → teamwork → consequence.

**Central question:** When the rule is right and the clock is faster, what does trust cost, and who pays?

### 11. Repetition & Rhythm Controls

- No scene ends on a silent walk-away, "Neither of them…," an unexplained sound, a door, a shadow, a symbol or a "for now."
- Scene endings vary: light detail (S1); hole and cold (S2); descent (S3); lip and frost (S4); paced escort at dusk (S5); the entry (S6).
- No anomaly → inspect → debate → tell Voss sequence anywhere. Investigation is incidental to the danger.
- Only one dry exchange under strain in S4; humor in S1, one exchange in S4, and S5.
- Keep chalk from becoming a clue; keep the note from becoming a document plot.
- Sentence rhythm: Aurelia's fast, physical, plainly subordinated; break long runs during the rescue.


### 12. Reader Experience Contract

A first-time reader finishing Chapter 21 should be able to say:
1. Aarav could have died, and the cause was the cold.
2. Aurelia broke a rule she understood, and said so.
3. Neither could have done it alone.
4. Mira did something that mattered.
5. The Academy responded like an institution and opened a review.
6. Two people are now visibly on each other's side.
7. Nothing was explained, and something physical happened that the wards did not record.

The reader should **not** need to know what the Echo is, why it was there, what the drain means, or anything about the fourth stone, the marks, Serpentfire, Elyra, Varun or the First Ember.
