# Chapter 10 — Scene Breakdown

Five scenes, continuous single evening (the Chapter 9-named Thursday session), Aarav POV throughout. This is the final, calibrated architecture (Phase 3.2). Do not add, remove, merge, or reorder scenes without a new authorized calibration pass.

---

## Scene 1 — Threshold and Terms

**POV:** Aarav
**Location:** Veilward Archive — entry hall into the general reading room
**Time:** Thursday evening, immediately after drills
**Characters:** Aarav, Aurelia, the Archivist (Voss's named-for-this-session delegate)
**Starting State:** Chapter 9's supervised access is legally active but untested; neither has been in this part of the Archive before.
**Objective:** Aarav wants to understand exactly what's permitted and how the general records are organized before touching anything, so the limited session isn't wasted.
**Conflict:** A time-limited session; procedural friction between Aarav's need to map the system and Aurelia's impatience to start pulling records immediately.
**Physical Action:** Handing over Voss's authorization; moving from entry hall to reading room; the Archivist demonstrating what the general index can and can't produce; Aarav testing the index against their own Chapter 4 incident's likely filing.
**Investigation:** Aarav learns the general index organizes incidents by date and a plain, ordinary description rather than any formal classification system — meaning a description-based search could surface other incidents like theirs.
**Information Boundary:** Aarav and Aurelia learn the system together; the Archivist knows only ordinary archival procedure.
**Mystery Movement:** None yet — groundwork only.
**Institutional Movement:** Access formally begins on-page.
**Scene Turn:** The description-based search approach is identified as viable.
**Exit State:** A legitimate search method exists; the session clock is running.

**Must Happen:** Access used without re-authorization; the Archive established as a real, physical, ordinary place; a plausible, ordinary search method identified through Aarav's own reasoning.
**Must Not Happen:** Any formal tag/classification system named; any batch-limit or permissions-list mechanism introduced; extended procedural exposition; any mystery content revealed.

## Scene 2 — The Search

**POV:** Aarav
**Location:** Reading room and adjoining stacks
**Time:** Same session, continuing
**Characters:** Aarav, Aurelia; the Archivist present intermittently
**Starting State:** Description-based search method established.
**Objective:** Narrow a long list of superficially similar incidents to anything resembling their own event, through cross-reference rather than reading everything cover to cover.
**Conflict:** The two divide the work differently than Aarav would have chosen alone; Aurelia wants to grab promising-looking entries directly rather than cross-referencing systematically.
**Physical Action:** Requesting records at the Archivist's discretion; reading standing at a sorting table; Aurelia sorting by date while Aarav checks descriptions for consistency; cross-checking one older entry's reference number against the general index.
**Investigation:** One entry — decades old, described in the same flat, generic "unexplained irregularity" language their own incident would carry — is isolated as the strongest candidate.
**Information Boundary:** No asymmetry; both see everything together.
**Mystery Movement:** A specific older incident is identified as the lead.
**Institutional Movement:** None yet.
**Scene Turn:** The candidate file is pulled and opened.
**Exit State:** They are reading the old disturbance record together.

**Must Happen:** Genuine physical/procedural search action; visible method friction between Aarav and Aurelia; the candidate record isolated through their own reasoning, not coincidence.
**Must Not Happen:** Any mechanical batch-retrieval rule; any formal permissions check beyond the Archivist's ordinary judgment; premature reading of the candidate file's content.

## Scene 3 — The Record That Stops

**POV:** Aarav
**Location:** Reading room, sorting table
**Time:** Same session, midpoint
**Characters:** Aarav, Aurelia
**Starting State:** Holding the candidate file.
**Objective:** Verify this is a genuine parallel, not a coincidence.
**Conflict:** The file itself creates the pressure — short, procedural, stopping mid-process, redirecting to an inaccessible supplement.
**Physical Action:** Aarav reading closely — witness count (unspecified, plural), a generic unexplained-irregularity description, a ward check that also produced no accounting; Aurelia reading alongside; both noting the entry's last line cites a numbered supplement; Aarav noticing and re-checking a restricted-holdings stamp dated later than the record's original filing.
**Investigation:** (1) A prior disturbance is confirmed real, witnessed, and procedurally logged. (2) It shares exactly one meaningful parallel with their own event — the ward check found nothing to account for it. (3) The entry ends at a supplement reference. (4) The restricted-holdings stamp proves the file was reclassified after its original filing — the "quiet classification" made concrete.
**Information Boundary:** Shared discovery, no asymmetry from this scene's content. Privately, internally, Aarav notes — unspoken — that the old file's brief mention of divided witness accounts echoes his own Chapter 8 verification instinct. Not voiced, not new plot information, not inferable by Aurelia.
**Character Movement:** Controlled reaction — he re-checks the stamp date before treating it as significant, consistent with his verification-first characterization.
**Relationship Movement:** A scene-local unity — both looking at the same page for the same reason — not a relationship-stage shift; neither reflects on it or names it.
**Mystery Movement:** Major — first concrete evidence this has happened before, and was obscured afterward.
**Institutional Movement:** They now know exactly what they need next, and that it sits outside ordinary retrieval.
**Scene Turn:** Aurelia decides to request the supplement immediately.
**Exit State:** A formal request for the sealed supplement is about to be made.

**Must Happen:** The old record confirmed real, generic, and parallel only through the ward-failure trait; the supplement reference and the postdated restriction stamp both discovered on-page through direct reading.
**Must Not Happen:** Cold, silence, sound disruption, or any voice/speech reference in the old record; any confirmation the two events are identical; any reveal of cause; any voiced sharing of Aarav's Chapter 8 knowledge; a static, purely-conversational feel — physical handling of the file must carry the scene alongside the reading.

## Scene 4 — The Refusal

**POV:** Aarav
**Location:** Reading room / retrieval counter
**Time:** Same session, running past its nominal end
**Characters:** Aarav, Aurelia, the Archivist, then Voss
**Starting State:** The supplement request has been submitted.
**Objective:** Get a straight answer, framed as legitimately as possible.
**Conflict:** The gap between what they're allowed to ask and what they want; Voss's arrival raises the stakes of the whole session.
**Physical Action:** The Archivist checking the request against what she can authorize and declining to process it further; a message sent; a wait; Voss arriving into the reading room (into the work, per established Book 1 pattern — not a summons elsewhere); Voss handling the file himself, confirming its restriction without needing to be told, closing it, stating plainly the supplement was not part of what he authorized.
**Investigation:** Voss's calm, already-informed handling of the file is itself the evidence — he doesn't deny the old disturbance or act surprised by the request. He confirms only that the incident occurred and its record was classified; he explicitly declines to say why or by whom.
**Information Boundary:** Witnessed identically by both. Proves institutional secrecy and Voss's pre-existing awareness of the file. Does not prove Voss knows the disturbance's cause, that it relates to their own event beyond the one shared trait, or that he ordered the classification himself.
**Character Movement:** Aarav applies his "notice what doesn't fit" method to an authority figure for the first time, rather than to a magical anomaly.
**Relationship Movement:** Aurelia's more confrontational push and Aarav's more measured approach both contribute; neither is framed as more correct. Aarav privately, silently notes that her push is what got them an answer this session — unstated, not a repeat of Chapter 9's grudging-credit beat.
**Mystery Movement:** Institutional secrecy around the precedent becomes confirmed fact, not inference.
**Institutional Movement:** Voss reasserts his Chapter-9-reserved right to personally supervise any future session he judges warrants it — tightening, not revoking, access.
**Scene Turn:** "Did this happen before?" becomes "Yes — and someone made sure it wouldn't be easy to find."
**Exit State:** Session ends on Voss's terms; the supplement remains inaccessible; future sessions will have Voss present by his own choice.

**Must Happen:** Voss's arrival into the existing scene (not a summons elsewhere); a limited, enforcement-only confirmation from Voss; his reassertion of personal supervision, explicitly framed as exercising the exact clause reserved in Chapter 9.
**Must Not Happen:** Any Voss line that explains cause, history, or the relationship between the two disturbances; any implication Voss knows the anomaly's nature; any new policy, tier, or department invented to justify the tightening.

## Scene 5 — Corridor (Compressed)

**POV:** Aarav
**Location:** Academy corridor, walking back from the Archive
**Time:** Immediately after, same evening
**Characters:** Aarav, Aurelia; ordinary background Academy life only
**Starting State:** Just left the Archive under Voss's new terms.
**Objective:** Process what happened and what it means for continuing at all.
**Conflict:** Low-key, earned frustration — confirmation without access.
**Physical Action:** Walking through ordinary evening Academy bustle — brief, unforced texture, no new subplot.
**Investigation:** None new; this scene consolidates rather than reveals.
**Information Boundary:** Explicitly reconfirmed — Aarav's Chapter 8 detail is never voiced. It surfaces only as his private, wordless comparison between "an answer withheld by circumstance" (the old file) and "an answer withheld by choice" (what he alone knows about the voice).
**Character Movement:** Aarav formalizes, privately, that institutional behavior is now evidence alongside physical/testimonial evidence.
**Relationship Movement:** A practical, unresolved disagreement about what to do next — Stage 2 friction, deliberately unresolved, deliberately not framed as respect.
**Mystery Movement:** Net chapter gain consolidated: precedent confirmed; classification confirmed; cause and content still unknown.
**Institutional Movement:** Locked in from Scene 4.
**Scene Turn:** Neither has an answer for what happens next — the disagreement about method itself becomes the open thread.
**Exit State:** Investigation continues under tighter, Voss-controlled terms; unresolved, carrying directly into Chapter 11.

**Must Happen:** Brief, genuine Academy-life texture; a short, practical, forward-facing closing beat; explicit reconfirmation that Aarav's Chapter 8 knowledge stays private.
**Must Not Happen:** An analytical debrief; a recap of the discovery or Voss's refusal; an extended argument or "what do we do now" dialogue scene; any new mystery evidence, clue, or supernatural event; any emotional-bonding beat or relationship reclassification; a generic, unresolved walk-away tone identical to prior chapter endings — the closing beat must be decisive in tone even though the situation remains open.
