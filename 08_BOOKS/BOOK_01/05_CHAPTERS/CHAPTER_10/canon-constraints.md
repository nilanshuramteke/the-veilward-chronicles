# Chapter 10 — Canon Constraints

Phase 3.2 is the final authority for Chapter 10 architecture. This document translates that architecture into hard drafting constraints. It does not introduce new story decisions.

## 1. POV and Narrative Constraints

- Aarav — close third person, past tense, throughout.
- No omniscient knowledge or narration.
- No Aurelia interiority — she may be observed and inferred from, never read directly.
- No Voss interiority.
- No Archivist interiority.
- Aarav's inferences must be distinguishable in the prose from confirmed facts.

## 2. Chapter 9 Continuity Constraints

- Restitution is complete.
- Supervised archive access is already active — do not stage a re-authorization scene.
- The Chapter 9 arrangement (limited, supervised, personal and revocable) remains valid and unmodified.
- Voss already possesses the authority established in Chapter 9, including his retained right to personally supervise any session he judges warrants it.
- Chapter 10 uses the access rather than renegotiating it.

## 3. Relationship Constraints

**Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation.**

Allowed: tactical cooperation; friction; competitive methodology; practical dependence; competence recognition without emotional framing.

Forbidden: friendship; trust as a relationship milestone; romance; emotional vulnerability as bonding; explicit "respect" framing; any relationship reclassification; either character reflecting on what the other means to them.

**Chapter 11 owns the "Respect begins" movement. Chapter 10 must not perform it.**

## 4. Mystery Constraints

Chapter 10 may establish: a prior unexplained disturbance; multiple witnesses; an unaccounted ward result; an incomplete record; later restriction/classification of that record; an inaccessible supplement; Voss's refusal to explain further.

Chapter 10 must not establish: exact identity between the old and current disturbance; cause of either; entity identity; motive; any supernatural mechanism.

## 5. Historical Precedent Constraints

The old record must remain generic.

Allowed: multiple witnesses; a generic unexplained magical irregularity; an unaccounted ward result; flat, procedural institutional language; later restriction.

Forbidden: cold; silence; sound disruption; voices, fragments, or speech of any kind; any Echo-specific behavioral signature; breach/intrusion language; any reference to Serpentfire, Outer Dark, Seeker, or First Ember.

**Test:** a careful reader must not be able to conclude from the old record alone that it is definitely the same phenomenon as Chapter 4. The only permitted shared trait is that the wards failed to account for it.

## 6. Archive Constraints

Allowed: the established Archive (`04_WORLD_AND_LOCATIONS/book-one-locations.md`; `03_ACADEMY/academy-campus-and-locations.md`); ordinary records; an ordinary general index/reference volume; the canon-established Archivist role (`03_ACADEMY/faculty-and-mentors.md`); Archivist judgment in place of mechanical rules; restricted holdings; supervised access; Voss's existing gatekeeping authority; a restricted-holdings notation/stamp used only to show a record was later restricted.

Forbidden: an incident-tag classification system; a database-like search system; invented permissions tiers; batch retrieval limits; new archive technology; a magical archive interface or search mechanism; any new Academy administrative mechanism or department.

## 7. Voss Constraints

Voss may: recognize the file; know it is restricted; know a supplement exists and sits outside what he authorized; enforce that boundary; decline to explain further.

Voss must not be shown to know, and the text must not imply he knows: the cause of the old disturbance; the cause of the current anomaly; the exact relationship between the two events; Echo identity, origin, or motive; Serpentfire; Outer Dark; the Seeker; the First Ember; true Veil history; Veil weakness; the Lost Path.

Whether Voss himself ordered the original reclassification is explicitly unresolved and must stay unresolved.

**Voss dialogue test:** every line must be one of — confirmation of a limited fact; enforcement of access; refusal; redirection; statement of existing authority. No line may provide historical exposition.

## 8. Information Firewall

Aarav retains his Chapter 8 behavioral-mismatch knowledge (the specific error in the Dev-like voice). Aurelia does not know it and must not learn it in Chapter 10. No accidental transfer, no dialogue slip, no narrated implication that she has inferred it.

## 9. Protected Lore

The following remain fully unrevealed and unimplied:

- Serpentfire
- Elyra
- Varun
- Unknown Student
- False intruder theory
- Hidden chamber
- First Ember
- Echo identity
- Echo origin
- Echo motive
- Echo intelligence/agency
- Seeker
- Outer Dark
- Lost Path
- True Veil origin
- Veil weakness

## 10. Other Locked Clues

**Angular mark:** Dormant. Do not introduce it in Chapter 10.

**Chapter 3 wind:** Unrelated. No reference, no parallel.

## 11. Magic Constraint

No magical restoration, reconstruction, replay, recovery, or perfect historical reconstruction of the missing record or any damaged material. All discovery must proceed through ordinary reading, observation, institutional procedure, and character reasoning.
