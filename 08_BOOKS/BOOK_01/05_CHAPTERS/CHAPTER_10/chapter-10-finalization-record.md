## Chapter Function

Chapter 10 uses the supervised archive access earned in Chapter 9 to establish a concrete historical precedent for the Chapter 4 anomaly, and to demonstrate that the Academy's own institutional record-keeping actively obscures part of that history.

Locked progression:

**earned access → ordinary archival search → historical precedent → incomplete record → restriction → Voss boundary → changed investigative situation**

## Final Narrative State

### Mystery
The primary mystery remains unresolved:

> A real anomalous event occurred, but the Academy's ward record contains nothing capable of accounting for it.

Chapter 10 adds: **a previous, similarly witnessed disturbance exists in the Academy's own historical record, that record is incomplete, and the material that would complete it was deliberately restricted after the fact.**

The historical incident shares only one meaningful trait with the Chapter 4 anomaly — the wards failed to account for it. Chapter 10 does not establish cause, mechanism, entity identity, motive, or any definitive connection between the two events.

### Institutional State
The Chapter 9 supervised-access arrangement remains legitimate, active, and unmodified in its underlying terms. Voss exercises the exact right he reserved in Chapter 9 — personal supervision of any session he judges warrants it — rather than imposing a new policy. No new Academy department, certification, roster, or administrative system was introduced.

### Character State
**Aarav** remains analytical, precise, controlled, verification-first, and competitive. **Aurelia** remains direct, kinetic, and independently motivated; she locates the historical record and drives the supplement request rather than functioning as a passive assistant.

### Relationship State
Aurelia and Aarav remain **Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation**. The chapter demonstrates tactical dependence and methodological friction without advancing them toward friendship, trust, or romance. Chapter 11's "Respect begins" movement remains fully untouched and available.

### Information Lock
Aarav retains his Chapter 8 knowledge of the specific behavioral mismatch in the Dev-like voice. Aurelia does not know it. Chapter 10 does not transfer this knowledge in any form — dialogue, narration, or inference. Dev remains ordinary and unaware of the anomaly.

### Protected-Lore Lock
Chapter 10 does not reveal or establish: Outer Dark, the Seeker, the Harbinger, the First Ember, Serpentfire, Elyra, Varun, the Lost Path, the true Veil origin or weakness, breach/intrusion theory, the hidden chamber, or any Echo identity, origin, motive, or intelligence. The Unknown Student thread is not introduced. Serpentfire remains reserved for Chapter 12.

### Magic Lock
No magical restoration, reconstruction, replay, or recovery of the missing record occurs anywhere in the chapter. The record remains incomplete because the relevant material is institutionally restricted, not because of any failed or absent magical technique.

### Angular Mark and Chapter 3 Wind
The angular mark remains dormant and unconnected to the historical record, the Archive, or Voss. The Chapter 3 wind remains unrelated to any element of Chapter 10.
