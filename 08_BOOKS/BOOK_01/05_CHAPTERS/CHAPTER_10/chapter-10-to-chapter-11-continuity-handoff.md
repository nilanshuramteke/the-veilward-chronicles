Chapter 10 is finalized. This document is the authoritative continuity input for Chapter 11.

## 1. Immediate Starting Situation

At the end of Chapter 10:

- Aurelia and Aarav used the Chapter 9 supervised archive access for the first time.
- They located a previous, generically-described disturbance in the Academy's general incident record.
- That disturbance was witnessed and, like the Chapter 4 event, went unaccounted for by a ward check.
- The record terminates at a supplement reference that sits in restricted holdings.
- A restriction notation shows the file was reclassified after its original filing — someone later decided this needed to be harder to find.
- Voss personally intervened, confirmed the restriction, and refused to explain it.
- Voss has exercised his Chapter-9-reserved right to personally supervise all future sessions, rather than delegating.
- The underlying Chapter 9 access arrangement itself is unchanged — Voss tightened his own participation, not the terms.

Chapter 11 begins from this state. It must not restage the Chapter 9 authorization or the Chapter 10 archive session.

## 2. Ending Situation Entering Chapter 11

The investigation has advanced from "we don't know why the wards failed" to "this has failed before, and the Academy has taken active steps to keep that fact obscure." No answer to *why* exists yet. The obstacle has shifted from a lack of evidence to an institutional boundary.

## 3. Mystery State

The primary mystery remains unresolved:

> A real anomalous event occurred, but the Academy's ward record contains nothing capable of accounting for it.

Chapter 10 adds: a previous, similarly witnessed disturbance exists in the historical record; its ward check also failed; its record is incomplete; the missing portion was restricted after the fact.

**Fairness lock:** the historical disturbance shares only the ward-failure trait with the Chapter 4 event. It does not confirm identical cause, identical phenomenon, or any named entity.

Chapter 11 inherits an active, escalated mystery — not a solved one.

## 4. Institutional State

- The Chapter 9 supervised-access arrangement remains legitimate and active.
- Voss now personally supervises every session, by his own choice, invoking the exact clause he reserved in Chapter 9.
- The sealed supplement (ref. 14-C) remains inaccessible.
- The Archivist remains an ordinary, grounded role — not a source of hidden knowledge.
- No new Academy department, certification, roster, or administrative policy exists.

## 5. Aurelia State

- Direct, kinetic, independently motivated, willing to challenge restrictions.
- Located the historical record herself and pushed for the supplement request.
- Frustrated by the restriction but not defeated by it; the negotiated leverage from Chapter 9 is intact, not lost.
- Does not know Aarav's specific Chapter 8 behavioral-mismatch observation about the Dev-like voice.

## 6. Aarav State

- Analytical, precise, controlled, verification-first, competitive.
- Extended his verification method to reading institutional behavior (Voss's familiarity with the restricted file) as evidence for the first time.
- Privately, internally compared Voss's withholding to his own unshared Chapter 8 knowledge — this comparison was never voiced and must not surface in Chapter 11 either.
- Still retains the Chapter 8 behavioral-mismatch detail, unshared.

## 7. Relationship State

**Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation.**

Chapter 10 demonstrated tactical cooperation and unresolved methodological friction (Aarav's caution vs. Aurelia's directness) without advancing the relationship. Chapter 11 owns the next movement — **"Respect begins."** Chapter 10 does not perform or pre-empt that movement; it only leaves the relationship functional and unresolved, ready for Chapter 11's own material to advance it.

## 8. Information Asymmetry

Fully intact. Aurelia does not know the specific behavioral mismatch Aarav identified in the Dev-like voice in Chapter 8. Nothing in Chapter 10 transferred this knowledge. Dev remains ordinary and unaware of the anomaly.

Confirmed to enforce that boundary. **Not** confirmed to know: the cause of either disturbance, or that the two events are definitively connected beyond the shared ward-failure trait. Whether Voss himself ordered the original reclassification remains explicitly unresolved.

An ordinary, grounded, unnamed faculty role (consistent with the established "Archivists and Researchers" category). She enforces access boundaries and assists with general records.
