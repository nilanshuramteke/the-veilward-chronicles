## Chapter Function

Chapter 14 is the false-convergence chapter immediately preceding the Chapter 15 midpoint. A formal institutional review compels Aurelia and Aarav to disclose their three accumulated evidence threads together for the first time; the Academy responds with a credible but unproven theory built on a disturbed seal in a long-decommissioned sub-level corridor — that an intruder has been entering Veilward. Aarav is tempted to accept it as the investigation's first genuinely verifiable evidence, until Aurelia's plain, direct question exposes a gap in his own usually-rigorous method: passage proves nothing about origin. Unable to resolve the disagreement through argument, they choose to verify the corridor themselves, openly, before it is sealed — acting together while still disagreeing — and the chapter ends the instant the sealed door opens. Chapter 15 owns everything beyond it.
