## Chapter Function

Chapter 14 returns agency to Aurelia and Aarav after Chapter 13, then immediately tests what they do with it. A formal institutional review compels them to disclose their three accumulated evidence threads together for the first time, fulfilling the promise made at the end of Chapter 13. The Academy responds with a credible but unproven theory built on a disturbed seal in a long-decommissioned sub-level corridor — that an intruder has been entering Veilward through forgotten infrastructure. Aarav is tempted to accept it as the investigation's first genuinely verifiable evidence, until Aurelia's plain, direct question exposes a gap in his own usually-rigorous method: passage proves nothing about origin. Unable to resolve the disagreement through argument, they choose to verify the corridor themselves, openly, before it is sealed — acting together while still disagreeing — and the chapter ends the instant the sealed door opens.

Locked progression:

**Compelled disclosure → institutional false convergence → evidentiary temptation → directional gap exposed → deliberate, disclosed verification → protected threshold.**

The chapter does not explain the corridor, confirm or deny the intruder theory, or resolve any prior mystery thread. It advances the mystery from "three separate unresolved threads held in reserve by the protagonists" (Chapter 13) to "four threads, now formally disclosed, which an institutional authority proposes — without narrative confirmation — might be one phenomenon."
