## 1. Immediate Starting Situation

At the end of Chapter 14:

- Aurelia and Aarav were formally compelled, by an ongoing institutional security review, to disclose their three previously-separate evidence threads together for the first time: the Chapter 11 angular marker, the Chapter 12 archway sighting, and the Chapter 13 annex marks.
- The Academy, examining a widened security audit prompted by that same review, identified an old sub-level service corridor — logged decades ago as sealed/decommissioned — whose physical seal has recently been disturbed.
- Voss presented the institution's theory: that the disturbed seal, together with the three disclosed threads, suggests someone has been entering Veilward through forgotten infrastructure. The corridor was slated for permanent sealing once the review's recommendation was filed.
- Aarav initially found the theory compelling — the first record-based, physically verifiable evidence in the entire investigation. Aurelia raised three concrete objections, the decisive one being that the disturbed seal proves passage occurred, not where it originated.
- Aarav recognized a specific gap in his own method: he had tested Aurelia's claims and his own observations rigorously throughout the investigation, but had not yet needed to audit an institutional authority's own conclusion, since Voss had given him no prior reason to doubt one.
- Unable to resolve their disagreement through further argument, and with the corridor's independent-verification window closing once the recommendation was filed, Aurelia and Aarav chose to examine it themselves — openly, having told Voss beforehand, not covertly.
- They reached the corridor, confirmed that its physical condition matched the institutional record exactly, and opened the sealed door.

Chapter 15 begins at the instant that door opens. It must not restage the disclosure, the evidentiary disagreement, or the walk to the corridor.

## 3. Mystery State

The primary mystery remains unresolved, now carrying four distinct threads:

- **Chapter 11 angular marker** — unexplained.
- **Chapter 12 Unknown Student** — unidentified; referred to in Chapter 14 only as "the figure."
- **Chapter 13 annex marks** — unresolved; the site is now permanently inaccessible.
- **Chapter 14 sub-level corridor** — physically verified (it exists; its seal is genuinely disturbed; the physical condition matches the institutional record) but its cause, origin, and meaning remain entirely unestablished.

**Fairness lock:** the Academy's theory — that these four threads together indicate an intruder entering Veilward through the corridor — is presented strictly as an unconfirmed institutional interpretation, voiced only through Voss. Chapter 14's own narration never endorses it. The specific, unresolved gap Aurelia identified — that proof of passage through the corridor is not proof of where that passage originated, or whether it came from outside the Academy at all — is exposed but not answered.

Per the Book 1 architecture, Chapter 15 is the designated midpoint: the breach theory is revealed to be false, and the true shape of the mystery changes fundamentally. This handoff does not anticipate or restate that revelation; it only establishes that Chapter 14 leaves the intruder theory exactly as credible-but-unconfirmed as it was when proposed.
