# Chapter 14 — Canon Constraints

> **Authority:** This document is binding for Phase 4 Drafting. It consolidates Phase 1 Orientation, Phase 2 Detailed Plan, Phase 3.1 Targeted Calibration + Reconciliation, and Phase 3.2 Final Calibration. Where any earlier Chapter 14 document conflicts with this one, this one governs.

## POV

**Aarav — close third person, past tense.** Locked by the Book 1 chapter outline and reaffirmed through every planning phase. Chapter 14 is Aurelia's alternating counterpart chapter to Chapter 13.

- Full Aarav interiority is permitted and expected — his discomfort at losing interpretive control in Scene 1, his temptation toward the corridor theory, and his recognition of his own evidentiary blind spot in Scene 2 are all required interiority beats.
- Everything about Aurelia, Mira, and Voss must be inferred through their speech, behavior, physical action, and Aarav's interpretation. No Aurelia interiority. No Mira interiority. No Voss interiority.
- No omniscient narration may confirm, deny, or explain the mystery beyond what Aarav himself can observe or reason.

## Must Preserve (Established Canon/Continuity)

- The Chapter 13 exit state in full (see `chapter-13-continuity-input.md`).
- Archive access remains suspended, unresolved, since Chapter 12.
- The Chapter 11 angular marker remains unresolved.
- The Chapter 12 Unknown Student remains unidentified and does not appear on-page.
- SERPENTFIRE remains unexplained; the word may be referenced only obliquely, never named on-page, never given meaning.
- Aarav's Chapter 8 knowledge of the Dev-like voice's behavioral mismatch remains entirely private — non-issue by POV construction, but no narration may gesture at its content.
- Dev remains unaware of anything in this book's mystery threads; he does not appear in Chapter 14.
- The Chapter 3 wind remains unrelated and unreferenced.
- Voss remains a competent, procedural, credible institutional gatekeeper — never an exposition source, never wrong out of incompetence.

## Must Not Reveal

- What lies beyond the sub-level corridor's sealed door.
- The true cause of the corridor's disturbed seal.
- Who or what used the corridor, or why.
- The true direction of passage through it (whether anything ever came from outside the Academy at all).
- Any confirmed causal relationship among the Ch11 marker, the Ch12 Unknown Student sighting, the Ch13 annex marks, and the Ch14 corridor.
- The Chapter 15 midpoint conclusion ("the breach theory is false" / "the danger was already inside") in any form, explicit or implied.
- SERPENTFIRE's meaning, origin, or history.
- The Unknown Student's identity, species, motive, or affiliation.

## Must Not Imply

- That the annex/marker/sighting/corridor threads are confirmed manifestations of one phenomenon — the institution may propose this; the narrative may not endorse it.
- That the corridor's disturbed seal proves direction of origin — it proves passage only.
- Agency, intelligence, motive, or entity behavior behind any of the four accumulated threads.
- Any new magical mechanism, instrument, measurement, or diagnostic capability for either Aurelia's fire (off-page this chapter) or Aarav's Naga sensitivity.

## Character Constraints

### Aarav
- Analytical, verification-first, institutionally aware — consistent with Chapters 8–13.
- His temptation toward the corridor theory must be specific: he has extensive practice verifying raw evidence and testing Aurelia's claims (Ch11), but has not previously needed to audit an *institutional authority's own conclusion* — Voss has been reliably procedural throughout. This is a first-time blind spot in a new direction, not a general lapse in his established rigor.
- He must not become gullible, careless, emotionally irrational, or unlike himself at any point.
- His recognition of the blind spot is triggered specifically by Aurelia's directional question — not by generic self-doubt.

### Aurelia
- Direct, physically confident, independently motivated — consistent with Chapters 1–13.
- Her skepticism rests on three concrete objections: the archway figure's composure/timing (Ch12 memory); the annex worksite's active state (Ch13 memory); and the directional gap in the corridor evidence (passage ≠ origin).
- She does not know the intruder theory is false. She identifies an evidentiary gap; she does not solve the mystery.
- Her agreement to accompany Aarav into the corridor is her own choice, not capitulation.

### Mira
- Present only in Scene 3, in an ordinary, incidental campus encounter.
- Strictly behavioral observation only: she notices *how* Aarav is acting (short, distracted, insistent, poor listening), never *what* he believes or *why*.
- She has zero access to the investigation, the corridor, the institutional theory, or Aurelia's objections.
- She must not diagnose his reasoning, his bias, or his evidentiary process with any certainty beyond an ordinary friend's read of someone's mood.
- Does not reappear after Scene 3.

### Voss
- Procedural, credible, competent — consistent with Chapters 9–13.
- Presents the review's findings as a legitimate institutional conclusion, not exposition delivered for the reader's benefit.
- Does not know or reveal the true cause of the corridor disturbance.

## Relationship Constraints

**Current state:** Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation, approaching the beginning edge of Stage 3 — Respect (carried forward from Chapter 13).

**Chapter 14 movement:** They act together despite a genuinely unresolved disagreement about what the evidence means — a register beyond Chapter 13's joint-reasoning-to-consensus structure. This is tested through interpretation, not action.

Do not advance to:
- established friendship,
- emotional intimacy,
- declared trust,
- romance,
- confession,
- protective-romantic behavior.

No romance-temperature beat is authorized this chapter. The relationship movement is delivered entirely through direct dialogue and choice in Scene 4 (Aarav asks; Aurelia chooses to go) — not through proximity, unnamed feeling, or distance-recalibration constructions.

## Magic Constraints

- Aurelia's fire does not appear on-page this chapter (she is not shown using it) and must not be introduced as a detection or diagnostic tool if referenced at all.
- Aarav's Naga sensitivity, if used at all in Scene 4, remains bounded and non-diagnostic, exactly as established — no comparison to named phenomena, no confirmation of cause or entity.
- No new magical instrument, measurement, formal experiment, or repeatable detection procedure may be introduced.

## Structural Constraints

- Exactly four scenes: The Statement / What the Record Doesn't Show / Mira / The Door Beneath.
- POV locked to Aarav throughout.
- Scene 4 must be executed as an overt, disclosed act of verification — not a covert trespass. No sneaking, stealth, or infiltration framing. This is the chapter's explicit differentiation from Chapter 13's covert worksite investigation.
- The chapter must end at the physical instant the sealed door opens, with absolutely no perception of anything beyond the threshold (see `scene-breakdown.md` Scene 4 for the full hard lock).

## Superseded / Rejected Design Elements — Do Not Use

The following formulations were considered and explicitly rejected during planning; they must not appear in Phase 4 drafting:

- Aarav's bias framed as a generic "wanting to win the argument" (superseded by: a specific, institutional-trust blind spot triggered by a precise question).
- Mira diagnosing Aarav's reasoning, bias, or theory (superseded by: purely behavioral observation with zero content-level access).
- Scene 4 framed as covert, secretive, or infiltration-styled (superseded by: an overt, disclosed verification act).
- Any sensory perception, however brief, of what lies beyond the corridor door (superseded by: a hard cut at the moment the door opens).
- Aurelia's directional objection stated as a conclusion ("it never came from outside") rather than a question (superseded by: "we know something passed through; we don't know where it started").
