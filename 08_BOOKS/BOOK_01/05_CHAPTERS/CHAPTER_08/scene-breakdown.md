# Chapter 8 — Scene Breakdown

## Scene 1 — Crossing the Line
**Purpose:** Trigger the chapter through Aarav's deliberate action.

Aarav is moving through Academy grounds on an unrelated route. He notices Aurelia beyond the restricted cordon and changes direction, crossing the cordon himself.

No routine opening, recap conversation, Voss assignment continuation, or lore exposition.

The scene establishes that Aarav is choosing investigation and committing a real rule violation.

## Scene 2 — The Fading Explanation
**Purpose:** Construct a reasonable but incomplete explanation through physical testing.

Aarav registers the disturbance at one position, moves farther away, registers it again, and repeats from a third position. The observed intensity becomes weaker with distance.

Important: this establishes a spatial difference at that moment. It does NOT objectively establish temporal fading.

Aurelia produces a small, controlled flame near the affected point and observes that the cold does not react to or displace the flame.

Together, the results appear consistent with a faint remaining effect winding down.

This is a provisional working explanation and must be earned through action, not conversation.

They begin to disengage because the site appears inert/fading.

## Scene 3 — The Wrong Shadow
**Purpose:** Break the apparent explanation through active recurrence.

The environmental disturbance sharpens again: cold/stillness return or intensify.

Dev's voice is heard. The imitation uses established Dev voice/cadence and boot-lace/knot material, then makes the behavioral mistake: it asks Aarav a direct literal question instead of deflecting through humor.

Aarav initially registers the voice as Dev, then focuses on what was actually said and recognizes: **that is not how Dev behaves.**

Aurelia perceives the returning environmental shift before Aarav has finished processing the voice and physically pulls/repositions him. He moves with her.

Aarav concludes only that the fading explanation no longer accounts for the new observation and that the site remains active in an unknown way.

No intelligence, intent, observation, mechanism, or protected lore.

## Scene 4 — Caught
**Purpose:** Create irreversible institutional consequence and an active ending.

Aarav and Aurelia remain inside the restricted zone.

The posted proctor notices the observable effects and their unauthorized presence, intercepts them, and summons Seraphine.

Seraphine arrives as operational authority, takes control, orders the cordon secured/resealed/extended as appropriate, addresses the students, and asks Aarav a direct procedural question.

End before Aarav answers.

Neither protagonist leaves.

No alarm, diagnostic tool, ward detection, lore explanation, resolution, or walk-away ending.
