## Drafting objective
Make Chapter 8 the first unmistakable action-forward progression after the holding pattern of Chapters 5–7.

The chapter should feel like:
**They physically go somewhere → they test something → their working explanation seems plausible → the phenomenon acts again → the explanation fails → they are caught.**

## Investigation
The physical tests must appear on the page.
Aarav must actually move between positions and observe the disturbance.
Aurelia must actually use a small controlled flame and observe the result.
Do not summarize the investigation as "they tested it."

## Apparent explanation
Make the fading interpretation plausible, but never convert it into established magical law.

Correct logic:
**observed gradient + no fire response → provisional fading interpretation → new voice event → explanation fails.**

## Dev imitation
The imitation must initially register as Dev.
Use established Chapter 2 material only.
Exact error: the imitation asks Aarav directly for an answer/reassurance where real Dev would deflect through humor.
Aarav catches this through knowledge of Dev.
Do not make the voice error supernatural or cryptographic.

## Echo firewall
The event may demonstrate present-tense activity.
It must not reveal intelligence, awareness, intent, motive, observation, learning, origin, mechanism, or entity status.

## Aurelia/Aarav
Aurelia remains independently motivated.
Aarav remains her rival.
Her physical reaction during recurrence should happen before Aarav finishes analyzing.
He moves with her.
This is provisional reliance under pressure. Do not label it trust.

## Institutional consequence
The proctor notices them through ordinary observation.
Seraphine responds procedurally.
Do not introduce an alarm, ward response, diagnostic tool, or security technology.

## Ending
End with Seraphine actively taking control and asking Aarav a direct question.
Do not answer the question.
Do not let either protagonist leave.
The ending should feel like an event continuing beyond the page.

## Prose discipline
Avoid:
- repetitive "which..." constructions;
- repeated "the way..." constructions;
- repeated "the kind of..." constructions;
- "didn't have a clean answer";
- "filed it away" or similar cataloguing closure;
- excessive "already";
- symmetrical dialogue volleys;
- excessive hedging;
- repetitive walk-away endings.

Favor concrete physical behavior over analytical summary.

## POV discipline
Aarav's interiority should emphasize comparison, observation, physical verification, controlled inference, evidence limits, competitiveness, and uncertainty expressed through action.
Do not make him emotionless or mechanical.
Do not import Aurelia's heat/fire-centered interior register.

## Supporting cast
Use only where functional:
- Dev: voice only;
- posted proctor: institutional discovery;
- Seraphine: procedural consequence.
Do not insert Mira or Lyra merely for presence.

## Hard prohibition
Do not invent anything Phase 3.2 did not authorize.
If the prose seems to require a new mechanism, stop and flag it rather than improvising.

## Output
Return the complete Chapter 8 manuscript only.
Do not include analysis, audit findings, explanations, or commentary with the manuscript.
