## Primary mystery

## Epistemic firewall
Aarav may conclude only:
- the apparent fading explanation is insufficient;
- the site is still active in some unknown way;
- the voice is an imperfect reproduction;
- the reproduction contains a concrete behavioral error.

He must NOT conclude:
- intelligence;
- awareness;
- observation;
- learning;
- intent;
- motive;
- deliberate target selection;
- understanding of human behavior;
- origin;
- mechanism;
- entity status;
- inside/outside Veil origin.

## Ward firewall
