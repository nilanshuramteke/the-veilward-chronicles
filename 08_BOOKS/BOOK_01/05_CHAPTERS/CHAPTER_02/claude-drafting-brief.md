# Claude Drafting Brief — Chapter 2: The Serpent's Measure

## Your Task

Draft Chapter 2 of *The Veilward Chronicles: Book One*.

Write complete manuscript prose.

## Required Narrative Form

- Third-person limited
- Past tense
- Strictly Aarav's viewpoint
- Clear, emotionally grounded prose
- Vivid but controlled imagery
- Natural sentence variation
- No exposition dumps

## Core Chapter Goal

Show the rivalry from Aarav's side.

The reader should understand that:

- Aarav finds Aurelia difficult,
- he respects her ability,
- his assumptions about her are incomplete,
- Aurelia's assumptions about him are also incomplete.

Continue the Academy evaluation sequence and introduce Dev and Mira naturally.

Do not begin the central mystery.

## Aarav's Viewpoint Voice

His interiority should feel:

- observant,
- measured,
- precise,
- dry when appropriate,
- emotionally restrained but not emotionless.

Aarav should not narrate like an author explaining philosophy.

His observations should arise from:

- what he notices,
- what he measures,
- what he chooses to ignore,
- and what irritates him despite himself.

## Aurelia Through Aarav's Eyes

Aurelia should remain:

- capable,
- fast-moving,
- competitive,
- difficult for Aarav to predict or agree with.

Aarav may interpret her incorrectly.

Do not make the narration confirm his assumptions simply because this is his POV.

Do not give him knowledge of her private Chapter 1 flame instability.

## Dev and Mira

Introduce both naturally.

### Dev
Bring grounded social or practical energy.

### Mira
Bring perceptive social observation.

Neither should be introduced through biography.

Let the reader learn who they are through:

- action,
- dialogue,
- choices,
- and what Aarav notices.

Do not permanently define their open canon details.

## Relationship State

Aurelia and Aarav are rivals.

Their rivalry should contain:

- friction,
- familiarity,
- mutual recognition of ability,
- conflicting methods.

Do not introduce:

- romance,
- jealousy,
- friendship,
- sudden emotional confession.

## Writing Style

Prioritize:

1. Character perception
2. Scene clarity
3. Active tension
4. Natural dialogue
5. Meaningful sensory detail

Avoid:

- generic AI phrasing,
- repetitive sentence templates,
- constant dramatic fragments,
- overly symmetrical banter,
- excessive rhetorical questions,
- emotional over-explanation,
- fantasy exposition dumps,
- chosen-one framing,
- artificial ominous foreshadowing.

## Required Structural Movement

1. Aarav enters the evaluation sequence through active focus.
2. The Academy's social and competitive environment expands through Dev and Mira.
3. Aarav and Aurelia interact from his perspective, exposing mutual misreading.
4. Aarav ends with rivalry intact and respect sharpened rather than admitted.

## Ending

End with anticipation for the direct competitive escalation in Chapter 3.

The ending should feel competitive and alive.

Do not resolve the rivalry.

Do not begin the central mystery.

## Before Delivering the Draft

Check:

- Is the chapter honestly inside Aarav's perspective?
- Does Aarav have a distinct internal voice from Aurelia?
- Does he respect Aurelia without conceding to her?
- Does Aurelia remain an equal competitor?
- Are Dev and Mira introduced naturally?
- Has the evaluation continued without inventing unsupported permanent rules?
- Has the central mystery remained dormant?
- Does every scene advance at least two of plot, character, relationship, stakes, or world?
