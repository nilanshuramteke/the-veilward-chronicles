# Chapter 2 Canon Constraints

## POV and Information Control

- Third-person limited.
- Past tense.
- Aarav is the only viewpoint character.
- No access to Aurelia's private thoughts.
- Aarav may infer, but inference must remain inference.
- Do not give Aarav knowledge of Aurelia's private Chapter 1 flame instability.

## Timeline

- Aurelia and Aarav already knew each other before Book One.
- Their rivalry predates Book One.
- They are already students at Veilward Academy.
- Chapter 2 follows the evaluation threshold established at the end of Chapter 1.

Do not write:

- their first meeting,
- the beginning of their rivalry,
- arrival at Veilward.

## Relationship State

Current state:

**Rivals**

Allowed:

- competition,
- irritation,
- assumptions,
- conflicting methods,
- recognition of competence,
- partial misunderstanding.

Not allowed:

- friendship milestone,
- deep mutual vulnerability,
- emotional intimacy,
- romance,
- jealousy framing.

## Aarav

Aarav must remain:

- observant,
- controlled,
- intelligent,
- emotionally measured,
- independently meaningful.

His measured approach must not become passivity.

His restraint must not make him emotionally empty.

Do not position him as secretly superior to Aurelia.

## Aurelia

From Aarav's observable perspective, Aurelia should remain:

- capable,
- competitive,
- proud,
- energetic.

Aarav may misinterpret her.

The narration must not confirm his interpretation as objective truth unless the evidence supports it.

## Dev and Mira

### Dev
- Student at Veilward.
- Socially grounded.
- Provides meaningful perspective or pressure when present.
- Does not know protected information without believable involvement.

### Mira
- Student at Veilward.
- Perceptive and socially aware.
- Observations must have an observable basis.
- Must not become an unexplained reader of private emotions.

## Magic

Follow:

`08_BOOKS/BOOK_01/04_WORLD_AND_LOCATIONS/book-01-magic-usage-lock.md`

Chapters 1–3 allow:

- controlled student magic,
- species abilities,
- competitive training,
- supervised transformation where appropriate.

Do not introduce:

- unexplained advanced mastery,
- major prophecy,
- Serpentfire activation,
- a new combined Phoenix–Naga power.

Aarav's perception is useful but not omniscient.

Important conclusions require evidence.

## Academy Evaluation

The evaluation framework may be dramatized through existing Academy and Book One context.

Do not casually establish permanent new rules, rankings, titles, traditions, or scoring systems unless supported by existing canon.

If an evaluation detail is needed for a scene, keep it local and non-canonizing unless verified.

## Mystery Protection

Do not reveal or explicitly foreshadow:

- The Echo,
- The Impossible Breach,
- the recurring symbol,
- Serpentfire,
- the First Ember,
- Elyra,
- Varun,
- Outer Dark information,
- the hidden chamber.

No ominous language should be inserted merely to signal that something bad will happen later.

## Supporting Cast Protection

Dev and Mira have intentionally open areas of characterization.

Do not permanently invent:

- species,
- magical specialization,
- family history,
- detailed ambitions,
- deep backstory,

unless existing canon explicitly establishes them.

## Information Honesty

Remain honestly inside Aarav's perspective.

Do not hide thoughts Aarav genuinely has merely to manufacture mystery.

At the same time, do not turn every internal thought into exposition.
