# Chapter 18 → Chapter 19 Continuity Handoff

## Handoff Status
**Source:** Chapter 18 — The Echo Speaks Wrong
**Source POV:** Aarav
**Destination:** Chapter 19 — The Names in Stone
**Destination POV:** Aurelia (per the Book 1 chapter outline's alternation rule — odd chapters = Aurelia)
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

Chapter 18 is finalized. This document is the authoritative continuity input for Chapter 19. It does not decide Chapter 19's scenes, engine, or opening — only the state it inherits.

## 1. Immediate End-State

At the end of Chapter 18, on the same evening as the events below:

- Aurelia heard a voice in the east passage, between the east stair and the kitchen yard, two-thirds along and before the linen door. It began to move toward someone, slid, moved toward something else, and stopped being a voice. She could not name who it was reaching for and heard no words ("there were places where words would go"). She brought this account to Aarav first, unprompted, and told it plainly, gaps included.
- Aarav took her account seriously without treating it as proof. They ran limited mundane checks: a look at the passage, one question to a kitchen porter, a courtyard voice test, and the pipes. None explained it.
- While standing in the passage, they both experienced a second manifestation. Aarav experienced it directly. It came from the dark ahead, in something like Aurelia's own register, reached for the rise before words, found nothing where the words should be, slid into a second register that also failed to hold, and thinned to nothing. No words, name or place.
- They walked the quad and decided **not to report the event to Voss that night** and not to use the Outer Dark heading as an explanation. They agreed a rule: if it happens again, or if anything survives a question, "he hears it that day."
- Aarav's attention went to Aurelia before it went to the sound. He understood the order only afterward, and ended the chapter with an unresolved private question about it. Aurelia turned her head at the end of the manifestation and found him already looking at the passage. Whether she noticed anything is not established.
- Mira confronted them in the dining hall about being kept outside a secret ("I'm asking why I'm being handled"), then let them go. Her grievance is not resolved.

Chapter 19 begins after this unresolved experience. It must not restage the account, the checks, the second manifestation, or the reporting decision.

## 2. Knowledge Handoff

### They Know
- A voice-like phenomenon occurred twice in the east passage on the same evening, once for each of them.
- It imitates a recognizable register and fails to become anyone or say anything ("almost someone").
- The pipes, the courtyard, the linen door and the porter's account do not explain it. The passage was empty and nothing was found. The two of them came and went, and the porter saw only them.
- The Outer Dark heading in the index reads "Dormant" and has nothing written under it. Neither of them has enough to connect it to tonight, or to call it unrelated.
- They have agreed not to bring it to Voss yet, and on the conditions under which they will.
- Aurelia's evaluation is about ten days away.

### They Reasonably Suspect
- The phenomenon may be responsive to them. This rests on timing and on its direction toward Aarav's side of the passage, and it is unproven.

### Aarav Alone Knows
- The passage privately reminded him of the Chapter 8 imitation incident: "something reaching for a person and missing", "the same kind of wrong… and not the same thing". He did not say this aloud and did not make it a theory.
- His attention went to Aurelia first. He has not named it, and has said nothing about it to her.

### They Do Not Know
- What the phenomenon is, where it comes from, what it wants, or whether it understands them.
- Whether it is related to the Chapter 8 incident, the Chapter 4 anomaly or the Outer Dark.
- Anything about the fourth stone's difference from its neighbors, SERPENTFIRE's meaning, the Chapter 12 Unknown Student or the Chapter 13 annex marks beyond what Chapter 17 already established.
- The true nature of the Outer Dark, the Seeker, the First Ember, Elyra, Varun, the true Veil origin or mechanics, the true cause of the Chapter 4 anomaly, or any breach nature.

Chapter 19 must not treat any item in this last category as settled.

## 3. Reader State

The reader knows:

- Aurelia experienced the first imitation, and Aarav the second.
- The phenomenon can imitate recognizable registers but gets something fundamentally wrong.
- It may respond to their presence.
- Its identity remains unknown.
- Outer Dark remains an unresolved possibility, not an explanation.
- Aarav's attention moved to Aurelia before it moved to the phenomenon, and he does not know why.

## 4. Character Handoff

### Aurelia
Direct, physically confident, independently motivated. She came to Aarav first ("I'd rather be wrong out loud"), volunteered the flaws in her own account, offered the courtyard alternative, and made her own decision about not reporting ("That's my decision. I'd rather bring him something I can stand behind"). Entering Chapter 19 (her own POV chapter), she carries a first-hand, unverifiable experience, the agreement with Aarav, and her evaluation in about ten days. She knows Aarav took her account seriously. She has no knowledge of his private Chapter 8 comparison or of where his attention went.

### Aarav
Analytical, verification-first. He met evidence that resists his method, kept restraint rather than certainty, and finished with "smaller and more honest" competence. He carries an unresolved private question about what steadied him. He has not spoken to Aurelia about it.

### Mira
Ordinary, perceptive, and hurt in an unresolved but non-dramatic way. She reads people from behavior alone, has no mystery knowledge, and is not connected to the Echo. She said she would not follow them, and gave up asking "what" a fortnight before. Chapter 19 may leave her offstage.

### Voss
Offstage in Chapter 18. He has not been told. His Chapter 16 terms (anything that becomes an inquiry goes through him) stand, and Aarav treated the night's checking as a look at a public passage, not an inquiry.

### Relationship
**Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation, at the edge of Stage 3.** Trust has strengthened through shared uncertainty. **No confession, declaration, mutual romance, friendship-labeled status or physical intimacy should be assumed entering Chapter 19.** The Scene 4 attention beat is Aarav's, private, and unlabeled.
