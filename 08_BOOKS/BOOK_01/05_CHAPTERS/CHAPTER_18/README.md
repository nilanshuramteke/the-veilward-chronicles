## Purpose

This package documents the complete, approved Chapter 18 planning process: every approved planning and calibration decision, consolidated into a single, implementation-ready set of drafting instructions for the downstream Phase 3 Full Manuscript Draft step.

## Chapter Function

Chapter 18 converts the mystery from something Aarav and Aurelia investigate into something that appears to notice, and respond to, the fact that they are investigating it — without confirming what it is. Aurelia experiences an imitation-voice phenomenon alone, unable to sustain or complete a recognizable identity, and brings the raw, unverifiable account to Aarav, reversing Chapter 17's information flow. His verification instinct is tested and exhausted; their return to the site draws a second, stronger instance, directed at him through a voice-shape resembling Aurelia's, which dissolves before completing whatever it was reaching for. In the instant it begins, Aarav's attention moves to Aurelia before it moves to the phenomenon — an involuntary beat that crosses the relationship from warming into unmistakably attraction-coded territory, without either character naming it. Mira returns with a genuine, independent grievance about being kept outside their secret, entirely apart from the mystery. The chapter closes on a shared, reasoned decision not to report tonight's events to Voss, and on Aarav's unresolved private question about where his own attention went, and why.
