# Chapter 18 — Canon Constraints

> **Authority:** Binding for the Phase 3 Full Manuscript Draft. Consolidates Phase 1 Orientation, Phase 2 Detailed Scene Plan, Phase 2.1 Targeted Calibration, and Phase 3.1 Targeted Drafting Calibration. Where any earlier Chapter 18 document conflicts with this one, this one governs.

## POV

**Aarav — close third person, past tense.** Locked by the Book 1 chapter outline's alternation rule (even chapters = Aarav) and reaffirmed through every planning phase. Chapter 18 is Aurelia's alternating counterpart chapter to Chapter 17.

- Full Aarav interiority is permitted and expected — his discomfort at having no honest answer for Mira in Scene 1, his assessment of Aurelia's credibility in Scene 2, his method running out of road in Scene 3, his involuntary attention to Aurelia in Scene 4, and his private, unresolved self-questioning in Scene 5 are all required interiority beats.
- Everything about Aurelia, Mira, and Dev (if present) must be inferred through their speech, behavior, physical action, and Aarav's interpretation. **No Aurelia interiority. No Mira interiority. No Dev interiority.**
- Aurelia's first-hand Echo experience (Scene 2's antecedent event) must never be dramatized directly. It reaches the reader only through her account and Aarav's reading of her while she gives it.
- No omniscient narration may confirm, deny, or explain what the Echo is, what it wants, whether it can access memory, or whether Outer Dark relates to it.

## Must Preserve (Established Canon/Continuity)

- The Chapter 17 exit state in full (see `chapter-17-continuity-input.md`).
- Aurelia's Chapter 17 choice to remain on the index research, made voluntarily, at a real cost, unvindicated by anything since.
- General Archive access remains suspended, unresolved, since Chapter 12.
- The narrow, Voss-supervised, structure-only authorization to examine the old classification system's index (granted end of Chapter 16, exercised in Chapter 17) — unchanged in scope.
- The "Outer Dark — dormant/inactive" heading (Chapter 16): registered, unexplained, unconnected to anything, per the Revelation Matrix's Book One status ("distant implication" only).
- Chapter 8's established precedent: an imitation phenomenon that can reproduce cadence, subject, and recognizable behavioral pattern while making a decisive, revealing error. Chapter 18 may echo this *pattern*, never restate or re-stage the Chapter 8 scene itself.
- Relationship state: Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation, at the beginning edge of Stage 3 — earned but unsettled Respect, with one demonstrated act of Trust (Chapter 17, Aurelia's).
- Mira (`04_CHARACTERS/mira.md`): last on-page in Chapter 14; perceptive, socially aware, independent; must never become an accidental repository for protagonist secrets.
- Dev (`04_CHARACTERS/dev.md`): an ordinary student with no ongoing investigation role.
- The Chapter 11 fourth stone, the Angular Mark, SERPENTFIRE, the Chapter 12 Unknown Student, the Chapter 13 annex marks, and the Chapter 14–15 corridor/intruder theory (retired) all remain exactly as Chapter 17 left them — untouched, unmentioned, unconnected to anything in this chapter.

## Must Not Reveal

- What the Echo is, its identity, origin, motive, or intelligence.
- Whether the voice phenomenon can access memory, thought, or private history.
- Any connection between tonight's events and the hidden chamber, the Seeker, the Harbinger, the First Ember, Elyra, Varun, the Lost Path, the true Veil origin or mechanics, or true breach theory.
- Whether the Outer Dark classification relates to the Echo in any way.
- Anything about the Chapter 4 anomaly's true cause.

## Must Not Imply

- That the Echo is definitely the same entity as the Chapter 4–8 phenomenon — only that it belongs to the same recognizable category of wrongness.
- That the Echo definitely drew on real memories, private conversation, or surveillance of any specific character or scene.
- That "Outer Dark" is definitely unrelated to tonight's events — the correct epistemic position is insufficient evidence to connect them, never a ruling-out.
- That Mira's Scene 1 material is the literal source the Echo reproduces later — the two must remain causally unconnected.
- That the Chapter 11 fourth stone, the Angular Mark, SERPENTFIRE, or any other cold thread is active, relevant, or referenced tonight.
- That Aarav and Aurelia's decision not to report tonight to Voss is casual, rebellious, or plot-convenient rather than a considered, character-consistent choice.
- That the relationship has advanced to Friendship, confession, or named romance.

## Character Constraints

### Aarav
- Analytical, verification-first, hedging deliberately — consistent with Chapters 8–17.
- His Chapter 18 arc is pressure, not collapse: his method produces only negative results this chapter (what didn't happen, what can't explain it), and he must accept that without prematurely constructing a theory.
- Must explicitly avoid concluding that the Echo reads memory, that Outer Dark explains anything, or that tonight's events resemble Chapter 14's intruder theory in any way.
- His attention moving to Aurelia before the phenomenon, in Scene 4, must remain involuntary and unlabeled — he does not think the words "attraction" or equivalent.
- His reasoning for not reporting to Voss must be legible on the page as his own, evidence-grounded judgment, not authorial convenience.

### Aurelia
- Direct, composed, independently motivated — consistent with Chapters 1–17.
- Originates the chapter's central experience entirely alone, and discloses it to Aarav immediately and without prompting — this is her agency, not a plot device delivering information to him.
- Remains perceptive and capable of challenge throughout; must not be reduced to a frightened dependent or a passive presence Aarav manages.
- Does not originate or lead any part of the Outer Dark reasoning or the reporting decision alone — both are reached jointly, with Aarav voicing the reasoning.

### Mira
- Independent, socially aware, has her own unresolved grievance (being kept outside Aarav and Aurelia's secret) — this is her scene's real content.
- Possesses zero knowledge of the index research, SERPENTFIRE, the Echo, or any protected material, at any point.
- Nothing she says or does in Scene 1 may be mechanically reused, copied, or echoed by the Echo later — the connection between her scene and the Echo's wrongness is thematic and structural only, never causal or textual.

### Dev
- Optional. If included, present only for ordinary, non-investigative reasons; must not be secretly knowledgeable, must not repeat the Chapter 17 comic-consequence mechanism, and must not be granted archival, investigative, or supernatural knowledge.

## Relationship Constraints

**Current state:** Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation, at the beginning edge of Stage 3 — earned but unsettled Respect (carried forward from Chapter 17).

**Chapter 18 movement:** Attraction becomes unmistakable to the reader through Aarav's involuntary attention to Aurelia in Scene 4 — his focus reaches her before it reaches the phenomenon, and he registers this only in retrospect. This must be demonstrated entirely through behavior and perception, never through declarative naming, confession, or physical rescue staging.

Do not advance to:
- established Friendship, Trust-declaration as dialogue, emotional-intimacy breakthrough,
- a named romantic relationship, confession, or kiss,
- protective-physical-rescue behavior as the mechanism for the attraction beat.

Exactly one romance-coded beat is permitted, in Scene 4: an involuntary shift of attention, brief, unnamed by Aarav even internally. It must not use touch-as-rescue, shielding, grabbing, or any physical-protection action as its trigger. Proximity carried over from Scene 3's joint verification is sufficient physical context; no new protective gesture should be introduced.

## Magic Constraints

- No new magical instrument, measurement, terminology, or formal experiment.
- Aurelia's Phoenix fire is not required or expected this chapter and should not be invoked merely to echo Chapter 17's structure.
- Aarav's Naga sensitivity, if referenced at all, must remain exactly within its previously established scope (per `04_CHARACTERS/aarav.md` and prior chapters) and must not be upgraded into a new detection ability aimed at the Echo specifically.
- The Echo's phenomenon is not classified, measured, or explained through any magical framework this chapter.

## Structural Constraints

- Exactly five scenes: The Impression / She Arrived Already Changed / What Can Be Checked / Not Quite Her / What They Don't Say Yet.
- POV locked to Aarav throughout, with no direct dramatization of Aurelia's Scene 2 antecedent experience.
- The Echo appears exactly twice (reported once, directly once); no third instance.
- Scene 5 must end the chapter on Aarav's unresolved private realization — not a new clue, an explanation, or a cliffhanger.

## Superseded / Rejected Design Elements — Do Not Use

- A standalone Aurelia-POV scene dramatizing her first Echo experience directly (rejected at Phase 2.1 — violates absolute POV lock; her experience must reach the reader only through disclosure).
- The Echo reproducing Mira's specific Scene 1 material (cadence, phrase, or impression) as its later imitation source (rejected at Phase 2.1 — implies an unestablished surveillance capability and reduces Mira to a plot mechanism).
- A completed, nameable Echo fragment in Scene 4 — e.g., a resolved quotation, name, or location (rejected at Phase 3.1 — collapses the deliberate memory-access ambiguity).
- Aarav concluding definitively that the Echo reads memory, or that Outer Dark is unrelated to tonight's events (both rejected — the correct epistemic position in each case is insufficient evidence, not a verdict).
- Any staging of the Echo encounters at or near the Chapter 11 fourth stone / Training Grounds (rejected at Phase 2.1 — relocated to an unconnected ordinary passage to eliminate any fourth-stone proximity risk).
- A rescue-trope romance mechanism (grabbing, shielding, physical protection triggering the attraction beat) (rejected at Phase 3.1 — replaced with involuntary attention as the sole mechanism).
- A sixth scene, a new mystery object/symbol/person, or any forced convergence of Outer Dark, SERPENTFIRE, the Angular Mark, the fourth stone, the annex marks, or the Unknown Student with the Echo thread (all rejected).
- Ending the chapter on physical departure/walk-away action rather than Aarav's internal, unresolved realization (rejected at Phase 2.1).
