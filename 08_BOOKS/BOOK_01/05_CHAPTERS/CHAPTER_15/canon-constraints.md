# Chapter 15 — Canon Constraints

> **Authority:** This document is binding for Phase 4 Drafting. It consolidates Phase 1 Orientation, Phase 2 Detailed Plan, Phase 2 Targeted Revision, Phase 3.1 Targeted Calibration (TC-1 through TC-4), and Phase 3.2 Final Calibration. Where any earlier Chapter 15 document conflicts with this one, this one governs.

## POV

**Aurelia — close third person, past tense.** Locked by the Book 1 chapter outline and reaffirmed through every planning phase. Chapter 15 is Aarav's alternating counterpart chapter to Chapter 14, and the confirmed Book 1 midpoint.

- Full Aurelia interiority is permitted and expected — her readiness with nowhere to land (Scene 1), her instinct-versus-evidence destabilization (Scene 4), and her calibrated realization are all required interiority beats.
- Everything about Aarav must be inferred through his speech, behavior, physical action, and Aurelia's interpretation. No Aarav interiority.
- No omniscient narration may confirm, deny, or explain the mystery beyond what Aurelia herself can observe or reason.

## Outline vs. Locked Evidentiary Boundary (Flagged, Not Silently Resolved)

`chapter-outline.md`'s two-line Chapter 15 summary states: "The protagonists discover historical evidence proving the anomaly existed inside Veilward long before the current incident. The breach theory collapses." This is high-level shorthand written before detailed planning. Phases 2 through 3.2 deliberately refined this into a more precise, non-overclaiming formulation, per explicit user instruction: the historical evidence proves **no external entry was established** in a materially comparable past case; it does **not** prove the current anomaly's origin is internal. **"The breach theory collapses" is preserved exactly** — the external-entry theory is invalidated — but "proving the anomaly existed inside Veilward" is treated as the outline's loose shorthand for that collapse, not as a separate, stronger claim to be dramatized literally. This refinement is consistent with the outline's core beat (breach theory collapses) and does not contradict it; it is flagged here as an authored precision, not a silent substitution.

## Must Not Reveal

- Whether the historical disturbance and the current incident are the same phenomenon.
- Who reclassified the Chapter 10 record after its original filing, or why.
- SERPENTFIRE's meaning, origin, or history.

- The Chapter 11 marker's or Chapter 13 annex marks' true explanation.
- Any protected lore (see Section 10 of the Drafting Brief).

## Must Not Imply

- That "no external entry was established" (historical, provable within the record's scope) is the same as "the phenomenon originated inside Veilward" (never established, must never be stated as fact).
- That the historical disturbance and the current anomaly are identical — only "materially comparable."
- That all four mystery threads (marker, Unknown Student, annex marks, corridor) share one common mechanism or origin.
- Agency, intelligence, motive, or entity behavior behind the historical or current disturbance.
- Any new magical mechanism, instrument, measurement, or diagnostic capability for either Aurelia's fire or Aarav's Naga sensitivity.

## Character Constraints

### Aurelia
- Direct, physically confident, independently motivated — consistent with Chapters 1–14.
- Arc: instinct → correct objection (carried from Ch14) → discovery → destabilization → recalibration. Her key realization is **"rejecting the wrong explanation does not tell me the right one"** — not "I was right all along."
- Behavioral expression required: a physical half-motion toward the doorway in Scene 4, stopped short — instinct with nowhere to aim.
- Must not become passive because the evidence is historical/documentary rather than physical danger.

### Aarav
- Analytical, verification-first, institutionally aware — consistent with Chapters 8–14.
- His Chapter 15 growth must be a **distinct extension** of his Chapter 14 blind-spot recognition (institutional authority's conclusions) into a **new category**: historical/documentary records carry their own evidentiary limits, separate from living authority. He self-corrects an initial misreading of "no point of ingress established" (first treating it as a settled negative, then catching that period administrative language may only mean "not thoroughly investigated") — **unprompted, without Aurelia's involvement.**
- Must not repeat Chapter 14's exact "believed an institutional conclusion too readily, caught by Aurelia's question" structure. This time he catches himself.

## Relationship Constraints

**Current state:** Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation, beginning edge of Stage 3 — Respect (carried forward from Chapter 14).

**Chapter 15 movement:** Earned, unsettled Respect — demonstrated through a shared, argument-free realization (Scene 4) and a collaborative physical act (Scene 5 transcription, Aurelia checking rather than directing). Rivalry remains alive underneath.

Do not advance to:
- Trust, Friendship, or emotional intimacy,
- romance, confession, or romantic subtext of any kind,
- protective-romantic behavior.

**Proximity-pattern lock:** the Scene 3 practical proximity beat (Aurelia holding flame steady for Aarav's reading) is functional only, driven by physical necessity, not romance. It must not trigger the four-chapter (Ch11–14) proximity/unnamed-feeling/non-naming construction. Ordinary distance resumes afterward without comment or lingering.

## Magic Constraints

- Aurelia's Phoenix fire: practical illumination only, non-diagnostic, on-page.
- Aarav's Naga sensitivity: **not invoked in this chapter.** The classification-mark recognition beat (Scene 2) is ordinary institutional literacy, not a magical ability.
- No new magical instrument, measurement, formal experiment, or repeatable detection procedure may be introduced.

## Structural Constraints

- Exactly five scenes: Threshold / Records / Evidence / It Never Entered (midpoint) / What Veilward Forgot (consequence).
- POV locked to Aurelia throughout.
- The historical evidence must arrive in **two separate physical discovery beats** (main record fragment in Scene 3; a loose investigator's slip found only via a more thorough search opening Scene 4) — not one continuous, convenient document.
- The "comparable, not identical" caveat must open Scene 4, after a full scene break from Scene 3 — not land in the same beat as the initial comparison.
- Scene 5 must be a concrete physical act (hand transcription, Aurelia witnessing against the original) — not a conversation ending in a stated intention to "tell Voss."
- The original document/artifact must remain in the chamber — no unauthorized removal of restricted material.

## Superseded / Rejected Design Elements — Do Not Use

- The historical evidence as a single, continuous, convenient document (superseded by: two separate discovery beats — TC-1).
- The "comparable ≠ identical" caveat landing in the same beat as the initial Ch4 comparison (superseded by: caveat opens Scene 4 — TC-2).
- Aarav's evidentiary correction being Aurelia-triggered (superseded by: a genuine, self-corrected misreading, distinct in register from his Chapter 14 beat — TC-3).
- Scene 5 as "they decide to tell Voss" (superseded by: a physical transcription act with the original left in place — TC-4).
- Framing the midpoint as "the anomaly's origin is proven internal" (superseded by: "no external entry was established," a strictly bounded historical finding).
- Establishing all four mystery threads as manifestations of one phenomenon (superseded by: only the Ch14 corridor/breach-assumption thread is touched; Ch11/12/13 remain fully independent).
