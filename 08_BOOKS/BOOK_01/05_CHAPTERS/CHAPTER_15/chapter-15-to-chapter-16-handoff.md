# Chapter 15 → Chapter 16 Continuity Handoff

## Handoff Status
**Source:** Chapter 15 — It Never Entered
**Source POV:** Aurelia
**Destination:** Chapter 16
**Status:** **AUTHORITATIVE**

Chapter 15 is finalized. This document is the authoritative continuity input for Chapter 16. Per the Book 1 chapter outline, Chapter 16 ("Dormant Things") is Aarav's alternating counterpart chapter. This handoff does not decide Chapter 16's scenes, engine, or opening — only the state it inherits.

## 1. Immediate End-State

At the end of Chapter 15:

- Aurelia and Aarav crossed the threshold Chapter 14 left them at and found a small, decommissioned storage chamber — old restricted-holdings infrastructure, physically a dead end, not a passage to anywhere external.
- A physical search located a corroded record chest containing a single damaged file, its classification mark identified as belonging to a superseded records system predating the Archive's current organization.
- The file's mark was read as "Fourteen-C" and tied, by Aarav's own hedged confidence ("consistent with it," not certainty), to the sealed supplement Chapter 10 referenced and could not access.
- A main record fragment, read by Aurelia's held-close flame, established: a historical, witnessed disturbance, generations old, which the boundary wards failed to register — materially comparable to the Chapter 4 anomaly, explicitly not identical to it.
- A second, separate, loose investigator's slip was found in the same chest. It states that no point of ingress was established for the historical case. Aarav initially read this as a settled negative, then caught his own misreading unprompted: the phrase may only mean the historical investigation never fully established one, not that ingress was confirmed absent.
- Both protagonists recognized, together and without disagreement, that the external-entry assumption underlying the entire current investigation was never actually validated — historically, or now.
- The original file was too fragile to remove. Aarav transcribed its legible contents by hand; Aurelia checked each line against the original. The original was returned to the chest and left in the chamber. They left with the transcription.

Chapter 16 begins from this state. It must not restage the threshold crossing, the search, the reading of either document, or the transcription itself.

## 2. Knowledge Handoff

### They Know
- A materially comparable, ward-evading disturbance occurred at Veilward generations before the current incident.
- The historical case never established a point of external ingress.
- The current investigation's working assumption (something entered Veilward from outside through forgotten infrastructure) has lost its evidentiary foundation.
- The recovered file's classification mark is consistent with the "ref. 14-C" supplement Chapter 10 could not access — a superseded restricted-holdings system, never fully migrated when the Archive reorganized.
- The old chamber is a dead end: it is not, and was never, a route to anywhere outside the Academy.

### They Reasonably Suspect
- That Veilward's own institutional record-keeping may hold further information relevant to the current anomaly that has been lost, damaged, or never migrated.
- That the "no ingress established" finding may reflect an incomplete historical investigation rather than a confirmed absence — this is Aarav's own stated reservation, not a settled fact.
- That the investigation's productive direction now lies with Academy history and memory rather than with the external-entry theory.

### They Do Not Know
- The current anomaly's true nature, cause, mechanism, or agency.
- Whether the historical disturbance and the current anomaly are the same phenomenon.
- Who reclassified the original Chapter 10 general record after its filing, or why.
- Anything about the Ch11 angular marker's cause.
- The Ch12 Unknown Student's identity, species, motive, or affiliation.
- The cause of the Ch13 annex marks.
- What SERPENTFIRE means.
- Whether any of the four accumulated mystery threads relate to one another beyond the institution's own unconfirmed Chapter 14 theory.

Chapter 16 must not treat any item in this last category as settled.
