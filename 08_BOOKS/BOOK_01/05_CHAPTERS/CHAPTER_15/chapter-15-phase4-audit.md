## 20. Required Corrections

### CA-01
- **Severity:** Medium
- **Location:** Scene 3, "It put her shoulder against his, the only way to hold the angle right, and neither of them commented on it. There wasn't room to do otherwise..."
- **Problem:** "and neither of them commented on it" is functionally identical to the explicitly prohibited proximity-avoidance construction ("neither mentioned it") named in the governing Chapter 15 drafting brief and this audit's own final rule.
- **Why it matters:** This chapter's entire design point for Scene 3 is to break the four-chapter (Ch11–14) proximity-avoidance pattern by making the proximity purely functional and unremarked *in substance* — not by naming the absence of remark, which reintroduces the pattern at the sentence level even while the scene's intent is to avoid it.
- **Required correction:** Remove the clause "and neither of them commented on it." The surrounding sentence ("There wasn't room to do otherwise, and the paper wouldn't survive being moved to somewhere more comfortable") already carries the functional justification without needing to name the non-reaction.
- **Relevant lock:** `canon-constraints.md` Proximity-pattern lock; Phase 4 manuscript-draft brief, Section 13 ("Do not write... 'neither mentioned it'... This is a pattern the chapter is deliberately breaking").
- **Affects:** Prose, relationship.

### CA-02
- **Severity:** Low-Medium
- **Location:** Lines "Aarav turned it toward the light and went still" (Scene 2), "Aarav went still in the particular way he did..." (Scene 2), "Aurelia went very still" (Scene 3).
- **Problem:** "went still" is used three times within roughly 30 lines as the reaction-beat shorthand for a realization/surprise moment, for both POV-adjacent characters.
- **Why it matters:** Noticeable patterning risks reading as a tic rather than a deliberate choice, per the prose watchlist's caution against "repetitive... habitual descriptors."
- **Required correction:** Vary at least one of the three instances (recommend the second, Aarav's Ch10-payoff recognition beat) with a different physical or behavioral marker of the same realization.
- **Relevant lock:** `drafting-brief.md` Prose Watchlist.
- **Affects:** Prose only.

### CA-03
- **Severity:** Low
- **Location:** Scene 3 opening: "The pages inside were too brittle to hold up to the room's general dark, and the only light either of them had was hers."
- **Problem:** Mixed metaphor — brittleness (physical fragility) is conflated with tolerating darkness (a visibility concept). The intended meaning (the pages required concentrated, careful light, not the room's ambient dim) doesn't fully parse as written.
- **Why it matters:** Minor clarity issue at a scene-opening sentence, worth a clean pass.
- **Required correction:** Rephrase to separate the two ideas — e.g., something to the effect that the pages needed careful, close light rather than the room's general dark, without conflating fragility with light tolerance.
- **Relevant lock:** None (pure prose clarity).
- **Affects:** Prose only.

### CA-04 (optional, not required)
- **Severity:** Low
- **Location:** Scene 4: "It's the same gap Voss's corridor left us with, and I still nearly walked past it."
- **Problem:** Explicitly names the Chapter 14 parallel. Not a violation of TC-3 (vocabulary and rhythm remain distinct from the Chapter 14 line), but it is the single place where the manuscript states the continuity connection outright rather than leaving it for the reader to recognize.
- **Why it matters:** A softer touch here would let the parallel land as something the reader notices rather than something Aarav announces, consistent with the chapter's general discipline of constructing realizations from evidence rather than stating them.
- **Suggested correction (optional):** Consider trimming to the self-contained observation about the document alone, without the explicit "same gap... Voss's corridor" cross-reference, if a future pass wants a lighter touch. Not required for finalization.
- **Relevant lock:** None (style preference).
- **Affects:** Prose only.
