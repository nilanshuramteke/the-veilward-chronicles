## Chapter Function

Chapter 15 is the Book 1 midpoint. Aurelia and Aarav cross the threshold Chapter 14 left them at and find a decommissioned restricted-holdings annex — the physical remnant of an earlier, superseded iteration of Veilward's own records system. Inside, a damaged historical record and a separate investigator's slip together establish that a materially comparable, ward-evading disturbance occurred generations ago, and that the historical case never established an external point of ingress either. Both protagonists recognize that the external-entry assumption driving the entire investigation was never actually validated — not proof that the current anomaly originated inside Veilward, only proof that the outside-intruder theory is no longer sufficient. Unable to remove the fragile original, they transcribe it by hand together before leaving, carrying a reframed investigative question into Act II-B: what has Veilward forgotten?

Locked progression:

**Threshold crossed → forgotten records found → historical evidence read → midpoint reversal constructed from evidence → concrete, physical changed condition.**

The chapter does not explain the current anomaly, confirm or deny an internal origin, or resolve any of the three other mystery threads. It advances the mystery from "an institutional theory that an intruder used forgotten infrastructure" (Chapter 14) to "that theory has lost its evidentiary foundation, and Veilward's own forgotten history is now implicated."

## Final Evidence State

### Established
- A historical, unexplained disturbance occurred generations ago, sharing the ward-failure trait with the Chapter 4 anomaly.
- That historical event is materially comparable to the current situation — not identical.
- The historical investigation did not establish a point of ingress, though this finding's own strength is limited (an administrative closure, not necessarily a thorough search).
- The current external-entry theory therefore lacks sufficient evidentiary validation.

### Reasonable inference
- The forgotten annex/decommissioned infrastructure may not be the current phenomenon's origin.
- Veilward's own institutional memory may hold information the current system has lost or never fully migrated.
- The investigation's working direction should shift away from assuming an external intruder.

### Not established
- Internal origin of the current anomaly.
- The current anomaly's true cause, nature, mechanism, or agency.
- Any motive or intelligence behind either the historical or current event.
- Whether the historical and current events are the same phenomenon.
- Who reclassified the Chapter 10 general record after its original filing, or why.
- Any connection to the Ch11 marker, the Ch12 Unknown Student, or the Ch13 annex marks.
