## Purpose

This package documents the complete, finalized Chapter 15 process: every approved planning and calibration decision, the full Phase 4–6.3 correction and verification history, and the final narrative/continuity state, consolidated into a single authoritative record.

## Chapter Function

Chapter 15 is the Book 1 midpoint. Aurelia and Aarav cross the threshold Chapter 14 left them at and find, not an external passage, but a decommissioned restricted-holdings annex — the physical remnant of an earlier iteration of Veilward's own records system. Inside, a damaged historical record and a loose investigator's slip together establish that a materially comparable, unexplained disturbance occurred generations ago, and that the historical case never established external entry either. Both protagonists recognize that the external-entry assumption driving the entire investigation was never actually validated — not proof that the current anomaly originated inside Veilward, only proof that the assumption of an outside intruder is no longer sufficient. Unable to remove the fragile original, they transcribe it by hand together before leaving, carrying a reframed investigative question into Act II-B: what has Veilward forgotten?
