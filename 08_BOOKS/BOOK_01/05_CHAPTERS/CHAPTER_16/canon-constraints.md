# Chapter 16 — Canon Constraints

> **Authority:** Binding for Phase 4 Drafting. Consolidates Phase 1 Orientation, Phase 2 Detailed Scene Plan, Phase 2.1 Targeted Correction, Phase 3.1 Targeted Calibration, and Phase 3.2 Final Calibration. Where any earlier Chapter 16 document conflicts with this one, this one governs.

## POV

**Aarav — close third person, past tense.** Locked by the Book 1 chapter outline ("Chapter 16 — Dormant Things," POV: Aarav) and reaffirmed through every planning phase. Chapter 16 is Aurelia's alternating counterpart chapter to Chapter 15.

- Full Aarav interiority is permitted and expected — his read of Aurelia's escalating posture in Scene 2, his private disagreement with her timing in Scene 3, his judgment about risk versus her agency, are all required interiority beats.
- Everything about Aurelia, Lyra, the instructor, and Voss must be inferred through their speech, behavior, physical action, and Aarav's interpretation. **No Aurelia interiority. No Lyra interiority. No Voss interiority.**
- No omniscient narration may confirm, deny, or explain the mystery, the Outer Dark heading's significance, or Aurelia's factual correctness.

## Must Preserve (Established Canon/Continuity)

- The Chapter 15 exit state in full (see `chapter-15-continuity-input.md`).
- General Archive access remains suspended, unresolved, since Chapter 12.
- The Ch11 marker, Ch12 Unknown Student, and Ch13 annex marks remain unresolved and untouched.
- SERPENTFIRE remains unexplained and is not named or referenced in any form.
- Aarav's Chapter 15 transcription remains privately in his possession, undisclosed until this chapter's report reaches Voss through the incident itself — not through voluntary disclosure of the transcription's contents.
- Voss remains a competent, procedural institutional actor whose authority is scoped specifically to his existing oversight of Aurelia and Aarav's arrangement — not general Academy policy, discipline, or the Archive broadly.
- Lyra (`04_CHARACTERS/lyra.md`): competitive, capable, motivated by her own goals; knowledge boundary strictly limited to what her role and direct experience justify — no access to protagonist secrets.

## Must Not Reveal

- The true nature, cause, or mechanism of the current anomaly or the Chapter 15 historical disturbance.
- Whether the two are the same phenomenon.
- Who reclassified the Chapter 10 record, or why.
- SERPENTFIRE's meaning, origin, or history.
- The Unknown Student's identity, species, motive, or affiliation.
- The Ch11 marker's or Ch13 annex marks' true explanation.
- The Outer Dark's true nature, cosmology, or any connection to the current mystery, Elyra, Varun, the Seeker, the Harbinger, the First Ember, the Lost Path, true Veil origin/weakness, or true breach theory.

## Must Not Imply

- That Aurelia's classroom doubt is factually validated by anything that follows, including Voss's admission.
- That Voss's admission is a reward for her having spoken.
- That Voss knows, secretly, more than he admits to.
- That the "Outer Dark" heading in the old classification index confirms a cosmological connection to the mystery — it must read as an old, unexplained bureaucratic label, not a revelation.
- That all four accumulated mystery threads share a mechanism.
- That the new bounded research access reopens the general Archive suspension.

## Character Constraints

### Aurelia
- Direct, physically confident, principled — consistent with Chapters 1–15.
- Her classroom choice (general, unembellished doubt) must remain genuinely uncertain in outcome — never validated by the room, the instructor, Voss, or the narration.
- She does not need or seek Aarav's approval, and does not treat his public backing as agreement.

### Aarav
- Analytical, verification-first, institutionally aware — consistent with Chapters 8–15.
- His Chapter 16 development is **behavioral, not epistemological**: he privately disagrees with Aurelia's timing but chooses not to undermine her agency publicly. This must not read as, or be described as, "trust." It must not repeat Chapter 15's self-correction structure (observation → error → correction) in any form.

### Lyra
- Present for her own academic/competitive reason (material shortage affecting assessed diagnostic work), independent of Aurelia's presence.
- Must not know, guess at, or stumble toward anything about the investigation, the transcription, the annex, or any protected lore.

### The Instructor
- Competent, ordinary, not villainous, not incompetent. Presents a **defensible general institutional principle**, not a lie. Has no special knowledge of the ongoing security review. Any reporting of the classroom exchange occurs through an ordinary, generic institutional channel — not because the instructor knows anything confidential.

### Voss
- Procedural, credible, competent — consistent with Chapters 9–15.
- His involvement flows from his own existing, specific oversight of Aurelia and Aarav's arrangement — not general authority.
- His admission is restrained to: older restrictions exist, predate his authority, and he lacks a full account of their original rationale. Nothing further.
- The admission must visibly cost him some measure of apparent completeness/authority in the protagonists' eyes — not delivered as a free concession.

## Relationship Constraints

**Current state:** Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation, beginning edge of Stage 3 — earned but unsettled Respect (carried forward from Chapter 15).

**Chapter 16 movement:** Aarav backs a choice he privately disagrees with, without taking it over; Aurelia does not read his backing as agreement or need it as validation. This is examined through friction, not warmth.

Do not advance to:
- established Friendship, Trust-declaration, emotional intimacy,
- romance, confession, or romantic subtext of any kind,
- protective-romantic behavior.

No trust speeches, declared-respect dialogue, gratitude, or physical softening. No engineered physical proximity — Scenes 1–2 keep Aurelia and Aarav physically separated; Scenes 3–4 are mobile/public, not stationary or enclosed.

## Magic Constraints

- Aurelia's Phoenix fire and Aarav's Naga sensitivity are not required in this chapter and should not be invoked as plot mechanisms. If referenced at all, strictly ordinary/background use only.
- No new magical instrument, measurement, terminology, or formal experiment.

## Structural Constraints

- Exactly five scenes: The Practicum / What She Chooses / After / Voss, in Motion / The First Ledger.
- POV locked to Aarav throughout.
- Scene 5 must end the chapter on a concrete, physical, active image — the old classification index open to a heading reading (or old-system-equivalent of) "Outer Dark — dormant/inactive" — registered, not read into, interpreted, or explained.

## Superseded / Rejected Design Elements — Do Not Use

- The Chapter 4 "fourth-week incident" as a named, publicly-known campus event, or Aurelia as a publicly-recognized witness of it (unverified against canon; rejected at Phase 2.1).
- Lyra having any special knowledge or intuition about the investigation (rejected — she must remain an ordinary student throughout).
- The instructor being incompetent, villainous, or aware of the confidential review (rejected — must remain competent and ordinary).
- Voss's admission functioning as validation of Aurelia's classroom claim (rejected — the two must remain structurally separate).
- Voss controlling general Archive policy or discipline broadly (rejected — his authority is scoped to the existing arrangement only).
- The "Outer Dark" heading functioning as a lore reveal or dramatic confirmation (rejected — it must read as an old, unexplained bureaucratic label only).
- A sixth scene, a new mystery object/symbol/person, or any forced convergence of the four accumulated threads (all rejected).
