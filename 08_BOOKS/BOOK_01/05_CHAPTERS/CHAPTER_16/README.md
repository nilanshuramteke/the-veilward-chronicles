## Chapter Function

Chapter 16 is the first chapter after the Book 1 midpoint. An ordinary Academy diagnostic practicum, complicated by the ongoing Archive suspension, puts an instructor's general institutional claim into a public room. Aurelia, grounded only in her own undisclosed doubt, contests it in general terms — a genuine, principled, uncertain choice that is never proven correct. Aarav privately disagrees with her timing but backs her publicly, a distinction he articulates directly rather than resolving into either agreement or control. The exchange is reported through an ordinary institutional channel and reaches Voss because of his existing, specific oversight of their arrangement. Pressed, he admits more than procedure requires — older restrictions predate his own authority, and he cannot fully explain them — and, because the reclassification problem is now his own accountability, authorizes a narrow, personally-supervised look at the old classification system's structural index. The chapter ends with that index open to an old heading reading "Outer Dark," marked dormant/inactive — registered, entirely unexplained.
