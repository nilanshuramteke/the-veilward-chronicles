# Chapter 7 Blueprint

## Chapter Function
Chapter 7 moves the story out of repeated confirmation and into the first bounded instance of forced cooperation. The chapter must create qualitative movement inside the primary mystery while preserving uncertainty.

## POV
Aurelia, close third person only.

## Starting State
- The Chapter 4 event remains objectively real and unexplained.
- Witness accounts agree on broad facts but conflict in granular detail.
- The ward record contains no accounting that explains the event.
- The recurring angular mark exists in three confirmed instances but remains unexplained.
- Aurelia and Aarav remain rivals with no partnership or shared agenda.
- Aurelia's Chapter 6 boundary remains active: she refuses to be reduced to a missing piece in Aarav's evidence.

## Core Institutional Setup
Professor Voss assigns Aurelia and Aarav a bounded statement-reconciliation task based only on information available through official witness statements. Voss does not know their private mark discoveries or Chapter 6 comparison.

## Four-Scene Progression

### Scene 1 — External Imposition
Purpose: Establish forced cooperation and immediate resistance.
- Aurelia is summoned regarding her statement.
- Aarav is present or arrives as the assignment becomes clear.
- Voss identifies their detailed, conflicting statements as useful material for reconciliation.
- The task is bounded to statement comparison and a reconciled account for institutional review.
- Aurelia resists the pairing and any implication that her account needs another person's framework.
- Aarav raises a restrained efficiency objection but does not overturn Voss's decision.
Exit: cooperation is imposed, not chosen.

### Scene 2 — Initial Friction
Purpose: Show conflict about method rather than replaying the Chapter 6 argument.
- Aurelia and Aarav begin the comparison without an agreed process.
- Their working styles collide.
- Aurelia insists on preserving what was actually said and experienced rather than forcing testimony into neat agreement.
- Aarav tests comparison methods without claiming his framework is authoritative.
- The primary mystery remains present as the reason reconciliation matters.
Exit: they cannot complete the task through parallel work alone.

### Scene 3 — Necessary Cooperation
Purpose: Chapter's investigative center of gravity and qualitative payoff.
- Systematic cross-referencing of the witness statements requires combined labor.
- The overlap is uneven rather than uniform or random.
- Some statements unexpectedly echo each other on specific, matchable points.
- Other details remain singular and resist comparison.
- No count, category, grouping logic, witness type, or repeatable rule is established.
- The pattern remains provisional and observational.
- The characters gain a sharper question about the shape of testimony, not an answer about the anomaly.
- Demonstrate the pattern with concrete comparisons drawn from authorized witness material; do not invent new anomalous phenomena merely to illustrate it.
Exit: the evidence is harder to dismiss but no closer to explanation.

### Scene 4 — Bounded Close
Purpose: Land the practical and relational consequence without creating partnership.
- The immediate task is completed.
- Aarav visibly handles Aurelia's contribution differently from before, including explicit attribution by name or equivalent observable respect for ownership.
- Aurelia notices the change but does not need to interpret it.
- No apology, trust breakthrough, friendship, romance, or future agreement.
- No Voss announcement about another assignment.
Exit: same rivals, task complete, independence complicated by one observed behavioral shift and unresolved evidence.

## Ending State
- No standing partnership.
- No mystery explanation.
- The reader understands that forced cooperation produced an observation neither character could have reached as cleanly alone.
- The uneven witness overlap remains a new question within the primary mystery.
