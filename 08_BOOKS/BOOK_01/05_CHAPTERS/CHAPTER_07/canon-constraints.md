# Chapter 7 Canon Constraints

## Primary Mystery
The primary mystery remains the contradiction between the lived event, conflicting witness evidence, and the ward record's lack of an accounting that explains what occurred.

Do not replace this with the witness-pattern question.

## Witness Overlap Firewall
Allowed:
- uneven overlap among testimony
- specific recurring details
- specific singular details
- provisional comparison

Forbidden:
- fixed versions
- numbered clusters
- witness categories
- magical sensitivity classes
- positional, distance, timing, personality, or affinity rules
- repeatable anomaly mechanics
- explanations for why overlap occurs

The anomaly must not be described as choosing, tailoring, responding, watching, wanting, remembering, or deliberately differentiating experiences.

## Secondary Mark Boundary
The recurring three-stroke angular mark remains secondary.
Chapter 7 must not:
- discover another mark
- explain its meaning, maker, age, origin, purpose, or magical status
- connect it causally to the Chapter 4 event

The approved Phase 3.2 record keeps the mark outside Chapter 7's active investigation.

## Voss Knowledge Boundary
Voss may know:
- official incident facts
- collected witness statements
- observable discrepancies
- institutional review needs

Voss may not know:
- Aurelia's private mark discovery
- Aarav's private mark discovery
- the third mark
- their private Chapter 6 comparison
- private complementary evidence
- the anomaly's nature

## Revelation Sequence
Do not introduce:
- historical precedent or lack of precedent
- historical record gaps
- a breach, entry, crossing, intrusion, or external-source theory as in-world knowledge
- anomaly intelligence or observation
- institutional memory of a similar event
- Chapter 8 or later revelations

## Protected Lore
Do not introduce or imply:
- Serpentfire
- Outer Dark
- First Ember
- ancient or erased history
- ancient containment
- hidden chambers
- protected future lore

## Ward Neutrality
Use only neutral framing: the record provides no accounting that explains the event.
Do not use entry, crossing, breach, failure, origin, or directional explanations.

## Chapter 3 Firewall
Chapter 3's wind remains unrelated and should not be reinterpreted or connected.

## Academy Mechanics
Any assignment, reporting, reconciliation, or supervision process introduced for Chapter 7 is situational unless established elsewhere. Do not silently create permanent Academy procedure.
