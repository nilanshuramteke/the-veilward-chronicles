# Chapter 7 Drafting Brief

## POV and Voice
Aurelia is the sole close-third POV.

Her narrative register should remain:
- direct
- kinetic
- action-first
- alert to behavior and practical consequences
- impatient with unsupported abstraction
- resistant to being managed or reduced to evidence

Do not allow her narration to drift into Aarav's analytical register.

Aarav's inner thoughts are inaccessible. Show him through dialogue, visible choices, timing, expression, and Aurelia's limited inference.

## Relationship Pressure
Starting state:
- rivals
- independent
- no trust
- no partnership

Required movement:
- one bounded task completed together
- one observable change in how Aarav handles Aurelia's contribution
- Aurelia notices but does not need to decode it

Do not establish:
- friendship
- trust
- romance
- confession
- emotional intimacy
- voluntary long-term collaboration
- shared investigative agenda

## Reader-Contract Requirement
Chapter 7 must not feel like another holding or confirmation chapter.

It must deliver:
1. qualitative mystery movement
2. forced cooperation that matters
3. a small emotional-temperature shift
4. genuine forward pressure

The emotional shift should feel like something no longer fits neatly inside the old rivalry, not like romance.

## Concrete Evidence Rule
Show the uneven overlap through concrete statement comparison:
- at least one or two matchable echoes
- at least one singular or resistant detail

Do not repeatedly summarize the discovery with abstract phrases such as "the accounts formed a pattern."

Do not invent new anomaly phenomena to make the comparison dramatic.

## Mystery Atmosphere Rule
Do not manufacture unease through vague narration such as:
- something felt wrong
- an unnamed unnatural feeling
- the room seemed to know

Use concrete contradictions and observable evidence.

## Dialogue
Avoid perfectly symmetrical banter.
Break exchanges with action, interruption, refusal, silence, practical work, or non-answers.

## Repetition Watch
Avoid repeated reliance on:
- trailing explanatory `which...` clauses
- `the way [subject][verb]` constructions
- `the kind of...that` constructions
- clustered trailing uses of `already`

Do not replace one repeated construction with another mechanical substitute.

## Ending Guidance
Do not end with:
- a solution
- a future assignment announcement
- a partnership agreement
- a romance button
- an explanation

End on earned unresolved pressure arising from the evidence and changed practical relationship.
