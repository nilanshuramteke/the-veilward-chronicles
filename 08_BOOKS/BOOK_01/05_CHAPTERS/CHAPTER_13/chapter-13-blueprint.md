# Chapter 13 — Blueprint

## Chapter Title
**Under the Surface**

## POV
Aurelia — close third person, past tense.

## Core Dramatic Question

**What do Aurelia and Aarav owe to evidence they cannot re-gather, and to each other, once they choose to act without institutional permission?**

## Primary Narrative Engine

**Ordinary obligation → time-bound opportunity → incomplete discovery → self-authored consequence.**

Dev's ordinary Academy clean-out duty in the eastern annex creates a real three-day renovation/sealing window, which gives Aurelia and Aarav a limited, informally-known opportunity to inspect an older physical space before it is permanently altered. Their investigation reveals recurring, patterned physical evidence, and they must decide — jointly, without an institution deciding for them — what responsibility they carry for evidence they can no longer re-gather.

## Core Chapter Movement

**Ordinary Academy life → recognized opportunity → physical investigation → forced incompleteness → deliberate, mutual decision.**

## Mandatory Functions

1. Dev's clean-out duty, established as an ordinary, one-off task assignment, opens the chapter through ordinary Academy life.
2. The eastern annex's three-day renovation/sealing window is established as real, ordinary, pre-scheduled, and coincidental to the mystery.
3. Dev's incidental, firsthand knowledge of the work crew's daily break routine (door propped, area briefly unwatched) is the sole source of the opportunity — he grants nothing.
4. Aurelia recognizes the opportunity herself; Aarav independently evaluates and accepts the risk.
5. They physically enter the annex during the crew's break and investigate the older section.
6. At least two spatially distinct marks are found, showing evidence of recurrence over time — pattern only, never intent.
7. The work window ends before the comparison between the two marks can be completed; the crew's return is heard, ordinary, and sufficient to end the window without any chase, confrontation, or near-capture.
8. The specific section becomes physically inaccessible once the renovation proceeds — this was their one viable window, not a repeatable one.
9. In the aftermath, Aarav explicitly applies his Chapter 12 disclosure principle forward: an isolated fragment produced no real progress last time, so a complete account is worth waiting for.
10. Aurelia's instinct to disclose immediately remains visible and is honored, not overridden — the decision to wait is reached jointly.
11. The Chapter 11 marker, the Chapter 12 sighting, and the Chapter 13 pattern are grouped only as unresolved observations that may eventually belong in the same report — never declared the same phenomenon.
12. Chapter 14 inherits a concrete changed condition: undocumented, non-repeatable physical evidence, held in reserve by mutual choice.

## Forbidden Functions

Do not:
- explain or reveal SERPENTFIRE or the Unknown Student in any respect,
- imply intent, intelligence, agency, motive, consciousness, breach, or entity behavior from the annex pattern,
- assert a causal connection among the Ch11 marker, Ch12 sighting, and Ch13 pattern,
- resolve the comparison between the two annex marks,
- let Dev grant formal access, possess institutional authority, or learn anything about the mystery,
- stage a chase, near-capture, confrontation, or hiding sequence,
- advance the relationship past the beginning edge of Respect, or frame the Scene 4 decision as secrecy rather than strategic patience,
- introduce a new Academy department, labor system, or administrative policy,
- let Aurelia's fire or Aarav's Naga sensitivity function as detection, diagnosis, or confirmation of anything about the mystery,
- touch Chapter 15's protected midpoint revelation.

## Ending Requirement

The chapter must end with a materially changed condition — a jointly-held, non-repeatable body of unresolved physical evidence, and a deliberate, mutual decision about when and how to disclose it — not a walk-away, a vague promise, or an externally imposed consequence.
