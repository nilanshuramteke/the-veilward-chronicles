## Chapter Function

Chapter 13 returns agency to Aurelia and Aarav after Chapter 12 took it away. Dev's ordinary, one-off clean-out task in the eastern annex incidentally exposes a real three-day renovation/sealing window and his own firsthand, incidental knowledge of the work crew's unwatched break routine. Aurelia and Aarav use that ordinary opportunity to physically investigate an older part of the Academy, find two spatially distinct marks showing recurrence over time, and run out of time before they can finish comparing them. The chapter ends with a self-authored consequence — a jointly-chosen decision to hold the incomplete evidence for a coherent future report rather than another isolated disclosure — in deliberate contrast to Chapter 12's institutionally-imposed consequence.

Locked progression:

**Ordinary clean-out duty → incidental break-routine knowledge → recognized opportunity → independent risk evaluation → physical annex investigation → recurring physical evidence found → work window expires → incomplete comparison → joint reporting decision.**

The chapter does not explain the annex pattern or resolve any prior mystery thread. It advances the mystery from "two new, unresolved threads discovered under conditions the protagonists did not control" (Chapter 12) to "a third unresolved thread, discovered and then deliberately managed by the protagonists themselves."
