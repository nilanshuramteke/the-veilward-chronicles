# Chapter 13 — Canon Constraints

> **Authority:** This document is binding for Phase 4 Drafting. It consolidates Phase 1 Orientation, Phase 2 Detailed Plan, the two Phase 2 Correction/Reconciliation passes, Phase 3.1 Targeted Calibration + Corrections, and Phase 3.2 Final Calibration. Where any earlier Chapter 13 document conflicts with this one, this one governs.

## POV

**Aurelia — close third person, past tense.** Locked by the Book 1 chapter outline and reaffirmed through every planning phase. Chapter 13 is Aurelia's alternating counterpart to Aarav's Chapter 12.

- Full Aurelia interiority is permitted and expected — her recognition of the opportunity in Scene 1, her experience of using fire practically under time pressure, and her reaction to leaving the comparison incomplete are all interiority beats that require her POV to function.
- Everything about Aarav must be inferred through his speech, behavior, physical action, and Aurelia's interpretation. No Aarav interiority.
- Aarav's private Chapter 8 knowledge (the Dev-like voice's behavioral mismatch) must not surface in this chapter in any form — not referenced, not alluded to, not compared to anything in this chapter's events. Because this is Aurelia's POV, this is a non-issue by construction, but no scene may create narration that implies Aarav is holding something back in a way that gestures at its content.

## Relationship

**Current state:** Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation, beginning edge of Stage 3 — Respect (carried forward from Chapter 12, tested but not advanced by Chapter 12's disagreement).

**Chapter 13 movement:** shared risk → joint choice → shared consequence. This is a third, distinct register from Chapter 11 (notice → test → validation) and Chapter 12 (disagreement → one restrains, other accepts). Respect is exercised through a decision reached *together*, under real stakes, not through one party yielding to the other.

Maintain:
- rivalry and friction as intact and undiminished,
- practical cooperation under shared physical risk,
- growing behavioral (never verbal) respect.

Do not advance to:
- established friendship,
- emotional intimacy,
- declared trust,
- romance.

**At most one subtle, unforced romance-temperature beat** is permitted (not required) — physical, involuntary, unremarked, immediately subordinated to the scene's actual action (e.g., proximity while working in a confined space by firelight). If used, it must emerge through behavior, never through interiority naming the feeling, and must never use a "noticed something → didn't examine why" refusal-to-introspect construction (flagged as an emerging tic after two appearances in Chapter 12).

## Characterization

### Aurelia
- Direct, physically confident, action-oriented, impatient with unnecessary restraint, independently motivated.
- Recognizes the annex opportunity herself, in Scene 1 — this is her judgment call, not something Aarav or Dev leads her to.
- Uses her fire practically (illumination/warmth) in Scene 2 — this must never function as detection, revelation, or confirmation of anything about the mystery.
- Her Scene 4 instinct to disclose immediately must remain visible and intact; the joint decision to wait must be reached, not conceded.

### Aarav
- Analytical, observant, verification-first, institutionally aware, measured under uncertainty.
- Independently evaluates the annex risk in Scene 1 — his agreement to proceed is his own conclusion, not automatic deference to Aurelia.
- His Scene 4 reasoning is a **forward application** of his Chapter 12 principle (a complete account is more useful than an isolated fragment), not a reversal of it. He must not be written as newly discovering secrecy or advocating concealment.
- His Naga sensitivity contributes one bounded, physical, inconclusive impression in the annex — never a diagnosis, never a comparison to a named phenomenon.
- His Chapter 8 knowledge remains entirely private and unreferenced (see POV section).

### Dev
- An ordinary student performing a situational, one-off clean-out task assignment — comparable in kind to Aurelia's one-time cataloguing assignment in Chapter 12, not a standing labor system, disciplinary program, or new Academy institution.
- Mildly put-upon, grumbling about the tedium, concerned primarily with his own obligation.
- His knowledge of the work crew's break routine is firsthand and incidental — he mentions it as part of complaining about his own task, not as help offered to Aurelia and Aarav.
- Carries real personal risk: if it ever surfaces that a security-relevant routine was passed along informally, the consequence lands on him first.
- Understands nothing about the mystery, the annex marks, or why Aurelia is interested. Must not become a lore source, a secret investigator, or a convenient keyholder.

## Magic — Hard Locks

- Aurelia's fire affinity does **not** detect the mystery, does **not** identify or classify the annex marks, and does **not** confirm the pattern. It provides illumination and warmth only — the same practical use already established for her, not a new capability.
- Aarav's Naga sensitivity in the annex registers only a bounded, unclassifiable spatial impression — no comparison to wards, no magical-signature language, no diagnostic certainty, no confirmation of cause, entity, or intent.
- No new magical instrument, numerical measurement, formal experiment, or repeatable detection procedure may be introduced.
- No combined Phoenix–Naga ability. No destiny-confirming magic.
- Governing principle, carried forward: **Aurelia's affinity can produce a question, not an answer.**

## Mystery

- The annex investigation may establish **recurrence/pattern only** — at least two spatially distinct marks showing evidence of repeated occurrence over time. It must never be described or implied as evidence of intent, intelligence, agency, motive, consciousness, breach, or entity behavior.
- The comparison between the two marks (whether they share a common origin) must remain **unresolved** at chapter's end — this incompleteness is a required structural outcome, not an oversight to be corrected.
- **SERPENTFIRE** receives zero on-page presence. No meaning, origin, mental flash, magical echo, or causal connection to this chapter's events.
- The **Unknown Student** does not appear. No identity, species, motive, affiliation, or connection to the annex is established.
- No causal connection may be asserted among the Chapter 11 angular marker, the Chapter 12 Unknown Student sighting, and the Chapter 13 annex pattern. Characters may group them as "three things they don't understand" or "three things that may eventually belong in the same report" — never as confirmed manifestations of one phenomenon.
- Chapter 15's protected midpoint revelation remains fully untouched. Chapter 13 sharpens the mystery's question without answering any part of it.

## Institutional / World

- The eastern annex renovation/sealing is ordinary, pre-scheduled Academy maintenance, coincidental in timing and origin to the mystery. It is not the Academy responding to or investigating anything related to the protagonists.
- The three-day window and the daily break-time access are real and load-bearing, but neither is dramatized as an artificial countdown; both are experienced as ordinary institutional scheduling with genuinely irreversible investigative consequences.
- No new Academy department, certification, roster, security policy, or administrative system may be introduced by this chapter.
- Archive access remains suspended (inherited from Chapter 12); its status is not resolved, referenced only insofar as it informs Aarav's Scene 4 reasoning.

## Superseded Design Elements — Do Not Use

The following formulations from earlier Chapter 13 drafts are explicitly superseded by the Phase 2 reconciliation passes and the Phase 3.1 corrections, and must not appear in Phase 4 drafting:

- An optional SERPENTFIRE "mental flash" or magical echo experienced by Aurelia in the annex (superseded by: zero on-page SERPENTFIRE presence).
- The annex pattern described as "the first physical evidence of intent" (superseded by: recurrence/pattern only, with intent explicitly withheld).
- A vague, undecided walk-away ending for Scene 4 (superseded by: a concrete, jointly-chosen evidentiary/reporting threshold).
- Dev formally granting or authorizing "temporary work-crew access" (superseded by: Dev possessing only incidental, firsthand knowledge of the crew's ordinary break routine).
- Scene 3 ending on a near-capture/chase/escape beat (superseded by: a running-out-of-time beat ending in a deliberate, unhurried departure with the comparison incomplete).
- Scene 4 framed as the protagonists choosing to hide or withhold evidence (superseded by: strategic patience — waiting for a complete, actionable account — explicitly reasoned from Aarav's Chapter 12 disclosure principle, not a reversal of it).
