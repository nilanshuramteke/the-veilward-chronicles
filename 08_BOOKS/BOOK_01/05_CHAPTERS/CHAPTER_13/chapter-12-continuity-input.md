## Important Transition

Chapter 13 must **not** repeat Chapter 12's engine:

> discovery under institutional constraint → consequence taken from the protagonists by an authority.

Chapter 13 uses a different engine: **an ordinary student obligation creates a naturally time-bound physical opportunity; the protagonists choose to use it, and the resulting incompleteness is a consequence they earn through the choice itself**, not one imposed on them by an institution. Agency returns to Aurelia and Aarav — this chapter's outcome is self-authored, in contrast to Chapter 12's outcome being taken from them.
