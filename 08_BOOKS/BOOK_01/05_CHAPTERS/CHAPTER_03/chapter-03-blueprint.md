# Chapter 3 Blueprint — The Trial

## POV
Aurelia, close third person, past tense.

## Chapter function
Chapter 3 converts the private and developing Aurelia–Aarav rivalry into a visible competitive conflict inside the Academy evaluation.

The chapter must:
1. Continue directly from the evaluation momentum established in Chapters 1–2.
2. Put Aurelia and Aarav into genuine direct opposition.
3. Demonstrate competence from both characters without establishing a permanent hierarchy.
4. Introduce or foreground Lyra as an active competitive pressure, not an exposition device.
5. Make the rivalry socially visible: other students can now see that these two push against one another.
6. End with rivalry intensified and unresolved.

## Required emotional movement
Focused confidence → irritation at public comparison → competitive engagement → recognition that Aarav is difficult to dismiss → sharper determination.

## What must not happen
- No romance coding or attraction framing.
- No friendship breakthrough or emotional vulnerability exchange.
- No definitive winner who settles the Aurelia/Aarav hierarchy permanently.
- No major evaluation outcome that canonizes the Academy's assessment system beyond this chapter's local needs.
- No central mystery material. Chapter 4 remains the beginning of that movement.
- No ominous atmosphere, strange sensations, unexplained environmental anomalies, or artificial foreshadowing.

## Central reader question
Not: Who is secretly more powerful?

Instead: Can either of them tolerate being publicly measured against the other without conceding anything?
