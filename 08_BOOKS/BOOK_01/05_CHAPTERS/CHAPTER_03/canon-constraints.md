# Chapter 3 Canon Constraints

## Authority
Higher-level canon overrides this package. If a needed detail is absent, do not silently invent permanent canon.

## POV and information control
- Close third person through Aurelia only.
- No access to Aarav's private thoughts.
- Aurelia may infer from observable behavior, but inference must remain inference.

## Character locks
### Aurelia
Capable, proud, direct, energetic, competitive. Her Phoenix fire remains controlled within established limits. No unexplained escalation.

### Aarav
Measured, analytical, controlled, dry. His competence must be demonstrated, not inflated into unexplained mastery.

### Relationship
Still rivals. Competitive familiarity is allowed. Romance, jealousy framing, vulnerability, or friendship resolution are not.

### Lyra
Use only what established character canon supports. Do not invent deeper motives, history, powers, or permanent relationship facts to make the chapter work.

## Magic restrictions
- Use established Phoenix and Naga capabilities only.
- No Serpentfire.
- No combined or hybrid power.
- No new permanent mechanics.
- Evaluation mechanics remain local unless existing Academy canon explicitly supports them.

## Mystery protection
All protected mystery elements remain dormant.

Forbidden before Chapter 4:
- strange sensations presented as significant
- unexplained environmental wrongness
- ominous negation
- suspicious coincidences framed as clues
- Echo-adjacent phenomena
- artificial foreshadowing that signals hidden danger

## Evaluation design
Chapter 2 deliberately left the format open. Chapter 3 may define the immediate exercise, but only as a scene-local mechanism unless verified elsewhere in canon.
