## Purpose

## Chapter Function

Chapter 17 gives Aurelia a genuine, unconditional opportunity to step back from the shared investigation to protect her evaluation standing — and has her choose, once, to remain involved instead. The pull to stay comes from two directions: Aarav's narrow, explicitly hedged administrative hypothesis connecting SERPENTFIRE and the general kind of case the Chapter 11 fourth stone represents, and Aurelia's own independent, physical discovery that the fourth stone hasn't weathered like its seven neighbors. Neither fact proves anything. Together, on a closing physical deadline, they make leaving feel like abandoning a real question rather than completing an assignment. Along the way, Aarav volunteers a brief, unguarded admission of uncertainty about the cost his own judgment is imposing on her — the chapter's single romance-coded beat. Aurelia weighs the real cost of continuing — her own time, Voss's spent goodwill, Dev's light consequence — and chooses it anyway, in the chapter outline's explicitly locked beat: her first active decision to trust Aarav.

## Critical Chapter Principle

**Aurelia is handed a clean, guilt-free way out of the investigation, and refuses it — not because she's certain of anything, but because a hedged hypothesis and her own two hands, held against a closing deadline, make walking away feel like giving up on a real question. She has simply decided, for the first time, to trust where the evidence points before anyone can prove it right.**
