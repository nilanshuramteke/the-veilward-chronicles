## 1. Immediate End-State

At the end of Chapter 17:

- Aurelia independently observed, through direct sight and touch, that the Chapter 11 fourth stone's wear pattern does not match its seven neighbors. Aarav independently confirmed the same physical fact through his own touch, after her observation, not before or in place of it.
- Aarav's Chapter 11 chalk line remains visible on the fourth stone, fainter and weathered, still plain and non-symbolic.
- Aarav presented a narrow, explicitly hedged hypothesis: it is plausible, not proven, that SERPENTFIRE's damaged fragment passed through the same broad structural filing category the old classification system used for unresolved material in general — the category the fourth stone would fall into, had it existed in that system's era. He explicitly distinguished this from Chapter 14's discredited intruder theory in his own dialogue.
- Voss offered Aurelia a genuine, unconditional release from the index work, tied to her evaluation timing, with a same-day deadline. Aurelia declined it and explicitly chose, once, to remain on the work — the chapter outline's locked beat: her first active decision to trust Aarav, demonstrated through action, not declaration.
- Exactly one romance-coded beat occurred, in Scene 4: Aarav's unprompted admission of uncertainty about the cost his own judgment was imposing on Aurelia. It was brief, unnamed by either character, and did not recur.
- Dev was assigned to help the Training Grounds regrading crew, a light, self-caused, proportionate consequence of his own repeated drainage complaints.

Chapter 18 begins from this state. It must not restage the stone examination, the Scene 2 offer, the Scene 3 hypothesis, the Scene 4 admission, or the Scene 6 choice.

- The fourth stone's wear pattern is physically anomalous compared to its seven neighbors — confirmed independently by both Aurelia and Aarav.

- Aurelia has chosen, on the record with Voss, to continue the index research.
- The Training Grounds' low stretch, including the fourth stone, is being regraded this week; the stone is scheduled to be dug up and reset before the week is out.

### They Reasonably Suspect
- It is plausible — not proven — that SERPENTFIRE's fragment passed through the same broad structural category as the fourth stone's general kind of case, generations ago.
- The old classification index may hold more structural information worth examining.

### They Do Not Know
- Why the fourth stone differs physically from its neighbors.
- What SERPENTFIRE actually means, or why Voss reacted to it as he did.
- Whether the fourth stone and SERPENTFIRE share any true connection, cause, or origin.
- Whether the old classification system's structure ever actually held either artifact.
- Anything about the Chapter 12 Unknown Student, the Chapter 13 annex marks, or the Chapter 16 "Outer Dark" heading beyond what was already known entering Chapter 17.

Chapter 18 must not treat any item in this last category as settled.
