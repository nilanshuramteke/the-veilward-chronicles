# Chapter 17 — Canon Constraints

> **Authority:** Binding for Phase 4 Drafting. Consolidates Phase 1 Orientation, Phase 2 Detailed Scene Plan, Phase 2 Correction Pass, Phase 3.1 Targeted Calibration, Phase 2.1 Canon Correction/Integration Pass, and Phase 3.2 Final Calibration. Where any earlier Chapter 17 document conflicts with this one, this one governs.

## POV

**Aurelia — close third person, past tense.** Locked by the Book 1 chapter outline's alternation rule (odd chapters = Aurelia) and reaffirmed through every planning phase. Chapter 17 is Aarav's alternating counterpart chapter to Chapter 16.

- Full Aurelia interiority is permitted and expected — her competitive dissatisfaction in Scene 1, her weighing of Voss's offer in Scene 2, her own physical reasoning about the fourth stone in Scene 4, her unnamed reaction to Aarav's admission, and her deliberation in Scene 5 are all required interiority beats.
- Everything about Aarav, Voss, Dev, and Lyra must be inferred through their speech, behavior, physical action, and Aurelia's interpretation. **No Aarav interiority. No Voss interiority. No Dev interiority.**
- No omniscient narration may confirm, deny, or explain the fourth stone's cause, SERPENTFIRE's meaning, or whether Aurelia's inference is correct.

## Must Preserve (Established Canon/Continuity)

- The Chapter 16 exit state in full (see `chapter-16-continuity-input.md`).
- General Archive access remains suspended, unresolved, since Chapter 12.
- The narrow, Voss-supervised authorization to examine the old classification system's structural index, granted at the end of Chapter 16 — this is Chapter 17's only legitimate access to that system, and covers structure/organization only, never incident content.
- The Chapter 11 fourth stone: the fourth of eight ordinary Training Grounds drill-stones; produces a slow, thin, guttering flame under Aurelia's touch; marked with a plain chalk line by Aarav (explicitly not a symbol); has **no historical documentation anywhere** — a present-day discovery only.
- SERPENTFIRE: surfaced on a fire-damaged inventory sheet during an authorized, current Archive cross-referencing task (Chapter 12); its age and origin-system are **unconfirmed**; Voss confiscated it and never explained his reaction.
- The Chapter 11 marker, the Chapter 12 sighting, and the Chapter 13 annex marks were formally disclosed together to an institutional review board in Chapter 14 — a current institutional record, not historical documentation.
- Chapter 14's institutional intruder theory (marker + sighting + annex marks + disturbed corridor seal = one intruder using forgotten infrastructure) was presented strictly as an unconfirmed institutional interpretation and was materially undermined by the Chapter 15 midpoint discovery that the external-entry assumption was never validated, historically or currently.
- The **Angular Mark** (Chapter 3/5–6 thread) remains a separate, dormant, unrelated thread — distinct from the Chapter 11 fourth stone.
- The Training Grounds' established drainage/runoff problem (Chapter 11) — an ordinary, long-standing maintenance complaint, not a mystery element.
- Voss remains a competent, procedural institutional actor whose authority is scoped to his existing, tightened, personal oversight of Aurelia and Aarav's arrangement — not general Academy policy, discipline, or the Archive broadly.
- Dev (`04_CHARACTERS/dev.md`): an ordinary student with no ongoing investigation role, established since Chapter 11/13; his knowledge is limited strictly to grapevine/personal-experience facts.

## Must Not Reveal

- Why the Chapter 11 fourth stone differs physically from its neighbors.
- What SERPENTFIRE actually means, or why Voss reacted to it as he did.
- Whether the fourth stone and SERPENTFIRE share any true connection.
- Whether the old classification system's structure ever actually held either artifact.
- The true nature of the Outer Dark, the Seeker, the Harbinger, the First Ember's purpose, Elyra, Varun, the Lost Path, the true Veil origin or weakness, true breach theory, or any Echo identity, origin, motive, or intelligence.
- The Unknown Student's identity, species, motive, or affiliation.
- The cause of the Chapter 13 annex marks.

## Must Not Imply

- That the SERPENTFIRE/fourth-stone hypothesis is a documented, confirmed, or causal connection — it must remain administrative/historical/categorical and explicitly hedged throughout.
- That the fourth stone's differing wear pattern proves replacement, tampering, deliberate concealment, agency, or intent.
- That Aarav's hypothesis is, or resembles, a restoration of Chapter 14's discredited intruder theory — the distinction (administrative/categorical vs. causal/mechanistic) must be made legible in Aarav's own dialogue.
- That the hypothesis extends to the Unknown Student, the annex marks, the corridor, or any intruder framing.
- That the Training Grounds regrading/resurfacing is anything other than an ordinary, mundane maintenance deadline.
- That Voss's offer in Scene 2 is a test, a trap, or secretly conditional.
- That Aurelia has decided anything before Scene 6.
- That Aurelia's choice in Scene 6 is validated as factually correct by anything in the chapter — her value is in the choice, not in being proven right.

## Character Constraints

### Aurelia
- Direct, physically confident, independently motivated — consistent with Chapters 1–16.
- Generates the chapter's central physical insight (the fourth stone's wear mismatch) herself, through her own direct examination — Aarav may confirm it through his own touch, but must not supply or lead her to it.
- Does not decide anything until Scene 6. Scenes 4–5 contain inclination and deliberation only.
- Her choice in Scene 6 must be shown through stated decision and action, never through a declarative line such as "I trust you" or equivalent.

### Aarav
- Analytical, verification-first, hedging deliberately — consistent with Chapters 8–16.
- His Chapter 17 contribution is the old classification system's organizational structure, within the exact scope Voss authorized at the end of Chapter 16 — never incident content, never a claim of documented fact.
- Must explicitly and naturally distinguish his hypothesis from Chapter 14's discredited intruder theory, in his own dialogue, not as authorial exposition.
- Offers his single romance-coded admission in Scene 4 as a genuine, uncontrolled beat — brief, not dwelt upon, not repeated.
- Must not persuade Aurelia toward staying, frame his backing as owed, or solve Scene 4's insight for her.

### Voss
- Procedural, credible, competent, genuinely well-intentioned — consistent with Chapters 9–16.
- His Scene 2 offer is unconditional and sincere; no hidden terms, no institutional-record consequence either way, no manipulation.
- Does not learn about Aarav's SERPENTFIRE hypothesis or the fourth-stone observation at any point in this chapter.
- His tightened, personal-supervision posture from Chapter 16 remains in force and is not contradicted by the genuineness of his Scene 2 offer — the two are compatible, not in tension.

### Dev
- Present for his own ordinary reasons (a long-standing, genuine complaint about Training Grounds drainage), independent of the investigation.
- Must not know, guess at, or stumble toward anything about the fourth stone, SERPENTFIRE, or any protected lore.
- His regrading-crew consequence must remain light, proportionate, and self-caused (repeated complaining), never connected to Aurelia or Aarav's actions.

### Lyra
- Brief, unbothered, present only in Scene 1 for ordinary competitive/social texture. No knowledge of, or curiosity about, the investigation.

## Relationship Constraints

**Current state:** Stage 2 — Forced Allies / Rival Cooperation, at the beginning edge of Stage 3 — earned but unsettled Respect (carried forward from Chapter 16).

**Chapter 17 movement:** Aurelia's Scene 6 choice is the chapter outline's explicitly locked beat — "her first active decision to trust him." This must be demonstrated entirely through behavior (declining Voss's offer, accepting the cost, continuing the work on the strength of Aarav's evidence and her own reasoning), never through declarative dialogue naming trust.

Do not advance to:
- established Friendship, Trust-declaration as dialogue, emotional-intimacy breakthrough,
- a named romantic relationship, confession, or kiss,
- protective-romantic behavior beyond the single Scene 4 admission.

Exactly one romance-coded beat is permitted, in Scene 4: brief, embedded, vulnerability-driven, unnamed by either character. It must not use engineered shared-light staging, shared-object staging, artificial shoulder proximity, or any repetition of the retired Chapter 11–15 proximity device (see `chapter-16-continuity-input.md`, Section "Immediate Inherited State").

## Magic Constraints

- Aurelia's Phoenix fire is used exactly once, in Scene 4, in its established practical/illumination register (per `book-01-magic-usage-lock.md`) — the same ordinary test she performed in Chapter 11. It produces a question, not an answer, and is never diagnostic or definitive.
- Aarav's Naga sensitivity is not required in this chapter and should not be invoked as a plot mechanism.
- No new magical instrument, measurement, terminology, or formal experiment.

## Structural Constraints

- Exactly six scenes: The List / The Easy Way Out / Old Filing / What the Light Finds / What It Would Cost / The Choice.
- POV locked to Aurelia throughout.
- The single definitive choice occurs in Scene 6 only. Scenes 4 and 5 must not contain it, even implicitly.
- Scene 6 must end the chapter on Aurelia's felt ownership of the cost she chose — not a new clue, a cliffhanger, or an ominous image.

## Superseded / Rejected Design Elements — Do Not Use

- The original Phase 2 "deliberate placement/category mismatch" framing implying the fourth stone was deliberately hidden or obscured (rejected at the Phase 2 Correction Pass for creating an unauthorized new mystery — "who hid it and why").
- A documentary/historical-record link between the fourth stone and SERPENTFIRE (rejected at Phase 3.1 — the fourth stone has no historical documentation of any kind; any connection must be structural/categorical only).
- An invented records, copying, or access job for Dev (rejected at Phase 3.1/2.1 — his contribution must come from ordinary grapevine knowledge already established in Chapter 11).
- A dedicated, standalone romance scene (rejected at the Phase 2 Correction Pass — the romance beat must be embedded inside an existing scene, not staged separately).
- A duplicated decision point (Aurelia deciding privately in Scene 4 and again formally in Scene 6) (rejected at the Phase 2 Correction Pass — the choice must occur exactly once, in Scene 6).
- Any claim that the fourth stone was definitely replaced, tampered with, or concealed with intent (rejected at Phase 2.1 — even "was replaced" was judged too certain; the locked observation is comparative and cautious only: "hasn't weathered like the others" / "not the same age").
- A seventh scene, a new mystery object/symbol/person, or any forced convergence of the accumulated mystery threads beyond the single SERPENTFIRE/fourth-stone hypothesis (all rejected).
- Any use of the word "angular" in reference to the fourth stone, or any other terminology merging it with the Angular Mark thread (rejected at Phase 3.1/3.2 as a standing project-wide ambiguity this task must not compound).
