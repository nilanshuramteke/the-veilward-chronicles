## Chapter Function

Chapter 17 gives Aurelia a genuine, unconditional opportunity to step back from the shared investigation to protect her evaluation standing — and has her choose, once, to remain involved instead. The pull to stay comes from two directions: Aarav's narrow, explicitly hedged administrative hypothesis connecting SERPENTFIRE and the general kind of case the Chapter 11 fourth stone represents, and Aurelia's own independent, physical discovery that the fourth stone hasn't weathered like its seven neighbors. Neither fact proves anything. Together, on a closing physical deadline (the Training Grounds regrading), they make leaving feel like abandoning a real question rather than completing an assignment. Aarav volunteers a brief, unguarded admission of uncertainty about the cost his own judgment is imposing on her — the chapter's single romance-coded beat. Aurelia weighs the real cost of continuing — her own time, Voss's spent goodwill, Dev's light consequence — and chooses it anyway, delivering the chapter outline's explicitly locked beat: her first active decision to trust Aarav, demonstrated through behavior, not declaration.
