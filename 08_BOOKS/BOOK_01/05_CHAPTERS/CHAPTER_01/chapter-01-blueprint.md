# Chapter 1 Blueprint — Fire Before Dawn

## POV
**Aurelia**

## Tense
Third-person limited, past tense.

## Chapter Function

The chapter must accomplish four things:

1. Establish Aurelia as a capable, competitive Academy student.
2. Reveal that confidence coexists with private pressure and fear of failing expectations.
3. Introduce Aarav as an established rival and immediate irritation.
4. Create anticipation for the major Academy evaluation without beginning the central mystery yet.

## Opening State

Aurelia enters the chapter:

- already established at Veilward Academy,
- preparing for a major Academy evaluation,
- confident in her abilities,
- carrying pressure she prefers not to show,
- accustomed to handling responsibility alone.

## External Objective

Prepare for and reach the beginning of the evaluation in control.

## Internal Objective

Prove to herself that she deserves confidence in her own ability.

## Core Tension

Aurelia wants control.

The approaching evaluation makes that control feel less certain than she allows anyone else to see.

## Aarav Dynamic

Aurelia and Aarav:

- already know each other,
- already have an established rivalry,
- are not enemies,
- are not strangers,
- should not behave with melodramatic hostility.

Their interaction should reveal:

- competitive friction,
- familiarity,
- conflicting methods,
- irritation sharpened by mutual awareness of ability.

## Emotional Movement

Beginning:

**Focused confidence**

↓

Middle:

**Private pressure exposed**

↓

Aarav interaction:

**Irritation and competitive focus**

↓

Ending:

**Determination sharpened by anticipation**

## Mystery Information

### Reveal
None of the central mystery.

### Withhold
- The Echo
- The Impossible Breach
- The recurring symbol
- Serpentfire
- The First Ember
- the hidden chamber
- Outer Dark information

Do not foreshadow these through artificial ominous language.

## Desired Reader Questions

By the end of the chapter, the reader should naturally wonder:

- What exactly will the evaluation test?
- Why does Aurelia care so much about succeeding?
- Why are Aurelia and Aarav rivals?
- Which of them is actually better?
- What happens when the competition becomes public?

## Ending Position

End with Aurelia entering, approaching, or fully committing herself to the evaluation sequence.

The ending should create forward movement into Chapter 2 and Chapter 3.

## Target Length

No fixed word count is locked.

Draft to the needs of:

- scene completeness,
- pacing,
- character establishment.

Do not pad description or internal monologue to reach an arbitrary length.
