# Claude Drafting Brief — Chapter 1: Fire Before Dawn

## Your Task

Draft Chapter 1 of *The Veilward Chronicles: Book One*.

Write the chapter in complete manuscript prose.

## Narrative Requirements

- Third-person limited
- Past tense
- Strictly Aurelia's viewpoint
- Clear, emotionally grounded prose
- Vivid but controlled imagery
- Natural sentence variation
- No exposition dumps

## Chapter Goal

Establish Aurelia, her ambition, and the private pressure beneath her confidence.

Introduce Aarav as an established rival through a competitive interaction that shows familiarity and mutual recognition of ability.

Build anticipation for a major Academy evaluation.

Do not begin the central mystery yet.

## Character Voice

### Aurelia

Her perspective should feel:

- direct,
- energetic,
- competitive,
- emotionally intense beneath restraint.

She should not be permanently sarcastic, angry, or melodramatic.

### Aarav

His dialogue should feel:

- measured,
- observant,
- capable of dry humor.

Do not give him access to private thoughts because this chapter belongs to Aurelia.

## Relationship State

They are rivals.

Their rivalry is based on:

- competition,
- assumptions,
- personality differences,
- conflicting methods,
- difficulty trusting one another.

They are not enemies.

Do not introduce romance or instant attraction.

## Writing Style

Prioritize:

1. Character emotion
2. Scene clarity
3. Sensory detail
4. Natural rhythm
5. Meaningful imagery

Describe what Aurelia notices and why it matters to her.

Do not inventory rooms, clothing, or magical architecture.

Mystery should come from genuine uncertainty, not artificial withholding.

## Avoid

- generic AI phrasing,
- repetitive sentence structures,
- constant dramatic fragments,
- excessive rhetorical questions,
- emotional over-explanation,
- fantasy exposition dumps,
- instant destined-love signals,
- chosen-one framing,
- melodramatic rivalry.

## Required Structural Movement

1. Before-dawn preparation
2. Academy environment waking around Aurelia
3. Competitive interaction with Aarav
4. Strong forward movement toward the evaluation

## Ending

End with anticipation.

The reader should want to continue into the evaluation.

Do not resolve the evaluation in this chapter.

## Before Delivering the Draft

Check:

- Is the entire chapter honestly inside Aurelia's perspective?
- Does Aurelia feel capable without feeling invulnerable?
- Does Aarav feel like an established rival rather than a stranger?
- Does the chapter avoid romance?
- Has the central mystery remained unrevealed?
- Does every scene move character, relationship, or plot forward?
