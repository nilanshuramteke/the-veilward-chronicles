# Chapter 1 Canon Constraints

## Timeline

Aurelia and Aarav:

- arrived at Veilward before Book One,
- already know each other,
- already developed a rivalry.

Do not write:

- first arrival at the Academy,
- first meeting,
- first rivalry.

## Relationship State

Current state:

**Rivals**

Allowed:

- competition,
- irritation,
- assumptions,
- conflicting approaches,
- recognition of competence.

Not allowed:

- trust,
- deep vulnerability shared with one another,
- friendship,
- romantic intimacy.

## Aurelia

Must remain:

- capable,
- competitive,
- proud,
- accustomed to carrying responsibility alone.

Her Book One growth begins from the belief that needing others may make her less reliable.

Do not make her helpless merely to create sympathy.

## Aarav

Must remain:

- observant,
- controlled,
- intelligent,
- emotionally measured.

Do not turn him into a cold or personality-less stereotype.

## Magic

Follow:

`08_BOOKS/BOOK_01/04_WORLD_AND_LOCATIONS/book-01-magic-usage-lock.md`

If Aurelia uses magic:

- it must be controlled and appropriate to her Book One ability,
- it must not reveal central mystery information,
- it must have believable limits.

Do not introduce:

- Serpentfire activation,
- new combined powers,
- unlimited Phoenix abilities,
- unexplained advanced mastery.

## Mystery Protection

Do not reveal or foreshadow through explicit clues:

- The Echo
- The Impossible Breach
- the recurring symbol
- Serpentfire
- the First Ember
- Elyra
- Varun

Chapter 1 establishes normal life before the disruption.

## Information Honesty

Remain inside Aurelia's perspective.

Do not hide thoughts Aurelia genuinely has merely to manufacture mystery.

## Supporting Cast

No supporting character appearance is required unless it serves the chapter naturally.

Do not force introductions simply to complete a cast checklist.
