# ANTAGONIST ARCHITECTURE

## Harbinger
Visible catalyst that destabilizes anchors and exploits fear. Stopped before the final climax.

## Seeker
Strategic force whose earned transformation culminates in self-sacrifice.

## Larger Intelligence
Remains immense and partly unknowable. It is never fully personified or defeated.

## Institutional Opposition
Fear and secrecy create conflict without requiring everyone to be malicious.
