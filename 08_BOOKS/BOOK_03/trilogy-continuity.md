# TRILOGY CONTINUITY

Book One established the external mystery and Aurelia/Aarav's trust. Book Two revealed the First Ember, challenged history, let the Seeker learn the Pattern, and brought in the Harbinger.

Locked rules: no chosen ones, no reincarnation, Serpentfire is philosophical, the Outer Dark remains partly unknowable, and the Veil is transformed rather than destroyed or restored.
