# SETUP FOR BOOK 03

Book Two must leave Book Three with unresolved consequences, not merely unanswered trivia.

## Established by Book Two
- The Veil can be breached.
- The Hidden World's official history cannot be trusted completely.
- The First Ember contains knowledge of the Veil's deeper structure.
- The Seeker is intelligent and strategic.
- The Outer Dark contains more than one kind of entity.

## Emotional Setup
Aurelia and Aarav enter Book Three together but aware that love does not remove conflict.

## Political Setup
Institutions must confront the consequences of their secrecy.

## Threat Setup
The Harbinger is now physically present.

## Central Book Three Question
If the old system of isolation is failing, what can replace it without destroying the worlds it was meant to protect?
