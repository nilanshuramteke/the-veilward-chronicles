# MAGIC DEVELOPMENT

## Book Two Focus
Magic becomes less about individual abilities and more about systems, resonance, memory, and boundaries.

## Lost Path Magic
The Path responds to:
- intent
- resonance
- cooperation

It cannot reliably be conquered through force.

## First Ember
The Ember reveals memory and pattern rather than functioning as unlimited power.

## Character Magic
Aurelia and Aarav should become more skilled, but power growth must not solve the central conflict.

## Serpentfire
Do not turn Serpentfire into a combat spell.

Its importance remains philosophical and historical.
