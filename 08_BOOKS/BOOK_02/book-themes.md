# BOOK THEMES

## Truth Has a Cost
The truth about Elyra and Varun is not comforting. Recovering it requires confronting the damage caused by institutions and by the choices of the people being defended.

## Connection Without Possession
Love is not proof of ownership. Aurelia and Aarav must learn to support one another without trying to control one another's choices.

## History Is a Story Told by Survivors
Records are incomplete. Institutions preserve what is useful to preserve.

## Difference Is Not Failure
Phoenix and Naga instincts create genuine disagreement. The story must not solve conflict by making either character abandon who they are.

## Knowledge Is Not Wisdom
The Seeker can understand patterns without understanding meaning.

## The Mystery of Boundaries
A boundary can protect. A boundary can imprison. The difference lies in why and how it is maintained.
