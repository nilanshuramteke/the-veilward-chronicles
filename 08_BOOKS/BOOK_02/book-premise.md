# BOOK PREMISE

After the events of Book One, Veilward Academy can no longer dismiss the disturbances around the Veil as isolated anomalies.

Aurelia and Aarav discover evidence that the disgraced Phoenix Elyra and the Naga Varun may have deliberately hidden the truth about an ancient network called the Lost Path.

Their search leads them toward the First Ember, a surviving fragment connected to the creation of the Veil.

But they are not the only ones searching.

An intelligence known only to the reader as the Seeker has been studying every disturbance, every choice, and every act of sacrifice. It does not need to steal the Ember.

It needs to understand how the Veil was made.

Book Two is a mystery, expedition, romance, and escalating supernatural threat centered on the dangerous difference between **finding the truth** and **understanding what the truth will cost**.
