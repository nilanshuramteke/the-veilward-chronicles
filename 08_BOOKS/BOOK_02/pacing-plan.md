# PACING PLAN

## Recommended Length
Approximately 32–36 chapters.

## Pacing Principle
Book Two should feel more expansive and mystical than Book One, but not slower.

## Rhythm
Investigation → discovery → consequence → relationship development → escalation.

## Mandatory Pressure Points
- Early anomaly
- First major historical contradiction
- First Lost Path entry
- Midpoint Ember revelation
- Major relationship fracture
- Seeker confirmation
- Climax activation
- Breach ending

## Quiet Chapters
Use quiet scenes for:
- intimacy
- reflection
- cultural worldbuilding
- moral disagreement

Quiet scenes must still change relationships or understanding.
