# AUTHOR NOTES

## Desired Reader Experience
Book Two should feel like opening a hidden door behind the story the reader thought they understood.

## Tone
More mystical than Book One.
More geographically expansive.
More emotionally intimate.
More intellectually dangerous.

## Do Not Over-Explain
The Outer Dark must remain partly unknowable.

## The Seeker
Do not make the Seeker theatrical.
Its danger comes from patience, intelligence, and observation.

## Romance
The romance should emerge naturally from accumulated trust and shared choices.

## Ending Philosophy
The ending must feel victorious for the characters and terrifying for the trilogy.

They protect the Ember.

They still lose something they did not know they were risking.

## Primary Author Reminder
Book Two is not about discovering a magical object.

It is about discovering that the past was hidden because the truth changes what the future must become.
