# REVELATION SCHEDULE

## Early Book
- Historical inconsistencies surrounding Elyra and Varun.
- Evidence that the Lost Path was deliberately damaged.
- The Academy does not possess the complete record.

## Early-Middle
- Surviving Lost Path routes exist.
- Elyra and Varun hid something rather than merely fleeing responsibility.
- Someone or something appears to be tracking disturbances.

## Midpoint
- The First Ember is confirmed.
- Its significance exceeds normal artifact magic.

## Late-Middle
- Elyra and Varun deliberately collapsed parts of the Path.
- Their choice prevented an immediate catastrophe.
- The Seeker's intelligence becomes undeniable.

## Climax
- The Ember responds to collective choice.
- The protagonists protect it.
- The Seeker observes the activation.

## Ending
- The Pattern is learned.
- The breach opens.
- The Harbinger enters.
