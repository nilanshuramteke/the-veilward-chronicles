# BOOK OBJECTIVES

## Story Objectives
1. Expand the story beyond Veilward Academy.
2. Reveal that the historical account of Elyra and Varun is incomplete.
3. Introduce the Lost Path as an active mystery and exploration system.
4. Reveal the First Ember.
5. Establish the Seeker as a strategic intelligence.
6. End with the Harbinger entering the Hidden World.

## Character Objectives
- Aurelia learns leadership without control.
- Aarav learns that patience is not the same as emotional withdrawal.
- Their friendship becomes a romantic relationship.
- Their relationship survives its first major ideological fracture.

## Trilogy Objectives
Book Two must answer meaningful Book One questions while creating larger Book Three questions.

The reader should finish with:
> We finally understand more of what happened.

Immediately followed by:
> Then the danger is much bigger than we thought.
