# MYSTERY ARCHITECTURE

## Central Mystery
Why did Elyra and Varun destroy access to the Lost Path?

## Layer One
The historical story is inconsistent.

## Layer Two
Elyra and Varun were trying to protect something.

## Layer Three
They discovered the First Ember.

## Layer Four
The Ember is connected to the creation and memory of the Veil.

## Layer Five
The protagonists' activation of the Ember is being observed.

## Final Reversal
The Seeker did not need possession of the Ember.

It needed to witness the Pattern.

## Mystery Rule
Every answer must feel inevitable in hindsight but not obvious in advance.
