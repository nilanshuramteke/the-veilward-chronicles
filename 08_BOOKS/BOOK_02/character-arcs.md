# CHARACTER ARCS

## Aurelia
### Beginning
Driven, protective, and increasingly willing to carry responsibility alone.

### Pressure
She discovers that leadership can become another form of control when she refuses to trust others with difficult choices.

### Growth
She learns that protecting someone does not mean deciding their path for them.

### End
Aurelia chooses trust even when she cannot guarantee the outcome.

## Aarav
### Beginning
Controlled, observant, and emotionally cautious.

### Pressure
His instinct is to wait, watch, and survive uncertainty.

### Growth
He learns that patience can become avoidance.

### End
Aarav chooses to openly commit, speak, and act before certainty arrives.

## Supporting Cast
Supporting characters should represent competing responses to truth:
- preserve stability
- reveal everything
- weaponize knowledge
- deny the past
