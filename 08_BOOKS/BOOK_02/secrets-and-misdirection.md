# SECRETS AND MISDIRECTION

## Productive Misdirection
Readers should initially suspect:
- a surviving political faction wants the Ember
- Elyra and Varun may have caused the disaster through recklessness
- the Lost Path itself may be the primary threat

## Hidden Truth
The greatest danger is the unseen observer learning from everyone else's actions.

## Fairness Rule
Do not hide information through author deception.

Clues must exist before revelations.

## False Beliefs
Characters may sincerely believe false history.

False belief is not the same as deliberate lying.

## Avoid
- surprise villains with no groundwork
- secret twins
- prophecy retcons
- unexplained personality reversals
