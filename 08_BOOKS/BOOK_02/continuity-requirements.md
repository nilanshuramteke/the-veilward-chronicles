# CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS

## Book One Carryover
- Aurelia and Aarav already trust one another more than they did initially.
- Their relationship begins Book Two as close friendship, not instant romance.
- The Echo has established that something beyond the known world is active.
- Veilward Academy has been changed by Book One events.

## Core Canon
- Shapeshifting species with mortal forms retain their single personal human face.
- True shapeshifters remain distinct and can assume different forms and faces.
- No chosen-one prophecy.
- No reincarnation connection between Elyra/Varun and Aurelia/Aarav.
- Serpentfire is not a spell.
- The First Ember cannot be controlled through bloodline.

## Book Two Ending Lock
The Seeker learns the Pattern of the Veil.
The Harbinger enters through a temporary breach.
