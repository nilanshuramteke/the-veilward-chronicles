# STAKES AND CONFLICT

## Personal Stakes
Aurelia and Aarav risk losing the trust they spent Book One building.

## Relationship Stakes
Their first major romantic conflict must arise from legitimate disagreement, not a manufactured misunderstanding.

## Institutional Stakes
The truth about Elyra and Varun threatens the credibility of established historical institutions.

## Magical Stakes
Damage to surviving Lost Path routes can destabilize nearby Veil anchors.

## Existential Stakes
The Seeker is learning how the Veil works.

## Escalation Rule
Every major answer should create a larger consequence.
