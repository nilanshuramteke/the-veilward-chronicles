# WORLD EXPANSION

## Book Two Expansion
The story must leave the Academy regularly.

Explore:
- hidden settlements
- damaged Veil locations
- archives outside institutional control
- ancient pathways
- communities affected by the Lost Path collapse

## Purpose
World expansion must reveal consequences.

Every new location should answer:
> How has the history of the Veil shaped the people who live here?

## Tone
Mystical, ancient, beautiful, and increasingly unsettling.

The Hidden World should feel larger than the Academy and older than its official history.
