# PLOT ARCHITECTURE

## Opening Movement
After Book One, new Veil disturbances and recovered records force the protagonists toward the mystery of Elyra and Varun.

## Investigation Movement
The group follows historical contradictions and discovers surviving traces of the Lost Path.

## Expedition Movement
The search leaves the Academy and becomes increasingly dangerous.

## Midpoint Revelation
The First Ember exists.

## Descent Movement
The truth about Elyra and Varun emerges. Institutional opposition intensifies. Aurelia and Aarav fracture over responsibility and risk.

## Final Movement
The protagonists reach the Ember and choose collective protection over individual advantage.

## Ending Reversal
They win the immediate battle.

The Seeker wins the strategic battle.

The Harbinger enters.
