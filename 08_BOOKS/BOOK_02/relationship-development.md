# RELATIONSHIP DEVELOPMENT

## Phase One: Familiarity
They are comfortable with one another after Book One, but neither treats the connection as guaranteed.

## Phase Two: Recognition
During the Lost Path investigation, they begin recognizing that their partnership has become emotionally intimate.

## Phase Three: Choice
Their first kiss or explicit romantic turning point should occur after a meaningful shared choice, not merely during danger.

## Phase Four: Fracture
They disagree about what to do with dangerous knowledge connected to the Ember.

Neither is entirely wrong.

## Phase Five: Rebuilding
They reconcile through honesty and changed behavior, not through an apology alone.

## End State
They are together, but their relationship remains vulnerable to the larger pressures that Book Three will bring.

## Rule
No destiny. No reincarnation. No magical bond forcing romance.
