# ANTAGONIST ARCHITECTURE

## The Seeker
The primary unseen antagonist of Book Two.

It is strategic, patient, and interested in patterns rather than domination.

Its objective is to understand the Pattern of the Veil.

## Institutional Opposition
Not every antagonist is supernatural.

Some characters oppose the protagonists because they believe:
- historical truth could destabilize society
- dangerous information should remain buried
- the Academy must protect its authority

## The Harbinger
Not a conventional active antagonist for most of the book.

It is the consequence waiting beyond the breach.

Its arrival is the Book Two ending escalation.
