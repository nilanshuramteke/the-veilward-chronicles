# CHAPTER REQUIREMENTS

Every chapter must accomplish at least one primary function:
- advance plot
- reveal character
- deepen mystery
- expand the world
- escalate danger
- alter a relationship

## Book Two Requirement
Most chapters should accomplish at least two functions.

## Chapter Endings
Prefer:
- new information
- a changed decision
- an emotional reversal
- an approaching consequence

Avoid artificial cliffhangers at the end of every chapter.

## Mystery Rule
A chapter may answer a question, but should usually introduce a consequence or better question.
