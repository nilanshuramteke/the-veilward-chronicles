# The Veilward Chronicles

> **A mythological fantasy romance series about identity, difference, memory, and the choices that bring two opposing forces together.**

---

## Premise

Humanity believes mythology belongs to the past. It doesn't.

Mythical creatures from cultures and mythologies across the world live secretly among humans, protected by an ancient magical boundary known as **the Veil**. At the center of the series is the **Veilward Academy**, a hidden institution that trains the next generation of **Veilwardens** — the order sworn to defend both worlds.

The story follows two rivals-turned-partners:

- **Aurelia**, a young Phoenix whose fire burns as brightly as the expectations placed upon her.
- **Aarav**, a young Naga gifted with water magic and a rare ability to perceive echoes of memory.

When their magic collides, it creates something that shouldn't exist — an ancient, forgotten power called **Serpentfire** — and pulls them into a mystery that history itself appears to have erased.

## The Golden Rule

> **Never let the lore become more important than the people.**

The mythology exists to support the characters. The mysteries exist to challenge them. The Academy exists to shape them. The romance exists because of who they become together.
