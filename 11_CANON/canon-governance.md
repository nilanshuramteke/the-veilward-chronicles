# Canon Governance

## Role of 11_CANON
`11_CANON` is the repository's operational canon register.

It does not duplicate every detail from the Story Bible. Its job is to record:
- active confirmed truths,
- canon changes,
- resolved contradictions,
- deliberately protected unknowns,
- current canon status.

## Official Status Vocabulary
This folder follows `00_CORE/canon-rules.md`:

- CONFIRMED
- PROVISIONAL
- OPEN QUESTION
- DELIBERATELY UNKNOWN
- RETCONNED
- NON-CANON
- DISPUTED (temporary audit use)

## Governance Rules
1. `00_CORE/canon-rules.md` defines canon policy.
2. `11_CANON/canon-authority-matrix.md` determines which source wins.
3. The Revelation Matrix controls reader-facing timing.
4. Author-hidden and author-final truths may override lower-level planning documents.
5. Every resolved contradiction should be recorded before the affected manuscript is drafted.

## What This Folder Must Not Become
- a second copy of the entire Story Bible,
- a brainstorming folder,
- a dumping ground for unverified ideas.

## Update Trigger
Update canon when:
- a previously provisional decision becomes confirmed,
- a contradiction is resolved,
- a manuscript event is formally promoted into canon,
- a retcon changes an established fact.
