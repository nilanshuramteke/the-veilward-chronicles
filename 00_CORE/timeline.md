# Master Timeline

## Timeline Rule
This is the master chronological framework. It records established sequence without exposing every protected detail.

## I. Earliest Ages
- The earliest conditions of the world predate reliable surviving history.
- The origins of the Outer Dark remain partly unknown.
- Some truths from this era remain deliberately undefined.

## II. Formation of the Veil
- The Veil becomes the defining boundary between the Human World and the Hidden World.
- Its original purpose and later failures are central to the trilogy's deeper mystery.
- The complete truth is protected by author-only canon.

## III. The Forgotten Age
- Historical continuity becomes fragmented.
- Records are lost, restricted, altered, or misunderstood.
- Elyra and Varun become associated with events whose full truth is later recovered.

## IV. The Lost Path Era
- The Lost Path becomes central to hidden movement, lost history, and later discoveries.
- Its complete history is revealed gradually across the trilogy.

## V. Pre-Series Academy Phase
- Aurelia and Aarav arrive at Veilward Academy before Book One.
- They meet and develop an academic and personal rivalry.
- Their rivalry is rooted in competition, assumptions, personality, and conflicting methods.
- Book One does **not** begin with their first arrival at the Academy.

## VI. Book One — The Discovery
- Aurelia and Aarav are established Academy students.
- A disturbance forces them into deeper cooperation.
- Rivalry develops into respect, trust, and friendship.
- The mystery expands beyond an apparent breach.
- Elyra, Varun, Serpentfire, and the First Ember become increasingly significant.
- The Book One ending establishes that the central mystery is larger than initially believed.

## VII. Book Two — The Descent
- The protagonists pursue deeper historical and geographical mysteries.
- The Lost Path and forgotten history become active parts of the investigation.
- Aurelia and Aarav move from friendship into emotional intimacy and love.
- The truth surrounding Elyra and Varun's historical sacrifice is revealed.
- The First Ember and the Seeker become central to the larger pattern.
- The First True Breach opens.
- The Harbinger enters the wider conflict.

## VIII. Book Three — The Choice
- The Harbinger crisis becomes the central immediate threat.
- The surviving truth of Elyra and Varun is discovered.
- The old assumptions surrounding the Veil are challenged.
- Victory over the immediate threat proves insufficient by itself.
- A collective choice determines the transformation of the Veil.
- The final truth remains governed by `00_CORE/author-final-truths.md`.

## IX. Post-Series
- The transformed world enters a new relationship with the boundary.
- Some mysteries remain intentionally unresolved.
- The Outer Dark is not fully explained.

## Status
The trilogy crisis is **CONFIRMED IN STRUCTURE**. Detailed revelations remain protected by the Revelation Matrix and author-only canon.
