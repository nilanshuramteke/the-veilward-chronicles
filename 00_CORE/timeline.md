# Timeline — The Veilward Chronicles

> **Document Type:** Core Series Timeline
> **Canon Status:** MIXED CANON
> **Scope:** Historical chronology, known eras, disputed history, and the present-day series timeline
> **Purpose:** Prevent chronological contradictions while preserving deliberate mysteries.

---

# 1. Timeline Philosophy

The history of **The Veilward Chronicles** is not completely known.

Some events are:

- Historically confirmed
- Partially documented
- Disputed
- Forgotten
- Deliberately concealed

This document establishes chronological order without prematurely defining mysteries.

> **An unknown event is not an empty space. It is a protected narrative space.**

---

# 2. Timeline Status System

## CONFIRMED
Established canon that should not be contradicted without an intentional series-level revision.

## PROVISIONAL
Working canon that may be refined later.

## DISPUTED
Historical accounts disagree about what happened.

## LOST / UNKNOWN
The event is believed to have occurred, but reliable details no longer exist.

## RESERVED
A deliberate timeline space kept open for future development.

# 4. The Earliest Age

## Status: RESERVED

This is the earliest known period of existence.

Questions intentionally left unanswered:

- How did mythical creatures first emerge?
- Did magic exist before the known world?
- Did the Veil exist?
- What existed beyond the protected world?

No definitive origin story should be established at this stage.

---

# 5. The Age Before the Veil

## Status: PROVISIONAL

There was a period before the modern Veil existed in its current form.

The relationship between mythical creatures, humans, magic, and Outer Dark threats remains largely unknown.

### Known Direction

The world was less protected than it is in the present era.

The exact nature of that vulnerability remains open.

---

# 6. The Formation of the Veil

## Status: CONFIRMED EVENT / UNKNOWN DETAILS

At some point in ancient history, the Veil came into existence or took on its present protective role.

### Confirmed

- The Veil is ancient.
- The Veil plays a central role in protecting the world.
- The Veil separates and protects different layers of reality.
- Its complete origin is not publicly known.

### Unknown

- Who created it?
- Was it created by one being or many?
- Was it always intended to protect humanity?
- Was it formed in response to the Outer Dark?

---

# 7. The Early Hidden World

Following the establishment of the Veil, mythical communities gradually developed systems for living alongside humanity while remaining hidden.

The exact chronology remains open.

# 35. Timeline Rules

All future documents must follow these rules.

## Rule One: Do Not Invent Dates Casually

Ancient history should not suddenly receive exact years unless the wider chronology requires them.

## Rule Two: Preserve Unknown Space

Unknown history is intentional.

Do not fill gaps merely to make the timeline look complete.

## Rule Three: Cause Before Consequence

Major events should have understandable chronological relationships.

## Rule Four: Character History Must Fit

Every future character biography must align with:

- The era
- The Academy timeline
- Established historical events

## Rule Five: No Secret Retcons

If an established event changes, update:

- `canon-rules.md`
- This timeline
- Every directly affected file

# 38. Final Timeline Principle

The history of the world should feel ancient.

But ancient does not mean every event needs an answer.

Some history survives.

Some history changes.

Some history disappears because someone wanted it forgotten.

And some history waits until the present is ready to discover it.

> **The past of The Veilward Chronicles is not finished. It is waiting to be understood.**
