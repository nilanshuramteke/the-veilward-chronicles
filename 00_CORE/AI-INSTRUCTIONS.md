# AI Instructions for The Veilward Chronicles

## Purpose
These instructions govern how AI may assist with planning, drafting, revising, and auditing within this repository.

## First Rule: Preserve Canon
Before generating new story material, consult the relevant source files.

Canon authority is governed by:
1. `00_CORE/canon-rules.md`
2. `11_CANON/canon-authority-matrix.md`
3. `07_SERIES_BLUEPRINT/revelation-matrix.md`

## AI Must Distinguish
- author truth,
- public knowledge,
- character knowledge,
- reader knowledge,
- future-book revelations.

A fact known to the author must not automatically appear in early-book prose.

## AI Must Not
- invent permanent canon to solve a gap,
- reveal later-book truths early,
- convert protected mysteries into exposition,
- treat rivals as generic enemies,
- contradict established magic, character, or world rules,
- silently overwrite a higher-authority source.

## Central Romance
Use the canonical progression:

**Rivals → Forced Cooperation → Respect → Trust → Friendship → Emotional Intimacy → Love → Conscious Commitment**

## When a Conflict Appears
1. Stop.
2. Identify the conflicting files.
3. Consult the Canon Authority Matrix.
4. Check the Revelation Matrix.
5. If unresolved, flag the issue rather than inventing a reconciliation.

## Separation of Responsibilities
- `00_CORE/AI-INSTRUCTIONS.md`: AI behavior and canon safety.
- `06_WRITING_GUIDE/`: prose craft and narrative execution.

## Drafting Principle
Generate only what the current book is allowed to reveal. Foreshadowing is permitted only when it does not confirm protected future truths.
