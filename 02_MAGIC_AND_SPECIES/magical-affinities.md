# Magical Affinities

> **Document Type:** Magic Classification
> **Canon Status:** PROVISIONAL FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

Magical affinity describes the natural relationship a creature has with particular forms of magic.

Affinity is not a ranking system.

## Core Principle

Different species experience magic differently.

An affinity may be connected to:

- Element
- Life force
- Transformation
- Perception
- Protection
- Movement
- Healing
- Ancestral inheritance

## Affinity Categories

The categories below are descriptive rather than rigid classes.

### Elemental Affinity
Connection with natural forces such as fire, water, air, earth, or related expressions.

### Vital Affinity
Magic connected to life force, renewal, healing, growth, or resilience.

### Transformative Affinity
Magic involving form, bodily transformation, concealment, or identity.

### Perceptive Affinity
Enhanced senses, magical awareness, truth perception, or environmental understanding.

### Protective Affinity
Wards, barriers, defensive magic, and resistance.

### Threshold Affinity
A rare or specialized relationship with boundaries, hidden pathways, and protected spaces.

## Multiple Affinities

A creature may demonstrate more than one affinity.

However, this should not automatically make them exceptionally powerful.

## Species and Individual Variation

Species traditions may have common affinities, but individuals remain different.

No character should be defined entirely by their species.

## Canon Boundary

Do not establish a universal elemental hierarchy.

> **Affinity explains tendency, not destiny.**
