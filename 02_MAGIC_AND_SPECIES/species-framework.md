# Species Framework

> **Document Type:** Species Design Standard
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

This document establishes how mythical species are represented across the series.

## Core Rule

A species is a people, not a personality template.

Every species file must distinguish between:

- Biological or magical traits
- Cultural traditions
- Social expectations
- Individual variation

## Required Species Sections

Every new species should define:

1. Overview
2. True Form
3. Mortal Form
4. Core affinities
5. Abilities
6. Limitations
7. Culture
8. Family and community
9. Relationships with other species
10. Individual variation
11. Open canon areas

## No Biological Destiny

Species traits may influence ability.

They must not automatically determine:

- Morality
- Intelligence
- Romantic compatibility
- Social worth
- Personality

## Mortal Form Rule

Ordinary mythical species possess one stable Mortal Form.

Their face and human identity remain consistent.

This distinguishes them from true shapeshifters.

## True Shapeshifters

True shapeshifters can alter:

- Appearance
- Face
- Identity presentation

Their abilities and limitations must be defined separately before introducing a major character of that type.

## Species Growth

The species registry should expand only when the story benefits from new species.

> **Mythology provides roots. Characters provide life.**
