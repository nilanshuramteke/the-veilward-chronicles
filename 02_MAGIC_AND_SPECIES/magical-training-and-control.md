# Magical Training and Control

> **Document Type:** Magic Practice Framework
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

This document defines how young mythical creatures learn to use power responsibly.

## Stages of Development

### 1. Recognition
The student learns what their abilities are and how they manifest.

### 2. Control
The student learns consistency and restraint.

### 3. Application
The student learns to use abilities in practical situations.

### 4. Integration
The student understands how power relates to identity, judgment, and responsibility.

## Training Principles

Training emphasizes:

- Control before intensity
- Understanding before experimentation
- Consent and safety
- Recovery after overuse
- Respect for different species

## Dangerous Training

Students are not encouraged to push beyond safe limits merely to prove strength.

Competition exists, but recklessness is not treated as courage.

## Individual Instruction

Some abilities require species-specific instruction.

This does not mean students should be isolated by species.

## Failure

Loss of control is a learning concern and sometimes a safety concern.

It should not automatically be treated as moral failure.

## Final Principle

> **Mastery is not the ability to use power at every opportunity. It is knowing when power should remain unused.**
