# Naga

> **Document Type:** Species Bible
> **Canon Status:** CORE FRAMEWORK / EXPANDABLE

## Overview

Nagas are mythical beings associated with serpentine form, ancient wisdom, hidden depths, protection, and transformation.

In The Veilward Chronicles, Nagas are a distinct people rather than a single stereotype.

---

# 1. True Form

A Naga possesses a true mythical form with serpentine characteristics.

The exact physical expression may vary by individual.

True Form is a natural identity, not a disguise.

---

# 2. Mortal Form

A Naga has one stable Mortal Form.

This form:

- Does not change casually
- Has a single consistent face
- Allows the Naga to live among humanity

Aarav's Mortal Form is uniquely his.

---

# 3. Core Affinities

Nagas may demonstrate affinities connected to:

- Perception
- Water or environment, where appropriate to individual tradition
- Physical transformation between Mortal and True Form
- Defensive instinct
- Magical awareness

No single ability should be assigned to every Naga without variation.

---

# 4. Senses

Many Nagas may possess heightened awareness.

This can include:

- Sensitivity to magical disturbance
- Environmental awareness
- Perception of danger

These abilities are not infallible.

---

# 5. Cultural Values

Naga communities may value:

- Patience
- Knowledge
- Loyalty
- Responsibility
- Careful judgment

These values must not become a stereotype that every Naga is quiet or wise.

---

# 6. Social Expectations

A Naga may experience pressure to be:

- Controlled
- Patient
- Strategic
- Emotionally reserved

This can conflict with an individual's natural personality.

---

# 7. Family and Tradition

Naga family and community structures should be developed with cultural diversity.

Aarav's family traditions will be defined in his character and extended species development.

---

# 8. Relationships with Other Species

Nagas interact with the broader Hidden World.

No automatic species-wide hostility toward Phoenixes is established.

Aurelia and Aarav's difficulties must remain grounded in their actual circumstances.

---

# 9. Limitations

Naga abilities may be affected by:

- Injury
- Exhaustion
- Environmental conditions
- Loss of concentration
- Overextension of magical senses

---

# 10. Aarav

Aarav is a male Naga.

He must remain an individual first.

His species influences his life but does not predetermine his choices.

---

# 11. Open Development Areas

- Exact True Form appearance
- Regional traditions
- Cultural homeland
- Water and environment affinities
- Ceremonies
- Family structures

> **Depth should never be mistaken for silence, and patience should never be mistaken for weakness.**
