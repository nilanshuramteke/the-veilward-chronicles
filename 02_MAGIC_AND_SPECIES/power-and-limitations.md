# Power and Limitations

> **Document Type:** Magic Framework
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

Power in this world must create responsibility, risk, and meaningful limits.

No ability should exist without boundaries.

## Core Rules

### Power Is Not Unlimited
Every creature has limits determined by:

- Natural affinity
- Training
- Experience
- Physical and emotional condition
- Environmental circumstances

### Greater Power Requires Greater Control
Raw strength does not equal mastery.

### Exhaustion Is Real
Heavy magical use can cause:

- Physical fatigue
- Reduced control
- Temporary inability to access certain abilities
- Emotional instability when power is strongly tied to feeling

### Magic Cannot Solve Every Problem
Magic cannot automatically:

- Undo every injury
- Restore every loss
- Reveal every truth
- Override another person's free will

## Emotional Influence

Strong emotions can affect magical control.

This is especially important for creatures whose powers are naturally expressive or elemental.

Emotion may amplify power, but amplification is not automatically beneficial.

## Training

Veilward Academy teaches students to understand:

- Capacity
- Precision
- Recovery
- Consequences
- When not to use power

## Canon Rule

Future species files must define abilities using this structure:

1. Core abilities
2. Advanced abilities
3. Limitations
4. Costs
5. Training requirements
6. Dangerous misuse

> **Power becomes meaningful when a character must decide whether using it is worth the cost.**
