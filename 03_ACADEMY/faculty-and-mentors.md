# Faculty and Mentors of Veilward Academy

> **Document Type:** Academy Character Framework
> **Canon Status:** PROVISIONAL FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

Faculty members are not a single-minded authority.

They represent different generations, philosophies, species backgrounds, and beliefs about education.

## Faculty Roles

### Instructors
Teach academic, magical, and practical disciplines.

### Mentors
Provide longer-term guidance and personal support.

### Wardens
Responsible for safety, boundaries, and serious disciplinary concerns.

### Archivists and Researchers
Preserve knowledge and investigate historical questions.

## Philosophical Differences

Faculty may disagree about:

- Tradition versus reform
- How much freedom students deserve
- The role of combat training
- Whether dangerous knowledge should be restricted
- How openly the Academy should discuss forgotten history

## Mentor Principle

A mentor should challenge a student without controlling their life.

Mentors may be flawed.

They may misunderstand students.

They may also change.

## Character Development

Named faculty should only be created when they serve a meaningful role in:

- Aurelia's growth
- Aarav's growth
- The Serpentfire mystery
- Academy life
- The wider series

> **Wisdom is not the absence of flaws.**
