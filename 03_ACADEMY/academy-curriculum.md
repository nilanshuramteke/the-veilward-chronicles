# Veilward Academy Curriculum

> **Document Type:** Academy Reference
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

Veilward Academy educates students for life, responsibility, and service. It is not merely a combat school.

## The Four Educational Pillars

### Self
Students learn identity, emotional awareness, control, and responsibility.

### World
Students study the Hidden World, humanity, history, and other cultures.

### Power
Students learn magical theory, practical control, safety, and the limits of their abilities.

### Duty
Students explore protection, ethics, judgment, and responsibility toward others.

## Core Areas of Study

- Magical foundations
- Species and cultural studies
- Hidden World history
- Human world studies
- Practical defense
- Ethics and responsibility
- Magical control
- Research and archives

## Advanced Study

Older students may pursue:

- Specialized magical disciplines
- Research
- Defensive preparation
- Cultural studies
- Individual mentorship

## Assessment

Students are evaluated through more than examinations.

Assessment may include:

- Practical demonstrations
- Teamwork
- Judgment under pressure
- Research
- Reflection
- Responsible use of power

> **Knowledge without judgment is not education.**
