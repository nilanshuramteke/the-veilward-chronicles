# Veilward Academy History

> **Document Type:** Academy History
> **Canon Status:** MIXED CANON

## Purpose

This document tracks the historical development of Veilward Academy.

It complements `00_CORE/timeline.md`.

## Founding

The Academy is ancient.

Its exact founder, date, and founding crisis remain unknown.

## Early Purpose

The earliest purpose of the Academy involved education, guidance, and preparation for responsibility.

It was never intended to be only a military institution.

## Growth

Over generations, Veilward expanded its role to include:

- Cultural exchange
- Research
- Magical education
- Protective preparation

## Historical Adaptation

The Academy changed as:

- The Hidden World changed
- New generations arrived
- Human civilization evolved

## Forgotten Records

Some Academy history is incomplete.

The reasons may include:

- Lost archives
- Restricted records
- Historical damage
- Deliberate concealment

This becomes relevant to the wider series.

## Elyra, Varun, and Serpentfire

Their connection to Academy history is not fully defined here.

Future historical canon must establish their relationship carefully.
