# Veilward Academy Traditions and Culture

> **Document Type:** Academy Cultural Reference
> **Canon Status:** PROVISIONAL / EXPANDABLE

## Purpose

The Academy has accumulated traditions across generations.

Tradition creates belonging, but tradition should also be questioned when necessary.

## Core Values

Veilward emphasizes:

- Honor
- Responsibility
- Knowledge
- Mutual respect
- Protection without domination

## Traditions

Specific traditions should develop naturally as the story requires them.

Possible categories include:

- Beginning-of-year ceremonies
- Graduation traditions
- Remembrance events
- Academic challenges
- Student celebrations

## Honor

Honor at Veilward should not mean blind obedience.

A student may act honorably while questioning authority.

## Competition

Competition may exist through:

- Academic challenges
- Training exercises
- Team events
- Research recognition

Competition must not create a permanent house-based social hierarchy.

## Remembrance

The Academy preserves memory through:

- Archives
- Memorial spaces
- Stories
- Ceremonies

The contrast between remembered and forgotten history is important to the series.

## Cultural Exchange

Students bring traditions from their homes.

The Academy does not erase those identities.

> **Unity is not created by making everyone the same.**
