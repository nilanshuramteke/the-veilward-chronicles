# Academy Rules and Discipline

> **Document Type:** Academy Governance Reference
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

Veilward Academy requires rules because students possess abilities that can create real consequences.

## Core Expectations

Students are expected to:

- Respect the safety of others
- Use magic responsibly
- Follow protected-area restrictions
- Avoid reckless exposure of the Hidden World
- Respect consent and personal boundaries
- Take responsibility for mistakes

## Discipline Philosophy

Discipline should consider:

- Intent
- Consequences
- Knowledge
- Pattern of behavior
- Ability to understand the harm caused

## Possible Responses

Depending on severity:

- Reflection and guidance
- Restitution
- Restricted privileges
- Additional training
- Formal disciplinary review

## Serious Violations

Serious cases may involve:

- Dangerous misuse of magic
- Deliberate harm
- Major secrecy breaches
- Accessing restricted knowledge

## Authority

Academy discipline does not automatically replace wider Hidden World law.

> **Rules exist to protect growth, not to demand obedience for its own sake.**
