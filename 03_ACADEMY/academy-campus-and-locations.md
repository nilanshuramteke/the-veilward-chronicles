# Veilward Academy Campus and Locations

> **Document Type:** Academy Location Reference
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED / EXPANDABLE

## Purpose

This document organizes the Academy's important physical spaces.

For the broader Academy philosophy, see `veilward-academy.md`.

## Heart Hall

A central gathering space associated with:

- Major announcements
- Community events
- Shared ceremonies

## The Archive

A major repository of:

- History
- Research
- Restricted knowledge

The Archive may become important to the Serpentfire mystery.

## Training Grounds

Used for:

- Practical defense
- Controlled magical training
- Team exercises

## Form Sanctuaries

Protected spaces designed for:

- True Form practice
- Physical adaptation
- Species-specific requirements

## Observatory

A location associated with observation, study, and potentially celestial or Veil-related phenomena.

Its deeper functions remain open.

## Threshold Hall

A controlled area connected to protected routes and travel.

Access is regulated.

## Residential Areas

Living spaces are designed to encourage community while respecting different needs.

## Open Court

An informal social space where students can:

- Meet
- Study
- Relax
- Observe Academy life

## Future Locations

New locations should only be added when they serve story or character needs.

> **A campus location matters when characters leave part of themselves there.**
