# Student Life at Veilward Academy

> **Document Type:** Academy Social Reference
> **Canon Status:** CONFIRMED FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

Students at Veilward are young people before they are future protectors.

Academy life includes friendship, conflict, humor, pressure, romance, and ordinary routines.

## Residential Life

Students live in mixed communities rather than rigid species houses.

This encourages:

- Friendship across species
- Cultural understanding
- Shared responsibility

Accommodation may be adapted to individual physical or magical needs.

## Daily Rhythm

A typical day may include:

- Classes
- Training
- Meals
- Independent study
- Social time
- Personal practice

The exact schedule remains flexible for story needs.

## Friendship

Friendships often cross cultural boundaries.

Not every friendship needs to be politically or magically significant.

## Romance

Student relationships are treated as part of ordinary life.

Inter-species romance may attract differing reactions depending on family and community traditions.

## Conflict

Students compete, argue, make mistakes, and reconcile.

The Academy should never feel like a perfectly harmonious institution.

## Aurelia and Aarav

Their relationship develops within ordinary Academy life.

Private conversations, shared work, arguments, friendships, and moments of vulnerability are as important as major magical events.

> **The Academy becomes memorable because students grow there, not because every day contains a crisis.**
