# 12_RESEARCH

## Purpose
A dedicated home for external research, source notes, inspiration, unanswered questions, and research-driven decisions for **The Veilward Chronicles**.

Research is not automatically story canon.

## Information Flow
Research → Story Decision → Story Bible → Manuscript → Established Canon

## Rules
- Keep factual research separate from creative adaptation.
- Record sources where practical.
- Do not treat inspiration as canon.
- Promote important decisions into the appropriate Story Bible file.
- If research changes established manuscript canon, record the change in `11_CANON`.
