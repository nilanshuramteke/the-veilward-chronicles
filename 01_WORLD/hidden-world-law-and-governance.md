# Hidden World Law and Governance

> **Document Type:** Social and Political Framework  
> **Canon Status:** MIXED CANON  
> **Scope:** Governance principles, law, authority, and inter-community responsibility

## Purpose

The Hidden World requires systems for cooperation.

This document establishes the framework without prematurely defining a complete political map.

---

# 1. Core Principle

The Hidden World is not governed as a single empire.

Different communities retain varying degrees of autonomy.

Shared systems exist where cooperation is necessary.

> **Unity does not require uniformity.**

---

# 2. Levels of Authority

## Community Authority

Local communities manage their own:

- Customs
- Internal affairs
- Family traditions
- Local disputes

## Shared Authority

Matters affecting multiple communities may require:

- Inter-community agreements
- Councils
- Recognized mediators
- Shared institutions

The exact structure remains open.

---

# 3. No Universal Species Hierarchy

No species should possess automatic legal superiority simply because of:

- Age
- Magical strength
- Historical reputation
- Population size

Social influence may still exist.

Legal principle and social reality are not always identical.

---

# 4. Core Shared Laws

The modern Hidden World broadly recognizes responsibilities concerning:

- Protection of life
- Prohibition of deliberate harm
- Responsible use of dangerous magic
- Protection of children and vulnerable individuals
- Preservation of secrecy where necessary

Specific legal codes remain open.

---

# 5. Human Exposure

## Status: CONFIRMED PRINCIPLE

Deliberately exposing the Hidden World to humanity can create serious consequences.

However, circumstances matter.

The system should distinguish between:

- Accidents
- Recklessness
- Deliberate exposure
- Necessary intervention to save lives

---

# 6. True Shapeshifters and Identity

True shapeshifters cannot be legally treated as suspicious merely because their abilities differ.

Governance and security systems must account for identity complexity without creating species discrimination.

---

# 7. Magical Harm

The seriousness of magical harm depends on:

- Intent
- Knowledge
- Consequences
- Ability to control the magic

Accidental loss of control should not automatically be treated as deliberate violence.

---

# 8. Inter-Species Disputes

Disputes involving multiple communities may require neutral processes.

Potential mechanisms include:

- Mediation
- Inter-community councils
- Shared legal review

Specific institutions remain open for future development.

---

# 9. Veilward Academy Jurisdiction

The Academy governs student life within its institutional responsibilities.

It is not automatically above the wider laws of the Hidden World.

The Academy may handle:

- Academic discipline
- Student safety
- Internal rule violations

Serious external matters may involve wider authorities.

---

# 10. Inter-Species Relationships

Inter-species relationships are not universally illegal or forbidden.

Social and cultural resistance may still exist.

The distinction is important:

> **Social disapproval is not the same as legal prohibition.**

---

# 11. Governance and Imperfection

Hidden World institutions can:

- Make mistakes
- Preserve outdated systems
- Protect influential groups
- Resist change

Law should not be portrayed as automatically just simply because it exists.

---

# 12. Open Governance Questions

Reserved for future development:

- The name of any central council
- Number of governing regions
- Election or appointment systems
- Relationship between communities and shared authorities
- Formal court structures
- Defender oversight

---

# 13. Final Principle

The Hidden World needs law because power without responsibility becomes dangerous.

But laws must remain capable of change.

> **A tradition becomes unjust when its survival matters more than the people living under it.**
