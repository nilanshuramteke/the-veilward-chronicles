# Locations and Landmarks

> **Document Type:** World Reference  
> **Canon Status:** PROVISIONAL / EXPANDABLE  
> **Scope:** Important specific locations within the Hidden World

## Purpose

This document tracks named locations.

It complements `hidden-world-geography.md`.

- Geography defines the broad structure of the world.
- This document records specific places that matter to the story.

---

# 1. Location Design Rule

A location should only become fully established when it serves:

- Character development
- Plot
- World-building
- Historical significance

Do not create a large catalogue of locations merely to make the world appear larger.

# 7. Human World Locations

Specific human cities should only be named when relevant to the plot.

The story should avoid accidentally creating contradictions regarding:

- Human geography
- Travel distance
- Hidden World access

---

# 8. Location Continuity Rules

Every major location added to the series should establish:

- Where it is broadly connected
- How it is accessed
- Who can enter
- Whether humans can perceive it
- Relevant historical significance

---

# 9. Final Principle

Locations should feel meaningful because characters have memories there.

A beautiful location without narrative significance is decoration.

> **A place becomes part of the story when something changes there.**
