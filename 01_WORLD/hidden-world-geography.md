# Hidden World Geography — The Veilward Chronicles

> **Document Type:** World Foundation  
> **Canon Status:** MIXED CANON  
> **Scope:** Geographic structure of the Hidden World, major territorial categories, travel, thresholds, and the provisional placement of important institutions  
> **Purpose:** Give the Hidden World a believable physical and magical geography without prematurely fixing every future location.

---

# 1. Purpose

The Hidden World does not exist on a separate planet.

It exists across the same Earth occupied by humanity.

Its geography is therefore a combination of:

- Physical places
- Hidden territories
- Veiled sanctuaries
- Magical thresholds
- Routes unavailable to ordinary humans

A human map is incomplete.

A Hidden World map is also incomplete.

No single map can show every place because some locations are:

- Concealed
- Restricted
- Mobile
- Accessible only under certain conditions

> **In the Hidden World, geography is not defined only by where a place is. It is also defined by who is able to reach it.**

---

# 2. Geographic Principles

All future locations should follow these principles.

## Principle One: Earth Is Shared

Hidden territories exist upon, beneath, around, or alongside the physical world.

## Principle Two: Hidden Does Not Mean Another Dimension

Most important locations should have a relationship with real geography, even when magical access is required.

## Principle Three: Access Is Part of Location

A sanctuary may be physically nearby but impossible to reach without the correct path or permission.

## Principle Four: Geography Creates Culture

Species and communities should not feel randomly placed. Environment should influence history and tradition.

## Principle Five: The World Should Expand Gradually

Book I should focus on a limited number of places. Later books can reveal the wider Hidden World.

---

# 3. The Geographic Layers

The Hidden World can be understood through four broad geographic layers.

## Layer I: The Visible Earth

The ordinary world experienced by humanity.

Examples:

- Cities
- Forests
- Mountains
- Oceans
- Deserts

Mythical creatures may move through these places in their Mortal Forms.

---

## Layer II: Overlapping Territories

Locations physically connected to the human world but concealed through the Veil.

Examples:

- A hidden district beside a major city
- A valley humans repeatedly fail to enter
- An ancient settlement concealed within a forest

---

## Layer III: Veiled Sanctuaries

Locations protected by stronger magical boundaries.

Access may require:

- Permission
- Magical recognition
- A guide
- Knowledge of a threshold

---

## Layer IV: Boundary Regions

Remote or heavily protected areas associated with:

- Ancient defensive systems
- Dangerous magical activity
- Weak points in the Veil
- The Outer Dark

These regions should remain mysterious during the early series.

---

# 4. Types of Hidden Territories

The Hidden World contains several broad categories of territory.

## Sanctuaries

Permanent protected settlements or regions.

## Enclaves

Smaller communities hidden within or near human civilization.

## Wild Territories

Regions where magical creatures live without centralized institutions.

## Threshold Regions

Areas containing important routes between territories.

## Boundary Wards

Highly protected locations associated with Veil defense.

---

# 5. The Veilward Academy Region

## CURRENT STATUS: PROVISIONAL CANON

The Veilward Academy should be located in a geographically dramatic but believable region.

The preferred model is:

> **A hidden mountain valley connected to the wider world through multiple controlled Thresholds.**

The Academy should not feel like a castle floating outside reality.

It should feel ancient, inhabited, and geographically grounded.

---

# 6. The Academy Valley

### Working Name: The Veilward Valley

The Veilward Academy is situated within a vast concealed valley.

The valley is surrounded by:

- High mountains
- Dense forests
- Ancient stone formations
- Natural magical currents

Ordinary humans cannot easily locate or enter it.

The exact real-world geographic placement remains intentionally unspecified until the wider series geography is finalized.

---

# 7. Why the Academy Is Hidden in a Valley

The valley provides several advantages.

## Natural Protection

Mountains and forests create physical isolation.

## Magical Stability

The region may possess naturally strong magical currents.

## Multiple Routes

Thresholds can connect the Academy to distant parts of the Hidden World.

## Training Environment

The surrounding wilderness allows students to train safely.

---

# 8. The Veilward Academy Grounds

The Academy grounds should eventually be developed in a dedicated institution file.

At the geographic level, the campus occupies a large area within the valley.

Important areas may include:

- Main academic buildings
- Student residences
- Training grounds
- A central gathering space
- Libraries and archives
- Restricted historical areas
- Access points to surrounding wilderness

The Academy should feel like:

> **A living institution built into an ancient landscape rather than a single isolated building.**

---

# 9. The Academy's Relationship to the Valley

The Academy does not control the entire valley.

The surrounding environment has its own history and dangers.

Some areas may be:

- Open to students
- Restricted
- Protected
- Dangerous

This creates opportunities for exploration without requiring students to leave the Academy region constantly.

---

# 10. Major Geographic Regions

The following regions are recommended as the first major geographic framework for the series.

These are intentionally broad and should be expanded only when needed.

---

# 11. The Central Hidden Network

The Central Hidden Network is not necessarily a political capital.

It is a loose geographic concept representing the territories most connected through:

- Institutions
- Trade
- Travel
- Threshold routes

The Veilward Academy may serve as one important connection point.

---

# 12. The Ember Territories

## STATUS: PROVISIONAL

Regions historically associated with fire-aligned and heat-adapted mythical species.

Possible environments include:

- Volcanic regions
- High-temperature valleys
- Ancient deserts

The Ember Territories should not be defined as exclusively Phoenix territory.

Multiple species may live there.

This avoids turning geography into species ownership.

---

# 13. The Serpent Sanctuaries

## STATUS: PROVISIONAL

A network of old sanctuaries and communities associated with various serpent and Naga traditions.

These may exist near:

- Rivers
- Lakes
- Underground waterways
- Ancient forests

Aarav's family history may eventually connect to one such region.

Specific locations remain intentionally undefined.

---

# 14. The Skybound Regions

## STATUS: PROVISIONAL

Mountain ranges, high plateaus, and remote aerial territories associated with species comfortable in extreme elevation.

These regions may contain:

- Ancient nesting sites
- Wind currents with magical significance
- Isolated settlements

Not every flying creature belongs to these regions.

---

# 15. The Deepwater Territories

## STATUS: PROVISIONAL

Hidden regions associated with aquatic and amphibious species.

These may include:

- Concealed lakes
- Deep rivers
- Coastal sanctuaries
- Underwater settlements

Access and political structures should be developed separately.

---

# 16. The Ancient Wilds

The Ancient Wilds are broad regions where the Hidden World has limited institutional control.

These territories may contain:

- Old magical ecosystems
- Independent creatures
- Ruins
- Unstable magical locations

They should create the feeling that:

> **Not every part of the world has been organized, understood, or made safe.**

---

# 17. The Boundary Marches

## STATUS: PROVISIONAL

The Boundary Marches are regions closer to important defensive boundaries.

They may contain:

- Veilwarden stations
- Ancient wards
- Restricted routes
- Evidence of past incursions

Students are unlikely to visit freely.

These regions become more important as the series expands.

---

# 18. The Outer Boundary

The Outer Boundary is not currently mapped completely.

It represents the uncertain geographic and magical edge where the known world becomes vulnerable to the influence of the Outer Dark.

Important rule:

> **The Outer Dark should not initially be treated as a simple neighboring country or place reachable by ordinary travel.**

Its relationship with geography and reality remains an Open Question.

---

# 19. Human Cities and Hidden Enclaves

Mythical creatures do not only live in isolated wilderness.

Some Hidden World communities exist near human population centers.

These may be:

- Small
- Temporary
- Permanent
- Culturally diverse

An enclave may exist within:

- An old district
- A protected underground space
- A forgotten section of a city

Future locations should avoid making every major human city secretly contain an enormous magical kingdom.

Most enclaves should remain modest.

---

# 20. Living Among Humans

Many individual mythical creatures live directly within human society.

Their geographic identity may therefore be divided between:

- Their ancestral territory
- Their current human residence

This creates a meaningful distinction between:

> **Where someone comes from and where they belong.**

---

# 21. Travel Through the Hidden World

The Hidden World does not rely on one universal transportation system.

Travel may occur through:

- Ordinary human transportation
- Personal movement
- Flying where appropriate
- Water routes
- Hidden roads
- Magical Thresholds

Different travel methods should have different advantages and limitations.

---

# 22. Thresholds

A Threshold is a geographic and magical transition point.

Thresholds connect:

- Hidden territories
- Sanctuaries
- Academy routes
- Protected regions

Not every Threshold is a portal.

Some may simply reveal a path that ordinary perception cannot recognize.

---

# 23. Categories of Threshold

## Open Thresholds

Controlled routes regularly used by authorized travelers.

## Restricted Thresholds

Routes requiring special permission.

## Ancient Thresholds

Old routes whose original purpose may no longer be fully understood.

## Unstable Thresholds

Routes affected by magical disturbances.

---

# 24. Threshold Rules

Every important Threshold should eventually define:

1. Where it leads
2. Who can use it
3. What activates it
4. What happens if it fails
5. Who is responsible for protecting it

This prevents magical travel from becoming an unlimited shortcut.

---

# 25. Travel Time Still Matters

A Threshold may reduce physical distance, but travel should not become meaningless.

Characters may still face:

- Waiting periods
- Authorization
- Limited routes
- Dangerous destinations

Travel must retain narrative weight.

---

# 26. Maps

The Hidden World may use multiple kinds of maps.

## Physical Maps

Showing geographic terrain.

## Threshold Maps

Showing authorized travel routes.

## Restricted Maps

Containing dangerous or classified locations.

## Living Maps

A possible future magical concept that should remain provisional.

No single map should automatically reveal the entire world.

---

# 27. Why Geography Is Partly Secret

Knowledge of a location can be dangerous.

Maps may be restricted because they reveal:

- Sanctuaries
- Weak boundaries
- Defensive systems
- Ancient routes

This creates tension between exploration and security.

---

# 28. The Academy as a Geographic Crossroads

The Veilward Academy is important partly because students from distant territories can reach it.

Its location should provide access through a network of controlled routes.

However, the Academy should not be easy to reach without authorization.

This reinforces its role as:

- A school
- A sanctuary
- A protected institution

---

# 29. The Student Arrival Experience

Students may arrive at the Academy through different Threshold routes.

This provides an opportunity to show:

- Cultural diversity
- Geographic scale
- Different travel traditions

A student arriving from a distant sanctuary may have a very different journey from one raised near human civilization.

---

# 30. Geographic Influence on Culture

Geography should influence communities naturally.

A mountain community may value:

- Isolation
- Endurance
- Navigation

A river community may value:

- Memory
- Movement
- Seasonal change

These are cultural influences, not biological rules.

---

# 31. Climate and Magical Ecology

Magic may affect local environments.

However, magical geography should not become visually random.

Each unusual ecosystem should have:

- A history
- A source
- Limitations

Examples might include:

- Permanently warm springs
- Unusual migration patterns
- Forests with heightened magical activity

---

# 32. The Academy Wilderness

The wilderness surrounding the Veilward Academy is important for Book I.

It should include:

- Forested regions
- Mountain paths
- Streams or lakes
- Training clearings
- Restricted areas

This allows students to encounter danger without immediately traveling across the world.

---

# 33. Restricted Areas Near the Academy

At least one significant region near the Academy should be restricted.

The reason should initially be incomplete.

Possible purposes include:

- Historical danger
- Unstable Veil activity
- Ancient ruins

The exact choice should be finalized during Book I blueprint development.

---

# 34. Geographic Mysteries

The world should contain places whose importance is not immediately understood.

Examples include:

- A Threshold no longer shown on modern maps
- A sanctuary that vanished from records
- A region where Mortal Forms become difficult to maintain

These should be used selectively.

Mystery is stronger when the world otherwise feels consistent.

---

# 35. Geographic Scale Across the Trilogy

## Book I

Primary focus:

- Veilward Academy
- Veilward Valley
- Nearby wilderness
- Limited human-world locations

## Book II

Expansion:

- Additional sanctuaries
- Important ancestral territories
- Greater travel across the Hidden World

## Book III

Expansion:

- Boundary regions
- Ancient geographic secrets
- Locations central to the Veil and Outer Dark

This structure keeps the world large without overwhelming the first book.

---

# 36. Current Confirmed Canon

The following is **CONFIRMED CANON**:

- The Hidden World exists on the same Earth as humanity.
- Hidden locations may physically overlap with the human world.
- The Veil can conceal locations and restrict access.
- The Veilward Academy is a hidden institution.
- The Academy trains students from diverse mythical backgrounds.
- Thresholds exist as important routes or transition points.
- The Hidden World contains geographically diverse territories.
- Not all mythical creatures live in isolated sanctuaries.
- Some mythical creatures live among humans.
- The Outer Dark is associated with dangerous boundary regions.

---

# 37. Current Provisional Canon

The following is **PROVISIONAL CANON**:

- The Academy is located within a concealed mountain valley.
- The valley is surrounded by mountains and wilderness.
- The Academy region connects to other territories through controlled Thresholds.
- The Ember Territories exist as a broad fire-associated geographic category.
- The Serpent Sanctuaries exist as a network of ancient communities.
- Skybound Regions and Deepwater Territories are useful future geographic categories.
- The Ancient Wilds contain regions with limited institutional control.
- The Boundary Marches contain important defensive locations.
- A restricted region exists near the Academy.

- Is the Academy geographically stationary?

## Major Territories

- Which cultures originate in each region?

## Travel

- How common are Thresholds?
- Who maintains them?
- Can Thresholds be permanently destroyed?

## Geography and the Veil

- Are some locations naturally Veiled?
- What happens to geography when a major boundary fails?

## The Outer Boundary

- Does it exist at one location or multiple vulnerable regions?
- Can physical travel approach it?

# 39. Canon Boundaries

Do not establish without deliberate series-level approval:

- The precise real-world location of the Veilward Academy.
- A complete global map of the Hidden World.
- A single capital governing all mythical communities.
- The complete geography of the Outer Dark.
- Permanent species ownership of major territories.

The world must remain expandable.

# 41. Final Geographic Principle

The Hidden World should never feel like a collection of random fantasy locations.

Every place should answer at least three questions:

> **Why is it here?**

> **Who lives here?**

> **What history made it important?**

The world is hidden from humanity.

It should never feel hidden from itself.

> **A map can show where a journey begins. In the Hidden World, the harder question is whether the path will allow you to reach where you are going.**
