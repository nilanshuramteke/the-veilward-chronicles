# World Technology and Modern Life

> **Document Type:** Modern World-Building Framework  
> **Canon Status:** MIXED CANON  
> **Scope:** Technology, daily life, modern society, and magical adaptation

## Purpose

The Veilward Chronicles takes place in a modern world.

Mythical creatures are ancient, but they do not live outside time.

This document defines how the Hidden World interacts with contemporary life.

---

# 1. Core Principle

The Hidden World exists alongside modern civilization.

Mythical creatures may understand and use:

- Smartphones
- Computers
- The internet
- Modern transportation
- Contemporary media

Magic does not automatically replace useful technology.

---

# 2. Technology at Veilward Academy

## Status: CONFIRMED DIRECTION

The Academy is ancient but technologically capable.

Students may use appropriate technology for:

- Communication
- Research
- Education
- Personal life

Technology use is controlled where secrecy and security require it.

---

# 3. The Internet and Secrecy

The internet creates one of the modern Hidden World's greatest challenges.

A single image or video can potentially expose:

- True Forms
- Magical events
- Hidden locations

Therefore, modern secrecy requires more than traditional concealment.

---

# 4. Digital Evidence

The Hidden World must account for:

- Cameras
- Smartphones
- Surveillance
- Social media
- Viral content

The exact systems used to manage accidental exposure remain open.

They should not become an unrealistic ability to erase every human memory or digital record effortlessly.

---

# 5. Social Media

Mythical creatures who live among humans may use social media.

However, they must maintain identities consistent with:

- Their fixed Mortal Form
- Their human-facing life
- Hidden World secrecy

Social media creates opportunities for:

- Friendship
- Humor
- Romance
- Mistakes
- Exposure risk

---

# 6. Modern Identity

A fixed Mortal Form means a mythical creature can maintain a consistent human identity.

This may include:

- Education records
- Employment
- Banking
- Housing

The precise systems supporting such identities remain open.

---

# 7. Transportation

Mythical creatures may use ordinary transportation when practical.

Examples include:

- Cars
- Trains
- Aircraft
- Public transportation

Magical travel may exist but should not make ordinary travel irrelevant.

---

# 8. Magic and Technology

Magic and technology coexist.

The relationship should remain practical rather than universally integrated.

Potential limitations:

- Some magical environments interfere with technology.
- Some technologies are difficult to use in True Form.
- Magical secrecy may limit digital convenience.

Specific rules must remain consistent with `02_MAGIC/magic-system.md`.

---

# 9. Education

The Veilward Academy uses a mixture of:

- Traditional teaching
- Practical training
- Research
- Modern educational tools

Ancient does not mean outdated.

---

# 10. Employment and Adult Life

Mythical creatures may participate in:

- Hidden World professions
- Human professions
- Academic work
- Community service
- Protective roles

A creature's magical identity does not determine their entire career.

---

# 11. Modern Culture

The younger generation experiences many of the same things humans do:

- Music
- Films
- Games
- Fashion
- Internet culture

They also experience unique Hidden World traditions.

The story should allow both cultures to coexist naturally.

---

# 12. Generational Differences

Older mythical creatures may have different relationships with technology.

Some embrace it.

Others distrust it.

Others simply use it poorly.

Age should not automatically equal technological ignorance.

---

# 13. Human Technology as a Narrative Tool

Technology should be used when it makes sense.

Avoid two extremes:

### Extreme One

Magic solves every technological problem.

### Extreme Two

The story pretends modern technology does not exist.

The world should feel contemporary.

---

# 14. Privacy

Digital privacy is especially important for creatures living among humanity.

Risks include:

- Facial recognition
- Persistent digital records
- Unexpected photographs
- Location tracking

The exact Hidden World response systems remain open.

---

# 15. Daily Life

Modern Hidden World life includes ordinary concerns:

- Studies
- Relationships
- Families
- Messages
- Entertainment
- Careers

The extraordinary world should still contain ordinary life.

---

# 16. Canon Boundaries

Do not establish without further development:

- A universal magical internet
- A perfect digital memory-erasure system
- A complete Hidden World banking system
- Exact government technology departments
- Specific modern human identities for every character

---

# 17. Final Principle

The world should feel mythological and modern at the same time.

A student may study ancient magic in the morning and send a message to a friend at night.

Neither makes the other less real.

> **The Hidden World survived the modern age by living through it, not by refusing to enter it.**
